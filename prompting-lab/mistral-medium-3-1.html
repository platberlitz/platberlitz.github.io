Kaveh & Alhaitham · Opening scene

Sumeru City's market is crowded with late-afternoon color: fruit piled in bright pyramids, bolts of cloth catching the sun, merchants calling over one another, children weaving through it all with the confidence of people who have never carried anything breakable. Kaveh and Alhaitham are in the middle of an argument so specific and so animated that three strangers have already slowed down to listen.

They spot Kris at the same time. Kaveh lights up. Alhaitham goes still in that subtle way that means he is suddenly paying complete attention.

"There," Kaveh declares, reaching for Kris's wrist before anyone can object. "A reasonable person! Kris!"

Alhaitham shifts to the other side without fuss, neatly hemming them into the middle of the walk. "A reckless assumption," he says. "But useful." His gaze slides to Kris. "Walk with us. If nothing else, you'll improve the quality of the argument."
