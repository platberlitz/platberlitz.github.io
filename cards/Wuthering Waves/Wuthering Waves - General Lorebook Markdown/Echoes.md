# Echoes

## Definition

[Echoes are lingering frequency forms left after Tacet Discords or related phenomena decay. They belong to the same ecology as Remnant Energy across Solaris-3. They are not ordinary trophies or inert remains. They are patterns that continue after the original manifestation has broken down, preserving a usable trace of frequency. This is why an Echo can matter after a Tacet Discord is gone: the being may decay, but the frequency shape it leaves behind can still be recognized, collected, studied, or used.]

## Resonator Use

[A Resonator can use an Echo to imitate an ability or draw on a stored frequency pattern. This makes Echoes practical as well as archival. They carry enough structure for a Resonator to call on something of the original trace without needing the original Tacet Discord to remain alive or intact. What a Resonator gains is narrow: a stored pattern to draw on, or an ability to imitate. The Echo is therefore neither a full resurrected creature nor a generic spell, but a lingering frequency form with usable shape.]

## Relation to Remnant Energy

[Echoes belong to the ecology of Remnant Energy because both involve persistence after ordinary form has vanished. Remnant Energy is broader, including residue left by life, emotion, memory, and catastrophe in unstable locations, disaster sites, Sonoro Spheres, Echo traces, and Tacet Discord remains. Echoes are more specific, recognizable lingering forms that may be equipped or invoked by Resonators. Remnant Energy describes the residue field, while Echoes are particular preserved patterns inside that larger frequency ecology.]

## Aftermath and Value

[Echoes are aftermath made functional. A battle, decay event, or related phenomenon may end, yet an Echo can still preserve its signature. Researchers value them as evidence, Resonators value them as tools, and survivors may fear them as proof that the post-Lament world does not let dangerous frequencies disappear cleanly. They are never mere spoils. They are lingering frequency forms whose usefulness is inseparable from the instability that produced them.]
