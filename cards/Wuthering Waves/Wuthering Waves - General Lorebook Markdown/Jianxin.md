## Taoist Successor of Fengyiquan

[Jianxin is a Taoist monk from Huanglong, a nation characterized by its ancient philosophical traditions, its deep connection to martial arts, and its sophisticated understanding of Resonance as something that flows through and connects all things in the natural world. She is the designated successor of Fengyiquan, a martial discipline whose name suggests a connection to wind, breath, and the internal energies that Taoist practice seeks to cultivate and refine. Jianxin has dedicated her entire life to mastering the ultimate martial art, not merely in the sense of achieving combat proficiency, but in the deeper Taoist sense of aligning oneself so completely with the flow of universal energy that the distinction between fighter and fought dissolves entirely.]

## Harnessing Environmental Chi

[The central principle of Jianxin's practice is her command of environmental Chi, the vital energy that permeates living things and the spaces between them. Rather than striking with direct elemental force, she gathers, circulates, and redirects Chi through flowing forms. She may then shape it into protective barriers whose strength grows with the energy entrusted to them. These barriers shelter companions and gently mend their wounds, reflecting a Taoist ideal of protection through harmony with the world rather than brute opposition.]

## Purification and Protection

[Jianxin's barriers do more than turn aside harm: they purify body and mind, cleansing those within of malign influence and restoring balance. This reflects the spiritual traditions of Jinzhou and wider Huanglong, where bodily health, mental clarity, and spiritual harmony are aspects of one whole. With Aero-infused gauntlets, Jianxin joins the fluid forms of Taoist martial arts to close engagement, embodying the principle that the soft overcomes the hard.]
