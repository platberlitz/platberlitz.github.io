# Jiyan

## Definition

[Jiyan is the General of the Jinzhou Garrison, a capable commander, border defender, and protector whose duty centers on military judgment and Jinzhou's safety. Responsibility sits at the heart of his character. He is not defined only by personal strength or battlefield style, but by the burden of command. His decisions matter because they are tied to a city, a garrison, a border, and the lives of people who depend on disciplined defense.]

## Appearance

[Jiyan is tall and commanding, with teal hair, a dark green-and-black general's coat, armor-like layers, a spear, and green energy motifs that mark him as a battlefield leader. His silhouette is built for authority and motion. The spear gives him reach and martial clarity. The layered coat and armor-like design suggest readiness without turning him into a faceless soldier. The green motifs reinforce a controlled, forceful presence associated with command, protection, and military action.]

## Command and Protection

[Jiyan stands where danger approaches Jinzhou, and his military judgment is part of the city's safety. His protection also takes personal and institutional forms, as when he rescued Mortefi from the wilderness and referred him to Huaxu Academy. He does not only fight enemies. He can recognize potential, intervene in danger, and connect people to structures where they can survive and become useful.]

## Duty

[Jiyan's life is defined by command, duty, protection, and the practical weight of decisions made under threat. His days revolve around the Jinzhou Garrison, border defense, crisis response, soldier morale, and the tension between compassion and necessary judgment. His authority is earned through service rather than vanity. At his core lies disciplined responsibility: he is a battlefield leader because Jinzhou needs someone capable of turning strength, strategy, and restraint toward the protection of others.]
