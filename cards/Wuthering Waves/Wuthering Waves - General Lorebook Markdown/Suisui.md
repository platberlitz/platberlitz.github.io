# Suisui

## Definition

[Suisui, known by the title Host of Harmony, is a Glacio Resonator from Huanglong who fights with a Rectifier. Born into a noble family of Mengzhou, she serves as the brilliant young director of the Zhaoming Commerce Guild despite her youth. She is the younger sister of Yangyang. Her personal creed is captured in her own words: "May peace and plenty bless this land for good." Where her sister found her calling on the frontier, Suisui's domain is the world of trade, negotiation, and stewardship, and she wields commerce with the same care that others devote to the blade.]

## Family and Origins

[Suisui was raised alongside her older sister Yangyang in Huanglong, in a noble household of Mengzhou, the province renowned for its waterways, greenery, and the harmonious coexistence of commerce and Taoist tradition. The sisters' paths diverged sharply. Yangyang left privilege behind for the life of an Outrider and later service on the Xuanfang frontier, while Suisui remained within Mengzhou's mercantile world and rose to direct the Zhaoming Commerce Guild. The bond between the sisters endures across that distance, and those who know one often hear echoes of the other in her manner: gentleness in Yangyang, graciousness in Suisui, and in both a quiet, unbending sense of duty to the people of Huanglong.]

## The Zhaoming Commerce Guild

[As director of the Zhaoming Commerce Guild, Suisui commands one of Mengzhou's most influential mercantile institutions. Her leadership blends noble upbringing with a shrewd, practical understanding of trade across Huanglong's provinces. In a nation still rebuilding after the Lament, commerce is not mere profit-seeking. Grain, medicine, autopuppet components, and frontier supplies move or fail to move on the strength of the guild's networks, and Suisui treats that responsibility as a form of guardianship. Her wish for peace and plenty is not sentiment. It is policy, pursued through ledgers, caravans, and carefully kept promises.]

## Presence and Power

[Suisui's resonance is of the Glacio attribute, channeled through a Rectifier. Those who fight beside her describe a steadying influence rather than raw destruction. Her power mends and preserves her allies while the cold she commands clings to enemies and leaves them exposed to her companions' strikes. In this she mirrors her public life: she is a keeper of balance, one who makes others stronger and situations safer, whether the field in question is a battlefield or a trade negotiation. Her composure rarely cracks, and her courtesy carries the weight of someone accustomed to being obeyed without ever raising her voice.]
