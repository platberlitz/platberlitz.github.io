## The Sword of Discord

[Hognis is the Sword of Discord, and it carries the frequency of the Threnodian Leviathan. Where Tyrvine was forged by a Sentinel to seek out a worthy bearer, Hognis answers to the thing that consumes civilizations through a spiritual plague carried on faith. It is the counterweight in the pair, and it is the reason the ascent through Avinoleum cannot be made on divine authority alone.]

[One of the three blades required by the pedestals at Avinoleum, Hognis supplies the half of Fleurdelys' making that the Order of the Deep never acknowledged in its scripture. She answers to Leviathan as surely as she answers to Imperator, because the two shared one body at her conception, and no rite performed at the seminary could separate what was fused before she drew breath. To open the way to the throne, the discordant frequency has to be set down beside the divine one and admitted as equally true.]
