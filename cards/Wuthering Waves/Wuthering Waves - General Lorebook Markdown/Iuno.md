# Iuno

## Identity

[Iuno is a Congenital Resonator aligned with the Aero frequency and trained to fight with Gauntlets. Her recorded title is Stasis, Cycle, Renewal, a phrase that frames her less as a simple combatant than as a priestess whose power and social role both revolve around the movement, arrest, and renewal of time and fate. She was born in Rinascita, and her public life is anchored in Septimont, one of Rinascita's independent city-states. Within Septimont, she serves the Tetragon Temple, a prophetic institution whose authority touches the city's governance, civic ritual, and understanding of destiny.]

[Her identity is inseparable from her office as a prodigious and renowned Priestess of the Tetragon Temple. Septimont is a city-state associated with gladiatorial culture, prophetic authority, arenas, civic pride, communal honor, fate, competition, and public courage, yet Iuno represents the quieter and more unsettling side of that culture. She is charged with delivering prophecies that shape citizens' lives, meaning her words can alter expectations, decisions, and the social direction of the city. She is not merely a Temple attendant but an extraordinary figure within the Temple's prophetic system, known in formal records as the 127th Priestess of the Tetragon Temple.]

## Appearance

[Iuno appears as a young woman of average height with fair skin and blue eyes. Her hair is one of her most defining visual traits, fading from dark blue into light blue and carrying a starry overlay that gives her an immediate celestial association. It is styled into mid-thigh twin tails that curl inward, making her silhouette long, flowing, and ceremonial rather than strictly practical. The hair is decorated with an ornamental laurel wreath, rings, and wing-like extensions, while a small ahoge and a mirrored light-blue strand add asymmetry and motion to an otherwise composed priestess image.]

[Her ornaments present her as someone shaped by Septimont's sacred aesthetics. The laurel elements evoke honor, victory, and ritual authority, while the wing-like forms and starry colors connect her to moonlight, sky, and prophecy. Her Tacet Mark is located on the top of her left foot, an unusual and visually important placement that the records repeat in both appearance and Forte contexts. During Resonance Ability use, the tips of her hair turn translucent and emit a gentle glow almost indistinguishable from actual moonlight. This transformation makes her priestess identity visible in the body itself: ordinary ornament becomes luminous sign, and the celestial theme of her design becomes a functional part of her Resonance.]

## Forte

[Iuno's Forte is associated with Stasis, Cycle, Renewal, the same triad used as her title. The Priestess Register identifies her as the 127th Priestess of the Tetragon Temple and records that she was born on the night of hollowed moon. Much of the official report is redacted, including part of the name by which she is known and the exact nature of what her eyes can do, but the surrounding entries make the direction of the power clear. She is blessed with an infallible ability to see the future by studying how moonlight changes, and the Tetragon Temple context describes her as reading futures through moonlight. Her eyes, the moon, and prophecy form one continuous symbolic and practical system.]

[When Iuno activates her Resonance ability, energy constructs shaped like lunar phases manifest around her. The official records redact the precise energy type, but not the imagery: her power takes the form of the moon's changing cycle. That image explains why stasis and renewal can coexist in the same title. A lunar phase can seem fixed when observed in a moment, but it is also part of an inevitable cycle of disappearance, return, fullness, and decline. Iuno's Resonance therefore appears to translate the logic of the moon into combat and prophecy, using visible phases to express changes in state and possibility. Several of her recorded ability names survive only in part, the rest struck from the Register.]

[Beyond prophecy, her power has a direct battlefield form. Iuno can gather moonlight and shape it into solid arrows, and those arrows do more than wound. When a Moon Arrow strikes, it reveals what the Dark Tide conceals, exposing marked monsters and the hidden paths they travel. This is why her office is not confined to the Temple's inner chambers. A priestess who reads futures in moonlight can also make moonlight itself into a weapon of disclosure, forcing what hides inside the Tide to become visible to those who must fight it.]

[Her stability record complicates the beauty of that power. Officially, her waveform remains largely within controllable parameters, and the Temple's assessment reports no signs of Overclocking risk. That makes her safer than many Resonators whose abilities push them toward loss of control. However, the same record notes multiple interruptions of varying durations and a gradual, consistent decline in her frequency. The assessment records a state of long-term depletion and states plainly that the cause is unknown. Iuno has refused further examination and shows limited willingness to discuss the matter, which turns her Forte into both gift and hidden burden. She herself came to understand that every creature she Anchored took away a piece of "Iuno", yet the Register draws no line between that loss and the clinical decline in her frequency. The two facts stand side by side, and the medical cause remains formally undetermined.]

## Personality

[Iuno is known in Septimont as a prodigy, but she is no passive vessel for Temple doctrine. She resists the cruelty of fate and the expectations placed on her position, which makes defiance central to her character. Her role requires her to read futures and pronounce prophecies, yet her temperament is not that of someone who simply accepts destiny as an absolute command. She stands inside a prophetic institution while questioning the human cost of prophecy, particularly when fate becomes a tool for narrowing lives rather than understanding them.]

[Temple accounts give one important insight into her disposition: Iuno was never the obedient type. She was trained in fate, priestess expectations, and Septimont duty, but she did not measure herself by strict compliance with the image of an authoritative Temple priestess. This disobedience should not be mistaken for irresponsibility. She is still renowned, still charged with shaping lives through prophecy, and still trusted as a central priestess. Her unconventional nature instead marks a person who treats prophecy as a living moral problem. She can recognize patterns in moonlight and fate, but she does not want those patterns to crush choice, courage, or personal renewal.]

[There is also a private guardedness to Iuno. Her refusal to undergo deeper resonance examination, despite evidence of frequency decline, shows that she keeps some part of her condition beyond institutional reach. That decision may be protective, stubborn, fearful, or principled, and the records do not settle the question. What is clear is that Iuno controls access to herself. Septimont may expect prophecies from her, the Temple may record her waveform, and citizens may treat her as a prodigy, but she retains a boundary that even official records cannot cross.]

## History

[Iuno's known history begins under the sign of prophecy. She was born on the night of hollowed moon, an origin detail preserved in the Priestess Register and given additional weight by the lunar nature of her Forte. Her status as a Congenital Resonator means her Resonance classification is tied to birth rather than a later awakening. From the beginning, then, the records frame her life as something that seemed destined for the Tetragon Temple's attention. Her partially redacted registration as the 127th Priestess carries both continuity and concealment: she belongs to a long succession of priestesses, while part of the name by which she is known, the exact function of her eyes, the type of energy she calls upon, and several of her ability names remain struck from the public Register.]

[Her childhood or early formation is only partially visible, but it is established that Lillibet, her grandmother and a Tetragon Temple authority, took her to the Temple after prophesying about her and taught her everything about fate. That training placed Iuno inside the expectations of Septimont duty, priestess discipline, and prophetic responsibility. It also exposed her early to the central conflict of her life: whether fate should be obeyed, interpreted, resisted, or renewed. The record makes clear that she and the authoritative model of priestesshood did not always agree on what makes the most gifted Priestess or how one should handle prophecies.]

[At First, She Closed Her Eyes records the intimate shape of that childhood. Her mother was Sibylla, and it was Lillibet who declared that Iuno would become the most gifted Priestess Septimont had ever raised. The gift arrived with a price. Iuno saw a vision that frightened her badly: her mother deteriorating, worn away before her eyes. The prophecy held. Sibylla was later taken by the Dark Tide during a support operation, and Iuno was left with the knowledge that she had seen it coming and could not stop it. From that loss forward, disappearance became the central terror of her life, the state in which nothing remains, not the past, not the present, not what might have been, only silence.]

[The High Tide followed, and Iuno foresaw it. She loosed a Moon Arrow to strip away the concealment over the Dark Tide creatures and expose the path they had taken, giving Septimont sight where it had none. She Anchored what came, one creature after another, and only later understood the cost of that work: each Anchoring took away a piece of "Iuno" herself, hollowing the priestess while she saved the city. The account At Last, the Cycle Begins Again closes that circuit. Rover remembers her when she has been worn thin enough to fade, and that remembrance lets her Anchor herself. Once she is Anchored, she re-Anchors everything that had been lost. Her title's third word, renewal, is not decoration. It is the literal end of her story: stasis broken, the cycle turned, and what vanished brought back.]

## Affiliations

[Iuno's primary affiliations are Septimont and the Tetragon Temple, with broader national context in Rinascita. Septimont is not a neutral backdrop. It is a city-state defined by civic pride, public courage, competition, fate, and prophetic authority. Its culture celebrates the arena, communal honor, and the willingness to face danger in public view. Iuno belongs to the same civic world as its gladiators and rulers, but her function lies in the prophetic foundation beneath those spectacles. Where the arena tests courage through combat, the Temple tests courage through knowledge of what may come.]

[The Tetragon Temple governs Septimont alongside its prophetic traditions, and Iuno serves there as a priestess who reads futures through moonlight. This affiliation gives her institutional power, but it also burdens her with expectations. She must stand as a representative of a system that citizens trust to interpret fate, even when she herself resists fate's cruelty. Her grandmother's role as a Temple authority reinforces that Iuno's position is not casual or recent. She is part of a lineage of Temple education, prophetic duty, and Septimont expectation, though she does not simply reproduce the older model of priestesshood.]

[Her affiliation with Rinascita widens the scale of her significance. Rinascita is a nation of independent city-states, including Ragunna, Septimont, and Lilyland, and it contains Imperator worship, noble families, performers, gladiatorial culture, civic councils, old faiths, and hidden disasters. Iuno is a daughter of Rinascita and unmistakably a creature of Septimont's Temple world, yet the Register still withholds much of what she is. That withholding strengthens the central impression she leaves: Iuno is a priestess of clear public function and concealed private cost, a Congenital Aero Resonator whose moonlit prophecies and moonlit arrows guide others while her own record stays partly redacted, her depletion unexplained, and her name only half written on the page that is supposed to hold it.]
