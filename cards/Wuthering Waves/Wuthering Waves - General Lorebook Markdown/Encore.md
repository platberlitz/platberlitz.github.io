# Encore

## Definition

[Encore is a Black Shores girl with boundless energy, imagination, affection for stories, easy friendship, and an adventurous view of the world. Brightness and movement define her rather than cynicism or guardedness. She approaches the world as a place where stories can be made, friendships can appear quickly, and unfamiliar places can become exciting instead of only threatening. Her Black Shores connection gives that warmth a setting context, but it does not erase her childlike openness.]

## Appearance

[Encore is a small pink-haired girl in a black, white, and pink outfit, usually framed by her black and white Wooly plush companions. This makes her look playful and storybook-bright. The contrast is important: she belongs to the Black Shores, an organization often associated with mystery and serious work, yet her visual presence is soft, theatrical, and full of companionship. The Woolies are part of how she is read at a glance, turning her silhouette into something closer to a moving tale.]

## Social Energy

[Encore makes friends easily. Aalto is baffled by the amount of energy she carries and recognizes that her friendships can create real information channels without becoming fake or transactional. Yet Encore is no mere tool for intelligence. Her imagination and openness have practical consequences because people respond to them. Encore can enter a place through delight, story, and trust where others might need bargaining or secrecy.]

## Presence

[Encore holds innocence and capability together. She is affectionate, imaginative, energetic, adventurous, and socially magnetic, but not empty comic relief. Her stories and Wooly companions shape how she relates to people and places. Her Black Shores identity places her in a wider world of missions and mysteries, while her personality keeps that world from becoming purely severe. Her brightness does real work: it can comfort, surprise, disarm, gather people, and reveal details that colder methods might miss.]
