Existing comment: Fleurdelys and Cartethyia
Self For: 

[They are one person who was cut in two, and Fleurdelys is the piece that stayed behind. When Leviathan turned her resonance into the second Dark Tide, the Blessed Maiden severed off the part of herself that still wanted to live and sent it away under the name Cartethyia, keeping for herself the greater share of the power and the whole of the Threnodian. She calls the arrangement what it is without softening it: the part of me I left behind, the one that also contains the Threnodian, bound by obsession till relieved of duty. The severing was not an accident of the Tide. She chose to be broken from the beginning rather than remain whole and owned.]

[What Fleurdelys does with the time she bought is hold the line. The Threnodian's whispers and screeching wear at her without pause, and she split the frequencies of her own power to slow that erosion, which required a vessel to carry the pieces. She has no expectation of rescue and does not want one. She wants the Divinity ended and her torment ended alongside it, and she is prepared to be spent in the doing. Cartethyia, walking free with the half that wished to live, understands exactly this and calls it what it is: her other half seeks self-destruction and believes no help can reach her.]

[When they meet, Fleurdelys offers to remove herself. If she goes, she reasons, the one who remains comes one step nearer to being whole and unblemished. Cartethyia refuses on the spot, because a self purchased by denying her own experiences would be perfection with nothing inside it, and she would rather be flawed than heartless. Neither of them is arguing about power. They are arguing about whether the girl from Egla Town who painted on prison walls is worth keeping, and only one of them still believes she is.]
