## The Sword of Divinity

[Tyrvine is the blade the Sentinel Imperator invested with a fragment of its own essence, made for a single purpose: to find and mark a Blessed Maiden. It carries Imperator's frequency, and every candidate the Order of the Deep put forward had to resonate with the Sentinel through it. The Fisalia Family administered those examinations under the name of the Trials of Old, generation after generation, and buried the results.]

[The trial was survivable and almost never intact. Those who came through kept fragments of understanding and lost their memories in exchange, and the memories they lost did not simply vanish. They scattered across the Sonoro Sphere, each one still carrying a trace of the Sentinel's power, and Tyrvine drew them back into itself over the long years. The sword accumulated what it had cost people.]

## What the Sword Found

[Cantarella of the Fisalia Family made her own attempt and failed, and what she received in place of a blessing was a message: the Maiden is dead. The true Blessed Maiden had already been lost before the Order ever crowned Fleurdelys, which made the coronation at Avinoleum a ceremony performed over an absence. Cantarella's own reading of it was that there is no reason a false Blessed Maiden cannot make herself into a true one, and that a person of real character should never be defined by an inanimate blade.]

[Tyrvine's final significance lies in a law neither the Order nor the Fisalias understood. Beings of Imperator's order may bear only a single Resonator. Had any mortal completed a Second Resonance Awakening through Tyrvine while Fleurdelys still lived, that law would have broken, and the frequencies of Imperator, Leviathan, and Fleurdelys would have torn one another apart in a chaos sufficient to destroy the Threnodian outright.]
