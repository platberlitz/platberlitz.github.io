## The Borderless Academic Metropolis

[Startorch Academy, officially titled "Spacetrek Collective: Startorch Academy" is a comprehensive multinational institution built specifically for Resonators in Lahai-Roi beneath the Roya Frostlands. It is not merely a school but "an academic metropolis driven solely by educational and research pursuits," constructed by the Spacetrek Collective as a borderless facility that doesn't belong to any single country. Its name embodies its core ethos: "to carry the flame of inquiry and ignite the torch of civilization," derived from the phrase "Let Knowledge Be Your Guiding Star, Raise High the Torch of Tomorrow." Under President Lucilla's leadership, the Academy strives to be a place where "seekers of knowledge from across the world gather, knowledge flows freely, and cultures meet in harmony." However, this idealistic vision exists in constant tension with political realities, the Spacetrek Collective's international stakeholders, competing national interests, academic factionalism, and the ever-present threat of organizations like the Fractsidus infiltrating its halls. The Academy sits literally beneath the frostlands ice cap, accessing light and energy from the massive Reactor Drive that powers both the city and the Exostrider project.]

## Four Major Departments

[Startorch Academy's educational structure revolves around four primary departments, each with specialized facilities and research focuses. The Department of Exostrider Engineering is dedicated to studying the giant humanoid mech known as Exostrider, known for radical approaches that have applications in vehicle engineering, humanoid mech technology, Tacetite weaponry, and strategic equipment development. It has two major divisions: the Division of Resonator-Mech Synchronization and the Division of Tactical Development. Notable faculty include Professor Mornye (known for painting dreams across star charts and serving as Biomatic Club consultant) and Professor Sevi (a memory-type Resonator, past Startorch Prize recipient, and creator of brilliant instruments who treats students like his own children). The Department of Light Energy Sciences focuses on converting light energy from the Reactor Drive for industrial production and biological cycling, split between the Division of Light Array Engineering and Division of Ecology Conservation. The department houses the Academy's largest solis lens and Resonance Nexus, the Telesto Lens, which "incessantly converts light energy emanating from the Reactor Drive to energy supply and powers the operation of all facilities on campus." The Department of Voidmatters serves as strategic response to Void Storm threats, tasked with monitoring and prevention. Its main building is the Academy's tallest signal tower, topped by the central observatory. Researchers investigate Void Storm origins and mechanisms while developing advanced communication and spatial anchoring technologies for extreme environments within Void Storms and beyond the Stridergate, enabling transdimensional localization of the Exostrider and real-time monitoring of Synchronists' vitals. Professor Voss leads this department, and Denia was enrolled in its Division of Spatial Warping.]

## Rabelle College: The Synchronist Path

[Rabelle College, also called Synchronist College, operates as a semi-independent entity within Startorch Academy, named after Mr. Rabelle who pioneered Rabelle's Curve, the standardized scale for characterizing a Resonator's frequency pattern. With pedagogy "rooted in empirical data and quantitative research," the College is dedicated to discovering and cultivating future Synchronists, rare candidates who can link their consciousness to the giant Exostrider mech via external mechanical interface and achieve control through Augmented Resonance. Beyond general education, Rabelle College runs the Synchronist Development Program independently of all departments and disciplines. All quasi-Synchronists receive unrestricted access to campus facilities. The College includes the Division of Ancient Studies and the Division of Synchronization Training (colloquially the Synchronist Department). Professor Sakya serves as Synchronization Professor and Consultant, she was Aemeath's teacher and thesis supervisor ten years ago. The Resonator Nursing Unit (ReNU) operates as a Rabelle College affiliate, led by Dr. Luuk Herssen, specializing in Frequency Therapeutics with cutting-edge medical facilities and expertise in treating diverse conditions. ReNU combines medical support with stress relief for students undergoing intensive training, assisted by N.A.N.A. (Nightingale Autonomous Nursing Assistant) and staff member Arman. Rover entered Startorch Academy as a first-year Rabelle College Synchronist, while Aemeath graduated as a Synchronist from the College years earlier.]

## Preparatory Program and Department Placement

[All Startorch Academy students except Synchronists admitted to Rabelle College begin with a cross-disciplinary Preparatory Program curriculum covering foundational sciences, humanities, and essential survival skills for life in Lahai-Roi. Students' specialized department placement is determined at the end of Year 2 based on stated interest and academic performance. This system ensures broad foundational education before specialization but also means first and second-year students like Sigrika and Lynae remain in preparatory status, not yet assigned to specific departments. Chisa's presence in the Department of Exostrider Engineering's Tactical Development Program follows an interrupted course rather than an unexplained bypass. She first entered Startorch at sixteen, then spent only two subjective months trapped in the Honami City Sonoro Sphere while two decades passed outside. After escaping with Rover and returning to Lahai-Roi, she re-enrolled into an academic life whose calendar no longer matched her own lived time. The preparatory curriculum balances theoretical knowledge with practical application, recognizing that Lahai-Roi's extreme environment demands both intellectual rigor and hands-on survival competence from all graduates regardless of specialization.]

## Campus Facilities and Student Life

[The Academy's infrastructure reflects its commitment to student wellbeing alongside academic rigor. The Startorch Food, Utilities & Energy Lounge (FUEL) operates 24 hours providing fully-automatic, intelligent, customizable catering via the "Human Energy Assessment and Replenishment System," offering finely curated menus balancing nutrition and flavor for all dietary needs. Academy Dorms give every student private space with thoughtfully designed resting areas, soundproofed walls, and freedom to decorate within school regulations. Auto-Clean Pods offer intelligent laundromat services integrating garment cleaning with nano-repair technology, automatically identifying fabric types and applying tailored care while extending garment lifespan. UNICODE (Uniform Consult & Design) provides bespoke uniform tailoring using intelligent fitting systems with encrypted measurement data accessible only with authorization. Recreative Program Generators (RPGs) are modular entertainment units around the residential quarter where "the tenth art is on full display" according to Rabelle College studies, when combined with Frequency Modulation technologies, video games help students relieve stress, regulate mood, and restore cognitive wellbeing. Startorch Can & Tin serves as the official convenience store chain bringing grocery shopping to doorsteps, stocking everything from snacks to stationery with handpicked quality items, also where the Gastro-Alchemist Fan Club hangs out. Auto Parking & Engineering Xpertise (APEX) offers comprehensive parking and vehicle modification services with professional maintenance and customization support. The public Auditorium serves as primary venue for large-scale indoor events, available for student activities with proposals submitted to the Student Council at least three working days in advance.]

## Faculty Politics and Academic Factionalism

[While Startorch Academy presents a unified front publicly, its faculty harbors significant political divisions. President Lucilla faces opposition from traditionalist professors including the respected Professors Karl and Sevi, who argue "she is a good teacher but is an average resonator with no expertise" compared to scholars like Professor Mornye or investor-backed leadership figures. These critics believe "there are better figures for the position: scholarship wise, geniuses like Mornye and leadership wise, like the investors with more expertise." Lucilla's appointment came through political maneuvering and strategic alliances rather than pure academic credentials, fueling ongoing resentment. The Academy's Council includes Professor Voss (Voidmatters) and formerly Professor Warren (who was head of the Research Institute before his expulsion for colluding with the Fractsidus). The Research Institute itself represents a powerful faction within Startorch, Professor Anslem, a prominent Exostrider Engineering professor, served on the team launching the reactor drive for the new Helios and worked with Warren on Augmented Resonance before Warren's betrayal. Young professors like Maeva (researching "superfoods" in the Dimmer Plains and food development in extreme environments) and others navigate these political waters while pursuing their research. The Spacetrek Collective's Information Security and Protection Act has been invoked to discontinue investigations and delete records deemed sensitive, suggesting layers of institutional secrecy beneath the Academy's transparent educational mission.]

## Student Organizations and Fan Clubs

[Startorch Academy's vibrant student culture includes numerous fan clubs and societies that shape campus life. The Birding Fan Club includes members Sigrika and Nivora. The Argus Workshop Club, described as "where modern technology and ancient Traditions meet," is "a paradise for arms and armament enthusiasts" that meets at the Academy's Crafting Bay to craft legendary weapons and explore Resonator-weapon-making craftsmanship inspired by Septimonian traditions, led by Garris with member Katarina. The Gastro-Alchemist Fan Club, considered the "weirdest" but "most practical," has the "grand mission" of improving Academy Food not through food engineering or nutrition research but by combining available campus foods to "taste better" they hang around Startorch Can & Tin, with member Felicia and an unnamed leader currently in the infirmary for stomach ache. They wish to recruit Dr. Luuk Herssen. The Paranormal Activity Club includes members Mayo and Rover. The Peaks of Prestige Fan Club is the most popular club, dedicated to Mr. Furious of Septimont's Echo Battle strategy card game with bold all-or-nothing tactics, Anita manages the Lahai Roi Ranked Tournament division, with dozens of members including students, faculty (Professors Calith, Dennis, Festa), automatons (I.R.I.S., N.A.N.A.), and even Rover. The Photography Fan Club gifted each member personal albums as "vessels for cherished campus memories" Denia received one and filled it with group shots "where Denia smiles quietly from the corner." These clubs represent more than extracurricular activities; they're spaces where students form bonds that sustain them through the Academy's intense academic demands and the dangerous world beyond graduation.]

## The Research Institute and Fractsidus Infiltration

[The Research Institute operates within Startorch Academy as a center for advanced scientific research, particularly focused on Exostrider development, Augmented Resonance, and Voidmatter studies. Professor Warren previously led the Institute and held a seat on the Academy's Council, demonstrating expertise across multiple fields from Exostrider Engineering to Voidmatters to Synchronization. However, Warren was discovered colluding with the Fractsidus and expelled. After the events of "Ode to the Second Sunrise," Warren went into hiding only to be killed later by Schwarzloch, a Fractsidus operative. This infiltration at the highest levels revealed the Academy's vulnerability to enemy operations. Denia's successful infiltration as a third-year Voidmatters student, complete with falsified examination reports and hidden abilities, demonstrated that the Fractsidus could place assets directly into the student body. Her Forte Examination Report RA2499-G was systematically doctored to hide her true nature as a vessel for Aleph-1, with researchers' marginal notes revealing they discovered discrepancies: "How did this get filed in the first place?! My goodness... Is anything in this report actually true?" and demands for "the complete record from the Dimmr Plains ASAP!" The Academy's struggle to balance openness (as a borderless institution welcoming students from across Solaris) with security (protecting against infiltration and sabotage) remains an ongoing challenge that President Lucilla and her allies must navigate.]

## The Exostrider Project and Aemeath

[The Exostrider, a giant humanoid mech, represents Startorch Academy's most ambitious project, developed collaboratively by the Department of Exostrider Engineering, the Research Institute, and Rabelle College. The mech operates through Synchronist pilots who link their consciousness via external mechanical interface and achieve control through Augmented Resonance. The development required breakthroughs in multiple fields: resonator-mech synchronization, tactical systems, light energy conversion (for power), spatial anchoring (for transdimensional operation), and frequency therapeutics (to support pilots). Aemeath emerged as the Exostrider's bonded pilot ten years after graduating from Rabelle College under Professor Sakya's supervision. Her successful synchronization validated decades of research and positioned the Exostrider as humanity's answer to existential threats like Aleph-1 and the Void Storms. The reactor drive launch, involving Professor Anslem and others, enabled the Exostrider's operation at scale. However, Professor Warren's betrayal and attempts to weaponize Augmented Resonance for the Fractsidus demonstrated how the same technologies developed for protection could be turned to apocalyptic ends. The Exostrider project continues under new leadership with heightened security, representing both the Academy's greatest achievement and its most vulnerable asset.]

## Automatons and Intelligent Systems

[Startorch Academy employs multiple intelligent automaton systems supporting various functions. B.I.N.G.O. (Battle Interface for Next-Gen Operations) manages combat simulations and tactical training. I.R.I.S. (Intelligent Retrieval & Indexing System) handles information management, library services, and database access, notably serving as a member of the Peaks of Prestige Fan Club. N.A.N.A. (Nightingale Autonomous Nursing Assistant) works at ReNU providing medical support under Dr. Luuk Herssen's supervision and also participates in the Peaks of Prestige Fan Club. S.I.G.M.A. (Signal Intelligent Guidance & Management Automaton) oversees campus-wide communications, scheduling, and navigation systems. These automatons represent the Academy's commitment to leveraging technology for educational support, though their integration into student life (including fan club memberships) suggests they're treated as community members rather than mere tools. The intelligent systems managing FUEL, UNICODE, Auto-Clean Pods, and RPGs demonstrate Startorch's advanced technological infrastructure, making daily life remarkably convenient for students who can focus on their demanding academic programs without logistical burdens.]

## Graduates and Influence Across Solaris

[Startorch Academy's graduates populate influential positions across Solaris-3, from combat units to research institutions to government advisory roles. Aemeath, the Exostrider pilot, represents the Academy's highest-profile success, a Fusion swordswoman whose multi-phase combat capabilities and bond with the giant mech stem from years of Startorch training. Beyond famous names, countless graduates serve in the Midnight Rangers, pioneer associations, national defense forces, research collectives, and exploration teams facing the dangers of the post-Lament world. The Academy's influence extends through these networks of alumni who share common training, values, and connections forged during their student years. Even students who struggled or were deemed average often find their niche after graduation, applying Startorch's cross-disciplinary approach to unexpected contexts. The institution's true measure of success isn't the exceptional individuals like Aemeath but the broad base of competent, principled Resonators who graduated believing, as President Lucilla teaches, that they should "never mind what others think" and "become the person you imagine yourself to be," carrying that philosophy into their professional lives and the communities they serve.]
