# Luuk Herssen

[Luuk Herssen is a Spectro-element Resonator who wields Gauntlets and serves as the head physician of the Resonator Nursing Unit at Startorch Academy in Lahai-Roi. Behind his warm bedside manner and counselor's gentle disposition lies a man shaped by a past steeped in blood and deception, a past he has neither forgotten nor allowed to dictate who he has become. His Forte, Phase Transition, works through an anomalous energy fluid called Ichor that circulates within him and changes phase state at his will. His Tacet Mark is a wound in the center of his right palm that has never closed. Affiliated with Startorch Academy and the Spacetrek Collective, and heir to the Novialle Regenerative Group, Luuk occupies an unusual cross-section of institutional power in the New Federation's sphere, trusted with the physical and psychological well-being of the next generation of Resonators.]

## Attending Doctor and Counselor

[Luuk Herssen's Resonator classification remains listed as "Unclear" in official Spacetrek Collective records, a deliberate ambiguity that may speak to the unconventional nature of his abilities, the classified circumstances of his intake, or simply the fact that his resonance profile defies easy categorization within standard frameworks. His title, Phase Transition, refers both to his Forte designation and to the thematic arc of his own life: a man who has undergone a fundamental shift in state, from one mode of existence to another. Operating under the jurisdiction of the New Federation and stationed within the nation of Lahai-Roi, Luuk holds the position of attending medical doctor and mental health counselor at Startorch Academy's Resonator Nursing Unit, making him simultaneously responsible for the physical treatment of injured or overtaxed Resonators and for their psychological care. This dual portfolio, body and mind, reflects an integrated approach to Resonator welfare that Startorch Academy, under President Lucilla's leadership, has come to depend upon. His role within the Academy extends far beyond bandaging wounds and filing prescriptions: he stands as one of the few figures on campus whom students and staff alike trust with their most vulnerable moments, from post-combat trauma recovery to the quiet anxieties that accompany life in an institution built atop the ruins of a Lament.]

## Appearance

[Luuk Herssen's appearance is immediately striking and carefully composed. His light-gold hair falls with pale green highlights that curl at the tips, creating a soft, almost sunlit effect that contrasts sharply with the intensity of his eyes. His irises are red, deep and vivid, gleaming like blood, carrying what observers describe as a "fleeting hint of danger" that contradicts his otherwise gentle demeanor. Those eyes see less than they appear to. Long-term use of Ichor cost him his color vision, and gold is the only hue that still reaches him, everything else arriving in grayscale. As Startorch Academy's attending doctor, he wears a teal-and-white overcoat that places him squarely within the institution's professional visual language, and he is rarely seen without white gloves, an accessory that serves practical medical purposes but also reads as a deliberate barrier between his hands and the world, as though the surgeon's discipline has never fully left him. The gloves also conceal the most telling mark on him. In the center of his right palm is an open wound that does not heal, and that wound is his Tacet Mark, the point at which his body and his Forte meet.]

## Forte: Phase Transition

[Phase Transition is the Forte associated with Luuk Herssen, filed in the records of the Spacetrek Collective under Startorch Academy's designation. Its mechanism is the fluid that runs in him. An anomalous energy fluid known as Ichor circulates through his body, and it can alter its phase state rapidly at his conscious command, shifting between states as he directs. The name of the Forte is literal. Using it is not painless. Every application brings a burning sensation and a transient rise in his body temperature, and the accumulated cost of years of use is the color blindness that leaves him able to see gold and little else. Where Ichor came from is not recorded anywhere in the Collective's archives, and the question remains unanswered. The Rabelle's Curve Fluctuation Stability Test performed on Luuk revealed a resonant signature characterized by an elongated elliptical waveform with considerable amplitude, indicating raw power of significant magnitude. Crucially, however, the test also documented a clear periodic rhythm within this high-amplitude pattern, and confirmed that Resonant Criticality thresholds have not been approached, meaning that despite the substantial energy his Forte channels, Luuk maintains stable control over his resonance and does not risk the catastrophic loss-of-self associated with criticality events. This combination of high power and stable periodicity is unusual. Most Resonators whose waveforms exhibit such amplitude tend to show signs of instability approaching criticality, but Luuk's curve remains clean and rhythmically predictable, a depth of self-regulation consistent with his medical training and his psychological discipline. What the curve measures, in the end, is a man whose own body is the vessel and the instrument, since the Ichor he commands has no existence apart from him and no effect that does not pass through him first.]

## Personality

[To the students and staff of Startorch Academy, Luuk Herssen presents as warm, approachable, and fundamentally kind, a doctor who listens more than he speaks, who treats every patient with genuine attention, and who maintains an unflappable composure even in the aftermath of combat injuries, Resonance overload incidents, or the emotional crises that afflict young Resonators far from home. This warmth is not a performance, or at least not entirely: those who know him well describe a man whose friendliness is authentic and whose care for others is real. Yet beneath this gentle exterior lies a past defined by blood and lies, specifics that Luuk has never fully disclosed but that occasionally surface in passing remarks, in the way his red eyes narrow at certain topics, or in the tension that Aemeath once observed in his bearing before Rover's visit to Lahai-Roi changed something in him. What defines Luuk most is not this dark history but his active, relentless refusal to be determined by it. He has committed himself to a philosophy of cutting open lies to reveal the truth within, a conviction that manifests both in his medical practice (diagnosis as an act of revelation, peeling away symptoms to find root causes) and in his approach to the political and personal deceptions that surround him. He is a man who understands darkness intimately and has chosen, deliberately and daily, to practice light.]

## History

[Luuk Herssen's history before his appointment at Startorch Academy is shrouded in deliberate obscurity, described in official records only as "a mysterious past" and in his own words as "blood and lies." Brother sets down the beginning of it. A cold light glaring deep into the night, a child sitting beneath it and holding out his hand without protest while the man he called "father" gripped his palm. That father had lost his own biological son to a rare disease, and afterward he pursued a cure by using Luuk as a vessel for it. Luuk endured what killed others, being one of the few able to tolerate Ichor at all. Rhein came into the household later: a sick child with no blood relation to any of them, brought in and given the name of brother. The bond between them was real, but it was built on a household organized around illness, substitution, and a cure that was never meant for either of them.]

[What Luuk did with that inheritance is recorded in Undercurrent. He took control of the Novialle Regenerative Group after his father's death and shut down the Golden Blood research laboratories outright, ending the line of work that had been conducted on his own body. He did not stop there. Suspecting that his father's death was connected to Fractsidus, he infiltrated the organization to find out. Whether Fractsidus was in fact responsible, and what truly happened to his father, are questions he has not been able to close. They remain open, and he continues to carry them.]

[After the Thaw records the price he was willing to pay for Rhein. When Rhein had to be restrained, Luuk used his own body as the anchor to hold him, turning the vessel his father had made of him into a restraint of his own choosing. It is the clearest statement of what separates the two men: the father spent Luuk to save a son already gone, while Luuk spent himself to hold onto the brother he still had. He eventually chose medicine as his vocation, bringing to it the same intensity and precision that his earlier life demanded of him. The arrival of Rover in Lahai-Roi appears to have marked another turning point: Aemeath's observations indicate that Luuk carried a constant tension before this event, as if bracing for battle at any moment, and that the change in him, a relaxation, a settling into his medical identity more fully, coincided with Rover's presence and the events their visit set in motion.]

## Affiliations

[Luuk Herssen's web of affiliations places him at a distinctive intersection of Lahai-Roi's institutional landscape. Startorch Academy is his primary professional home, where he leads the Resonator Nursing Unit and serves as both physician and counselor. The Spacetrek Collective, Lahai-Roi's premier research organization dedicated to space-time discovery and the understanding of the fractured world beyond ordinary boundaries, claims Luuk as well, in a capacity that bridges their medical research division with his clinical practice. Beyond both, he holds the Novialle Regenerative Group by inheritance, a corporate authority he has used chiefly to close things down rather than expand them. His origin in the New Federation and his current station within the nation of Lahai-Roi place him in a cross-cultural position, shaped by the Federation's military and institutional traditions but now operating within Lahai-Roi's more research-oriented and survival-focused society. His connection to the broader conflict against Fractsidus, and specifically to the figure of Schwarzloch (the Grand Architect), adds a layer of unresolved enmity to his profile. Luuk has spoken of wrenching the entirety of Fractsidus from their roots and casting them into the abyss they carved, and he entered the organization himself on the suspicion that it had a hand in his father's death, a suspicion that has never been confirmed or dismissed. This combination of healer, researcher, and someone with a personal vendetta against one of the world's most dangerous factions makes Luuk Herssen one of the most complex figures operating within the Startorch Academy ecosystem, a man whose present compassion is inseparable from the violence he has survived and the justice he still intends to see fulfilled.]
