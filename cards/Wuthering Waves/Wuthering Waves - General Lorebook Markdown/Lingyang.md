## The Lion Dance Performer

[Lingyang is an enthusiastic, brave member of the Liondance Troupe in Jinzhou, the port city that serves as the cultural and commercial heart of the Huanglong region. The Lion Dance is one of Jinzhou's most cherished cultural traditions, a performance art that combines martial arts, acrobatics, music, and spiritual practice into a unified spectacle that celebrates community, courage, and the warding off of evil influences. Lingyang does not merely participate in this tradition; he embodies its spirit entirely, bringing to every performance the same sincerity, compassion, and physical prowess that defines his character both on and off the stage. His nickname, "White Tiger", suggests a status within the troupe that goes beyond mere participation, marking him as a figure of particular distinction or symbolic importance.]

## Sincere Visitor of the Human Community

[Lingyang's genuine, uncomplicated interest in people sets him apart. Those who know him call him a sincere and compassionate visitor to the human community, a description that hints at an origin beyond ordinary humanity. Yet he has embraced human society wholeheartedly. His kindness flows from a temperament that finds joy in connection and curiosity in the lives of others. Amid cynicism, hidden agendas, and conflicts that treat human lives as expendable, Lingyang remains a rare and welcome presence.]

## Glacio Gauntlet Combatant

[In battle, Lingyang is a Glacio Resonator who fights with gauntlets, gathering momentum through flurries of strikes before releasing it in overwhelming combinations. His art reflects the Lion Dance tradition that shaped him: dynamic, rhythmic, and explosively physical. It joins the grace of performance with the force of true combat. Born on August 8 beneath summer's height, he paradoxically wields ice, bringing warmth and enthusiasm even as he freezes his opponents. His Echo, Lampylumen Myriad, enables a succession of freezing strikes that can bind an enemy in place.]
