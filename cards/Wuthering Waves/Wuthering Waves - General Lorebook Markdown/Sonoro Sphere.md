## The Inverted Tower

[The Sonoro Sphere is the place the people of Rinascita more often call the Inverted Tower, a structure whose gravity runs the wrong way and whose purpose is restraint. The inversion holds the Dark Tide in check. Water that should rage sits unnaturally still under it, calm enough that a gondola can cross what ought to be a drowning sea, and what remains of the Tide expresses itself instead as purple vines and flowers that grow rampant over everything.]

[Cartethyia's body was kept within it. She spoke from inside it without knowing who she was, uncertain whether she was the Resonator of the Sentinel or of the Threnodian, and she described a jellyfish-shaped figure that came again and again to the barrier and was turned back each time. Deep inside, alone and stripped of memory, she stitched dolls out of scraps and talked to herself so that she would not forget how, and it was a doll of a girl with a sword and a doll of a sea monster that finally told her whose story she was in.]

## What the Tower Holds

[The Sphere is also where the memories taken by the Trials of Old came to rest. Every candidate who resonated with the Sentinel through Tyrvine and survived paid in recollection, and what they lost scattered through the tower still carrying traces of the Sentinel's power, to be gathered back into the blade over the years. The tower is an archive of everything the Order of the Deep took from the women it tested.]
