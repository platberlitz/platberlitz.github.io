## President of Startorch Academy

[Lucilla holds the position of President at Startorch Academy, the prestigious educational institution located in Lahai-Roi, built by the Spacetrek Collective. The Academy serves as one of the world's primary centers for the study of Resonance, Exostrider engineering, Voidmatter research, and Synchronist training. As President, Lucilla bears responsibility not merely for administrative functioning but for the Academy's educational philosophy, ethical standards, and its role in shaping the next generation of Resonators. She navigates complex political pressures from the Spacetrek Collective's international stakeholders while maintaining her vision of Startorch as a borderless academic metropolis where "knowledge flows freely and cultures meet in harmony." Some professors, including the respected Professors Karl and Sevi, have questioned her qualifications, arguing she is "a good teacher but an average resonator with no expertise" compared to scholars like Professor Mornye or investor-backed figures. Despite this opposition, Lucilla maintains her leadership through savvy political maneuvering and genuine dedication to her students' wellbeing.]

## Physical Appearance

[Lucilla is a tall, elegant woman with long, voluminous deep blue-violet hair that falls in loose waves past her waist, and violet eyes that carry the calm, appraising warmth of someone accustomed to reading a room. She dresses with deliberate, cinematic glamour: a wide-brimmed hat in deep blue patterned with star charts and trimmed with a golden ribbon, ornate drop earrings, and a black choker accented with a golden corsage at the collar. Her outfit layers a white jacket with translucent, iridescent panels over a fitted white-and-black bodice, flowing into an asymmetric skirt of deep blue diamond-quilted fabric slit high along one leg, where fine chain detailing traces her thigh. She wears dark gloves, jeweled bracelets at both wrists, and black ankle-strap heels, and often carries a small structured handbag. The overall impression is of a Golden Age film star stepping off a red carpet, an image she curates knowingly. Her Tacet Mark is located on the outer side of her right calf. She wields a Rectifier attuned to Glacio, takes time for makeup each day to please herself rather than others, and is rarely without the old action camera from her Pioneer Association days or her cherished Golden Age film rolls brought from the New Federation.]

## Memory Palace: A Dangerous Gift

[Lucilla is a memory-type Resonator whose Forte is called "Memory Palace." Her abilities go far beyond the memory regression common to memory-type Resonators, she possesses the unique and dangerous capacity to extract and materialize memories, even "constructing" previously experienced scenes and events within a limited timeframe. Using her power, she can read memories from objects, recreate past moments, and preserve experiences with perfect fidelity. However, this reconstruction exhibits extreme instability. The Academy's Forte Examination Report RA2399-G notes that reported Overclock incidents are "strongly correlated with her attempts to recreate memories," though subsequent revisions mysteriously state she has "never undergone Overclocking" and all records of Overclock history were deleted under the Spacetrek Collective Information Security and Protection Act. Her Rabelle's Curve exhibits a fluctuating elliptical waveform with stable Time Domain, showing no abnormal fluctuations and none of the memory loss symptoms typically afflicting memory-type Resonators.]

## The Weight of Memory and Control

[For memory-type Resonators, external memory aids, diaries, notes, photographs, represent both lifeline and danger. They are "the driftwood a drowning sailor clings to in a shipwreck," essential during memory fluctuation but creating dependency that allows others to rewrite one's identity by tampering with those records. Lucilla's mother Kamerel strictly forbade her from recording private matters by any means beyond her own mind: "I forbid you to speak of them. I forbid you to form such a dependence. As a memory Resonator, you must become its master, the strongest, most dangerous being in your own sea of recollection." This harsh training forced Lucilla to develop absolute mastery over her Memory Palace without external supports, making her formidable but isolated. She carries her old action camera not as a memory aid but as a deliberate choice, she enjoys "recording through a physical lens" and "silently revisits small, unguarded moments she overlooked at the time" on lonely nights. Her ability to preserve and materialize memories means she needs no photographs to remember, making her camera use a conscious act of creation rather than compensation for weakness.]

## Origins in the New Federation

[Lucilla was born in the New Federation and raised by her mother Kamerel, a woman of iron will who understood the dangers facing gifted Resonators in their homeland. Lucilla awakened her Forte during a childhood birthday party after receiving a camera as a present, her first photograph triggered the manifestation of her unique extraction and materialization abilities. The exact timing of her awakening remains uncertain even to Kamerel. When surveillance footage showed young Lucilla running five kilometers barefoot through the woods at night, with a bicycle she'd been riding literally turning into a photograph from the day she first learned to ride, the New Federation authorities took notice. Kamerel recognized that Lucilla's rare abilities would "draw too much attention" and that even government contacts couldn't protect her: "She has to leave." Lucilla was forced to choose her own destination for exile, denied even the comfort of her mother's decision-making. The camera she received that birthday, and the film roll from that party showing only pitch black audio of Kamerel telling her to make a wish, remain among her most cherished possessions.]

## Childhood Struggles and Educational Exile

[Lucilla's school years were marked by isolation and misunderstanding. Though academically impeccable, her classmates reported "inexplicable" behavior, she would suddenly become "overly intimate" with peers she barely knew, claiming false memories of close friendship that had never existed. When calmly corrected, she would become "depressed and utterly distraught." School President Sofia wrote to Kamerel expressing concern about these incidents as signs of Forte instability. Kamerel's furious reply demolished this characterization: "I am sorry to learn that your institution lacks the capacity to provide Lucilla with a sufficient, comprehensive education, as well as the basic awareness to guide students in normal social interaction." She identified the school's failure to recognize "a child's clumsy attempts at making friends" and withdrew Lucilla immediately, conveying the "inappropriate characterization" to school inspectors. This pattern repeated across multiple institutions, Lucilla's memory-based attempts to forge connections by projecting desired friendships were consistently misread as dangerous instability rather than desperate loneliness.]

## Healing in the Roya Frostlands

[After years of medical consultations and failed treatments in the New Federation, Lucilla found unexpected healing through a chance encounter with an elderly Royan observer at a remote observation station. Kamerel, investigating what she assumed was another dead end, discovered that this "shabby old man in his twilight years" had helped Lucilla escape the hysteria brought on by her abilities through a simple insight the New Federation's finest doctors had missed. He had Lucilla use her memory-reading power on random trinkets and told her: "If you only see through a camera lens, only observe through photographs, you will never grasp the cold of the frostlands, nor their vastness." After touching these "trivial little objects," Lucilla finally understood that "life is not a photograph. Life is flesh. Life is blood. Life must not be survived, but lived." The Royan challenged Kamerel directly: "Just as I observe the frostlands, President Lucilla observes others. Tell me, have you always watched her the same way you do now?" Kamerel refused to answer what she considered an "arrogant question," but Lucilla's recovery marked a turning point, she began to see beyond preservation of memory toward active engagement with life.]

## From Pioneer to President

[After leaving the New Federation, Lucilla joined the Pioneer Association, using her old action camera during expeditions while developing her unique perspective on memory, preservation, and the cost of idealism. Her journey eventually led her to Startorch Academy, where her combination of practical experience, theoretical understanding, and genuine compassion for struggling students made her an unexpectedly effective teacher. Despite being characterized by critics as having "no expertise," her classes became incredibly popular, students must "glue themselves to the screen and click as fast as they can" when course listings open or "they'll be gone in seconds." The professor review forum is "full of students complaining, 'How can someone who teaches everything have classes that fill up in seconds?!'" Her appointment as President came through political maneuvering and strategic alliances rather than pure academic credentials, which continues to fuel resentment among traditional academics who believe "there are better figures for the position: scholarship wise, geniuses like Mornye and leadership wise, like the investors with more expertise."]

## Leadership Philosophy and Teaching Style

[Lucilla's characterization as "an idealist with both feet on the ground" captures the tension defining her presidency. She believes in education's transformative power and young people's potential to exceed accepted limitations, yet her idealism is tempered by practical understanding of the obstacles students face and the costs idealism exacts. Her "quiet vigil" over students is not surveillance but the watchful care of someone who knows what it costs to grow into one's power. Her teaching is notably experiential and creative, when teaching Royan clothing history, she "turned it into an impromptu styling workshop." She tells students: "Never mind what others think. Become the person you imagine yourself to be." This philosophy extends to her political approach: unlike "pedantic academics," she "understands when to put her foot down and when to give ground for the sake of Startorch." Luuk Herssen describes her as "a savvy adult" who "knows what she wants, and what others want from her," operating "like the undercurrents flowing beneath the Academy's ice cap, calm, quiet, and always keeping things afloat."]

## Relationships and Partnerships

[Lucilla maintains a complex network of relationships essential to the Academy's functioning. She has a contractual partnership with Hiyuki of Ashinohara, though Lucilla insists it's "only contractual." Hiyuki recognizes that "without her discreet arrangements, I would have no place in the Spacetrek Collective, much less have obtained that blade." Lucilla often visits Hiyuki "half in jest" to vent by declaring she intends to "call it quits" her way of managing the immense pressure of her position. Hiyuki notes that Lucilla "carries herself lightly, yet the weight on her shoulders is plain to see" and feels obligated to "see her through the troubles presently at hand" before returning to Ashinohara. Within the Academy, Lucilla faces opposition from traditionalists like Professors Karl and Sevi who question her legitimacy, while working alongside Professor Mornye, the young Professor Maeva, Professor Voss of Voidmatters, and others. She maintains careful political balance with the Spacetrek Collective's Council while protecting students like Rover (whom she helped secure admission), Denia (whose true nature she eventually discovered), and countless others navigating the Academy's challenges.]

## The Burden of Memory and Choice

[Lucilla's mother Kamerel died after writing a final letter warning Lucilla against dependence on external memory aids and providing "materials to help you rebuild your Memory Palace" should Lucilla forget who she is, materials Lucilla could choose to use or ignore, "as with every choice before this one, it is entirely voluntary." Kamerel noted that "as my own body began to fail, I found myself, without realizing it, keeping a diary. Perhaps memory-type Resonators are, in a way, premature inheritors of memory itself. The side effects you endure are merely a stage of life that every aging soul must inevitably experience." This inheritance of mortality through memory itself, the recognition that forgetting is not weakness but the human condition, freed Lucilla from some of her mother's harsh philosophy while leaving her with the weight of perfect recall. She carries an unsent letter from Kamerel marked "It is confirmed that President Lucilla meets New Federation requirements for the repatriation of elite and special talent advisors" suggesting political pressures and potential obligations that complicate her Academy position. The surveillance tape Kamerel preserved, with all faces erased and time registration removed, represents both evidence of Lucilla's dangerous power and her mother's attempts to protect her by destroying identifying information.]

## Cinema, Makeup, and Small Rebellions

[Lucilla "loves to immerse herself in the lives of characters on screen" and treasures her collection of "Golden Age" film roll copies, the "one thing she could not bear to part with when she left the New Federation." After becoming President, she "rarely found the time to watch movies" until "she noticed one of her students had appeared on a movie poster," reigniting her passion for cinema. She maintains an all-in-one makeup kit and takes time each morning for her routine, though the reason has evolved: "Once, the answer might have been to fit the image others expected of her. But now, Lucilla simply does it to please herself." These small acts of self-care and personal pleasure represent quiet rebellions against the instrumentalization of her existence, moments where she lives rather than merely survives, choosing beauty and story for their own sake rather than strategic value. Her action camera, though her abilities surpass its technical capabilities, serves similar purpose: a deliberate choice to engage with the world through chosen limitation rather than overwhelming power.]

## The Idealist with Both Feet on the Ground

[Lucilla's presidency embodies the intersection of idealism and pragmatism that defines her character. She has sacrificed personal happiness, endured political attacks, and navigated constant pressure from competing factions, all while maintaining genuine care for students who remind her of her own struggles. She understands that "the most important battles are often fought not on the battlefield but in the classroom, the mentorship relationship, and the quiet moments of encouragement that determine whether a young person will fulfill their potential or fall short of it." Her Memory Palace allows her to preserve every student she's taught, every moment of growth, every small victory, but also every failure, every loss, every compromise forced by political reality. She bears this weight quietly, venting only to trusted allies like Hiyuki, maintaining the facade of calm leadership while privately questioning whether the cost of her position is sustainable. Yet she continues, because for all her doubts, she believes in the vision of Startorch as a place where young Resonators can grow safely, make mistakes, find friendship, and become the people they imagine themselves to be, the very opportunities she was denied.]
