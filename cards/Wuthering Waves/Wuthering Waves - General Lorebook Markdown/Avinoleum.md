## The Great Seminary

[Avinoleum is the largest seminary the Order of the Deep ever raised in Rinascita, a monument of stone and stained glass built to house the training of Acolytes and the keeping of scripture. It was also the origin point of the second Dark Tide. Whatever reverence the building was meant to command, what it is remembered for now is what came out of it.]

[Above the seminary stands the Holy Spire of Confluence, and atop the Spire sits the throne where Fleurdelys waits. Those who describe the place speak of a prison hung in the heavens that feeds on the glory of the past, and of a divine seat wrapped in thorns whose occupant is waiting for her own death. The otherworldly being that slumbers there was presented to the mortal realm as a prophecy bestowed by the Divinity, which is one way of describing a thing that was manufactured.]

## The Three Pedestals

[Within Avinoleum stand three pedestals that must each receive a blade before the way upward opens. Tyrvine, the Sword of Divinity, carries the frequency of the Sentinel Imperator. Hognis, the Sword of Discord, carries the frequency of the Threnodian Leviathan. Haultir, the Sword of Virtue, belongs to Cartethyia alone and carries nothing but her. Set together, the three describe the whole of what Fleurdelys was made from and the one thing she was not made from.]

[The pedestals do not merely open a door. Each one surrenders a vision to whoever sets a blade upon it, and the third of them gives up the conversation between the Threnodian and its Resonator, in which Leviathan claims authorship of her life, her hopes, her will, and her past, and demands to know why she persists. The seminary keeps its worst confession for the moment before the ascent.]
