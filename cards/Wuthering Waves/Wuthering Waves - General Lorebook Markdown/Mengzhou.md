# Mengzhou

## Overview

[Mengzhou is a province of Huanglong and one of its six major cities, renowned for its abundant waterways, thriving greenery, and enchanting landscapes. Commerce and Taoism thrive there and coexist in harmony. Under the guidance of its Sentinel, Hsin, the people of Mengzhou developed unique automata mechanics, an art of constructing intelligent machines that has been deployed in the land of Xuanfang as a bulwark against the Tacet Discord threat, a role it filled especially before the founding of Jinzhou. Mengzhou borders Jinzhou, separated only by Xuanfang, which falls under the jurisdiction of Mengzhou's frontier court. The province is also famed for its rockery gardens, known as the Mengzhou Garden Style.]

## Xuanfang Hold

[Xuanfang Hold is a suspended mechanized hold under Mengzhou's authority, built to contain the Tacet Discords diverted from the Norfall Barren outbreak. Intricately constructed, it splits and traps incoming waves of Tacet Discords through a vast array of mechanisms. Its form can change at will, deploying autopuppets to rotate its defenses and to purge or suppress the Tacet Discords held inside. The Hold stands on the ruins of Yuan Fortress, which was destroyed during the Fortress and Gorges War; the Court of Savantae assisted Hsin in raising the Hold upon those ruins, a choice some members of the Zhuojun Atelier, partners in the original fortress, took as a snub.]

## The Lands of Xuanfang

[The territory surrounding the Hold is divided into distinct regions. The Eastern Xuan Peaks are forested, mountainous, and fog-bound, home to the maze-like Fogveil Pagoda, whose complex structure emits Disorienting Fog all year round; the fog carries frequencies that bewilder Tacet Discords and guide them into traps. The Western Fang Peaks house the Skyworks, a factory comprising the Thousandfold Foundry, which manufactures autopuppets, and the Myriad Snare, built for the sole purpose of destroying powerful Tacet Discords; both facilities run day and night in support of Xuanfang Hold. The Southern Yuan Hills sit beside the gateway between the territory and the outside world, where the way post called Shaftpost Yard subjects all goods, wares, and travelers to inspection by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.]

## The Xuanfang Wardens and the Xuan Triad

[Under the Ministry of War in Mengzhou, the soldiers who guard Xuanfang Hold are known as the Xuanfang Wardens. They serve as the military of Xuanfang, working alongside the autopuppets in the region's defense. The Xuan Triad is a special unit of three elite Wardens, whose members are also called Xuan Watchers, tasked with handling incidents beyond the capabilities of the standard Wardens. Notable figures include the Paragon Qingxiao, Warden Commander Qiuhong, and Yangyang, who serves as a temporary Xuan Watcher. The frontier has claimed its share of lives; Xuan Watchers Duzhong and Qingzhi and Wardens Yanlang and Mingyu fell during the recent storm of troubles that swept through Xuanfang.]

## History and Culture

[Mengzhou's history is woven through Huanglong's martial and scholarly traditions. A Martial Arts Championship among Huanglong's sects once took place on a lone mountain peak there, where Buling and Jianxin, disciples of Master Jiufeng and Master Shiling respectively, fought; after the bout, a mysterious white-robed master carrying a guqin confronted Buling about conceding the fight. The province's blend of mercantile prosperity, garden artistry, automata craft, and frontier vigilance makes it a place where Huanglong's oldest traditions and its most inventive engineering stand side by side.]
