## The Sword of Virtue

[Haultir is the Sword of Virtue, and it is Cartethyia's own. It is the third blade the pedestals of Avinoleum demand, and it is the only one of the three that no Sentinel forged and no Threnodian claimed. Tyrvine carries Imperator. Hognis carries Leviathan. Haultir carries nothing but the woman who wields it.]

[That is precisely why it is required. The way to the throne atop the Holy Spire of Confluence does not open for divinity and discord alone, because the being waiting there was never only their product. Something in her was hers, kept back through the crowning and the Dark Tide and the splitting, and Haultir is the shape that something takes when she puts her hand to it. A blade of virtue set between a blade of divinity and a blade of discord is an argument about what she is, made in steel.]
