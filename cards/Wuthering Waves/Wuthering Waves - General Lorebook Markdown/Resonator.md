## Definition and Nature

[A Resonator is a human being who possesses the extraordinary capacity to resonate with Frequency Waves permeating the environment of Solaris-3. This resonance is not merely a passive sensitivity but an active channel through which the Resonator can perceive, manipulate, and weaponize the invisible vibrational energies that course through the world. Unlike ordinary humans, whose bodies and senses are deaf to these frequencies, a Resonator's physiology is fundamentally attuned to the harmonic undercurrents of reality itself, making them uniquely suited to interact with the forces that shape the post-Lament world.]

## Role in Society

[In the fractured civilization of Solaris-3, Resonators occupy a critical societal role. They serve as both protectors and explorers, venturing into territories overrun by Tacet Discords and other hostile entities that emerged after the Lament. Their ability to resonate allows them to combat these threats directly, absorbing the residual frequencies left behind by defeated enemies and converting them into Echoes, which further amplify their power. Resonators are also essential to the operation of key infrastructure such as Resonance Beacons and Resonance Nexuses, devices that form the backbone of rapid resonance transit, surveying, and recovery efforts across the world.]

## Combat Abilities

[Resonators wield diverse powers through their resonance. Their affinities, Glacio, Fusion, Electro, Aero, Spectro, and Havoc, shape their means of attack and protection. In battle, exertion and evasion gather resonant strength until a Resonator can release their Resonance Liberation, a culminating art unique to the individual. Resonators who fight together may also join their powers through Concerto, harmonizing their frequencies as one combatant yields the field to another.]

## Echoes and Power Sources

[One of the most distinctive aspects of a Resonator's power is the ability to absorb and attune to Echoes. When a Tacet Discord falls, its remaining frequency may crystallize into an Echo, a fragment of its essence. A Resonator may invoke an attuned Echo's power, drawing upon the traits of a former foe. Thus each encounter can leave behind knowledge and resonance that deepen a survivor's art.]

## Variability Among Resonators

[No two Resonators resonate in exactly the same way. Innate aptitude, elemental alignment, weapon mastery, and personal history give each manifestation its own form. Some stand at the foremost line, while others protect, heal, or direct their companions. The Rover, a mysterious Resonator able to absorb all sounds, shows how strange these gifts may become. Such diversity is a strength, though it demands careful coordination from the organizations that defend Solaris-3.]
