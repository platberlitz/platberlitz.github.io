Existing comment: Resonance Beacons

[Resonance Beacons are resonance-attuned devices distributed across Solaris-3. A traveler who reaches a beacon may attune to it, making it an anchor to which they can return through resonance from another attuned beacon. Beacons are commonly paired with Resonance Nexuses, and together they sustain post-Lament travel and communication.]

[In the plural sense, "Resonance Beacons" denotes the global network that forms the backbone of post-Lament infrastructure. An attuned traveler can pass between its anchors through resonance with remarkable speed. Those wounded on the road or in battle make for the nearest Resonance Nexus, where its ambient field tends the injured. The network enables the swift movement of Resonator companies, communication between distant settlements, and frequency stabilization along vital routes. The Tethys System is linked to the network for observation and threat detection. In regions where Lament contamination destabilizes the land, Beacons also anchor stabilizing frequencies and aid the containment of resonant dangers.]
