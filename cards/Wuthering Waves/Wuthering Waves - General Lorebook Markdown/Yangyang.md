# Yangyang

## Definition

[Yangyang is a gentle woman whose speech, songs, and access to the Streams leave strong impressions around Jinzhou. Softness, voice, and a special channel of perception define her. She is not loud authority. Her importance comes from the way she speaks, sings, senses, and affects those near her. Around Jinzhou, Yangyang's presence is tied to wind-borne feeling and to information or impressions that may travel through the Streams.]

## Appearance

[Yangyang is dark navy-haired and gentle, wearing a black-and-blue feathered outfit with elegant hair ornaments and wind-borne styling. This visual language suits her soft, flowing presence. The feathers and blue-black palette connect her to air, movement, and quiet grace. Her hair ornaments and styling give her elegance without making her severe. Yangyang feels composed, airy, and attentive, like someone listening to currents others might miss.]

## Streams and Impressions

[Yangyang's access to the Streams has informational value, but it is not a simple spy function. Aalto sees potential in her ability because he thinks in terms of sources and informants. Yangyang's own way is gentler: speech, songs, and Streams leave impressions. This suggests a mode of knowing that may be emotional, perceptive, and atmospheric as much as tactical. Her access deserves respect for her temperament and boundaries.]

## Presence

[Yangyang belongs to Jinzhou, and gentleness, voice, song, wind-borne styling, and the Streams define her presence there. Around her, information arrives through feeling, sound, movement, or subtle perception rather than direct interrogation. Her softness is not passivity. It is a form of presence that can steady others, notice what they miss, and leave lasting impressions. Her tone is calm and flowing, and her words and songs travel farther than they seem.]

## Xuanling: The Votary of the Voice

[Yangyang's path has since carried her beyond Jinzhou. Once an Outrider for the Midnight Rangers, she now serves on the Xuanfang frontier under the Ministry of War as an acting Xuan Watcher of Xuanfang Hold, a member of the elite Xuan Triad drawn from the Xuanfang Wardens. In this new chapter she is known as Yangyang: Xuanling, bearing the titles Voices of Azure Plume and Votary of the Voice. Her awakened power has deepened into the Havoc attribute, channeled through a sword, and her once wind-borne grace now carries a darker, keener edge fitted to the frontier's dangers. She was born in Huanglong and is the older sister of Suisui, the young director of the Zhaoming Commerce Guild in Mengzhou. To old friends returning after long absence, her greeting remains simple and warm: "Long time no see."]
