# Clamorling

## Definition

[Clamorling-type Tacet Discords are a poorly understood Tacet Discord class. Their classification remains incomplete compared with clearer mimicry types such as Whisperin, Howler, Tranquilite, and Staticoid. That uncertainty is the defining feature of the class. No fully mapped origin, behavior chart, or visible rule set has been established for it.]

## Classification Gap

[The comparison to clearer types gives the shape of the problem. Whisperin and Murmurin relate to humanlike traces, Howlers imitate flora or fauna, Tranquilites imitate inorganic natural matter, and Staticoids mimic or parasitize man-made objects. Clamorling has no comparably stable mimicry description. It is still a Tacet Discord class, but surviving records leave its exact criteria unresolved. The class represents the limits of current categorization in a world where chaotic frequency keeps producing exceptions.]

## Handling Uncertainty

[Because Clamorlings are poorly understood, researchers treat the class with disciplined restraint. It stands as a recognized but incomplete category within Tacet Discord taxonomy, and specific claims about its appearance, ecology, intelligence, or behavior remain unverified. The uncertainty itself has a use. Clamorling serves as a marker for field reports that do not fit known types, research files with open questions, and encounters where the classification system itself fails to settle the danger.]

## Limits of Knowledge

[The Clamorling class marks a boundary of knowledge. The post-Lament world contains Tacet Discords whose mimicry can be named, but it also contains classes where the name precedes full understanding. Clamorling belongs in that second category. It shares Tacet Discord fundamentals, such as chaotic frequency formation, Reverberation Bodies, frequency consumption, and possible Echo traces, yet its incomplete status distinguishes it from the better described mimicry classes around it.]
