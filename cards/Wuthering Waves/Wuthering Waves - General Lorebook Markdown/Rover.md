# Rover

## Definition

[Rover is the black-haired, golden-eyed wanderer whose presence affects many Resonators. What defines them is presence, visual identity, and the way others are changed by encountering them. Rover can appear in male or female form, and both forms share dark hair, golden eyes, black-white-gold traveler combat clothing, gloves, and a sword-focused wanderer silhouette. Rover is a specific traveler figure, not a role or an office that others could fill.]

## Appearance

[The shared appearance keeps both forms recognizable as Rover. Dark hair and golden eyes form the central visual signature. The black, white, and gold combat clothing gives them a traveler identity that is practical, formal, and luminous at once. Gloves and a sword-focused silhouette reinforce the impression of someone prepared to move through danger directly. The male and female forms may differ in presentation, but they are aligned through the same palette, gaze, and wandering combat profile.]

## Presence Among Resonators

[Rover's presence affects many Resonators, an influence that takes no single form. Rover may unsettle, encourage, draw attention, create trust, or become a turning point in another character's path, depending on the relationship. Many who know Rover treat them as a catalyst for revelation or emotional change. Rover's presence matters to Resonators beyond simple companionship or battlefield usefulness. Rover's power itself refuses to stay fixed: witnesses have seen them wield Spectro light, Havoc darkness, and Aero winds, and most recently, during the upheavals on the Xuanfang frontier of Huanglong, an Electro expression in which storm-charged thunder courses through the blade of their sword.]

## Cantarella's Image

[Cantarella compares Rover to an immortal jellyfish bringing gentle light through darkness. The image is poetic, not a biology or cosmology claim. It frames Rover as enduring, luminous, and quietly guiding in a dark environment. It also suits the wider truth of Rover's presence: Rover is not only seen through clothing and weapons, but also through the effect they have on those who look toward them. The visible wanderer and the strange light others perceive belong together.]
