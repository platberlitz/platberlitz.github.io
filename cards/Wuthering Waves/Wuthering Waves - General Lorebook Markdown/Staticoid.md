# Staticoid

## Definition

[Staticoid-type Tacet Discords mimic or parasitize man-made objects. Tools, structures, devices, and other artificial objects touched by chaotic frequency are the kinds of forms associated with them. This makes Staticoids distinct from Tacet Discord types that imitate human bodies, animals, plants, stone, or minerals. Their point of reference is artificial matter shaped by people, then distorted or occupied by post-Lament frequency.]

## Artificial Forms

[A Staticoid may resemble something built, used, carried, inhabited, or engineered. Their nature includes both mimicry and parasitism, which allows two readings. Some Staticoids may form a Reverberation Body that imitates an artificial object. Others may attach themselves to or infest man-made objects touched by chaotic frequency. In either case, the objectlike appearance is not safe. A familiar tool, structure, or device can become a surface through which Tacet Discord behavior enters daily life.]

## Contrast With Other Types

[Staticoids are easiest to understand by contrast. Whisperin and Murmurin point toward humanlike forms and behavior. Howlers point toward flora and fauna. Tranquilites point toward earth, stone, minerals, and nonliving natural matter. Staticoids point toward the made environment: tools, structures, devices, and other artificial objects. That boundary keeps classification clear. A mineral imitation is not a Staticoid only because it is hard, and a machine-like or tool-like imitation is not a Tranquilite only because it seems nonliving.]

## Threat to Civilization

[Staticoids make civilization itself vulnerable to chaotic frequency. The post-Lament threat does not stop at wilderness, ruins, or monsters in open fields. It can reach objects people trust because those objects were built for use, shelter, work, or communication. The entity remains a Tacet Discord type that mimics or parasitizes man-made objects, with a frequency-born nature and a connection to the same Echo and Remnant Energy ecology as other Tacet Discords.]
