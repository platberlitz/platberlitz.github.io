## The Herssen Bloodline

[Rhein Herssen is Luuk Herssen's brother, a Spectro Resonator who fights with gauntlets and favors the air, an exacting art requiring precision, sustained aerial assaults, and careful conservation of strength. The Herssen name carries weight within the New Federation, whose military traditions emphasize discipline, strategy, and the use of Resonance in organized defense. As Luuk's brother, Rhein is bound by blood to one of the Federation's notable fighters, yet must be defined by his own choices, gifts, and commitments.]

## New Federation Military Traditions

[The New Federation cultivates Resonance through military discipline, in contrast to Huanglong's Taoist schools, the religious orders of Ragunna and Rinascita, and the mercantile practices of other lands. Its forces train and deploy Resonators in the service of organized defense. Rhein's connection to these traditions may arise from military service or from close ties to the establishment, and it shapes his understanding of force and duty. Whether he matches his brother's gifts or serves the Federation by another means is not publicly known.]

## Brotherhood and Its Obligations

[The details of Rhein and Luuk's brotherhood, their obligations to one another, and their understanding of the Herssen name remain unclear. Brothers raised amid military traditions may know competition, mutual protection, shared trauma, and pressure to emulate or depart from each other's path. Luuk's renown as an exceptionally gifted aerial combatant has brought close attention to the family. Rhein's own path, whether it follows, complements, or rejects that legacy, will define him in turn.]
