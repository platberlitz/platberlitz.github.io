## Identity and Title

[Augusta holds the title of Ephor of Septimont, a position at the apex of the city-state's civic and martial hierarchy. Called "the undying sun eternally ablaze," she is both an Electro Resonator and an unextinguishable emblem of Septimont's strength. The office of Ephor bears the weight of governance, adjudication, and military command, and Augusta meets each burden with an iron composure that has earned reverence and fear alike. She embodies Septimont's defiant spirit, a city that prizes courage and looks to a leader whose resolve does not waver before opposition or impossible odds.]

## The Arena and Public Persona

[Augusta is intimately connected to the arena culture of Septimont, where combat is a sacred expression of civic identity and personal worth. Called "a standard beneath which defeat does not exist," her presence on the field or in the stands raises every contest toward myth. The arena is where Septimont's citizens prove themselves, and Augusta stands as both its supreme champion and ultimate judge. Her Electro Broadblade joins overwhelming force to command over time itself. At the height of her power, she can halt the flow of moments and freeze the battlefield, displaying absolute dominion. In suspending the passage of reality, she acts as the authority who determines when and how conflicts end.]

## Defiance of Fate

[Augusta refuses to bow before fate. With blade held high, she awaits the next challenger or the fate that claims it cannot be defied. On Solaris-3, where Tacet Discords and Laments have claimed countless cities and peoples, fate is an existential threat rather than an abstraction. Her defiance is a deliberate act of will and a declaration that Septimont will not yield quietly to oblivion. Her Electro Broadblade, Thunderflare Dominion, makes her a ruler of storms, channeling lightning as weapon and as the symbol of an untamable spirit. Those beside her are sheltered by her storm and heartened to resist whatever doom approaches.]

## Burdens of Leadership

[Beneath the image of the conquering Ephor lies the loneliness of supreme authority. Augusta's office demands that she show neither weakness nor retreat. "She comes, she sees, she conquers" has become an expectation, almost a ritual, fulfilled not for glory but because her people require it. Her Broadblade discipline of gathering prowess, ascendancy, and majesty before a decisive blow mirrors the burdens she bears: martial strength, civic trust, and strategic foresight must be aligned. Augusta carries Septimont's hopes without complaint, for failure is the one luxury her city cannot afford.]
