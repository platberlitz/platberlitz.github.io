## Overview

[Huanglong is one of the major nations of Solaris-3, a vast and culturally rich country whose lands witnessed many of the defining struggles that followed the Lament. Steeped in ancient traditions of scholarship, ceremony, and craft, Huanglong is a nation defined by its deep connection to the natural world, its reverence for the Sentinels, and its determination to rebuild and prosper in the aftermath of the Lament. The nation's name, meaning Yellow Dragon, reflects its mythological heritage and the central importance of dragon symbolism in its cultural identity.]

## Geography and Regions

[Huanglong encompasses diverse territories, with its most prominent settlement being the frontier city of Jinzhou. The nation's geography ranges from lush forests and rolling hills to rugged frontier territories where the influence of the Lament remains strong. Jinzhou itself sits at the edge of civilization, serving as both a bastion of Huanglong's authority and a gateway to the untamed wilderness beyond. The surrounding regions are populated with Tacet Discords, wild creatures, and the lingering environmental effects of the Lament, making travel beyond the city walls a dangerous undertaking. The nation's infrastructure reflects centuries of architectural tradition adapted to the challenges of the post-Lament world. Beyond Jinzhou lies Mengzhou, a province counted among Huanglong's six major cities, famed for its waterways, greenery, and rockery gardens, where commerce and Taoism flourish together under the Sentinel Hsin. Between the two stands the land of Xuanfang, governed by Mengzhou's frontier court and anchored by Xuanfang Hold, a suspended mechanized fortress that traps and suppresses Tacet Discords diverted from the Norfall Barren outbreak. The lands of Xuanfang include the fog-bound Eastern Xuan Peaks with their maze-like Fogveil Pagoda, the Western Fang Peaks where the Skyworks factory forges autopuppets, and the Southern Yuan Hills, whose Shaftpost Yard inspects all traffic passing between the territory and the outside world.]

## Government and Society

[Huanglong is governed through a system that blends traditional authority with the practical necessities of survival in a world reshaped by catastrophe. The nation's leadership works in close cooperation with the Sentinel's Appointed Resonator, who serves as both a spiritual guide and a political advisor. Jinhsi, the current Magistrate of Jinzhou and the Sentinel's Appointed Resonator for the Sentinel Jué, exemplifies this dual role. The government of Huanglong places great emphasis on duty, community, and the collective effort required to maintain civilization against the constant threat of the Tacet Discords. Shell Credits serve as the primary currency, facilitating trade and commerce across the nation.]

## Resonators of Huanglong

[Huanglong is home to many formidable Resonators. Jiyan is a legendary Aero broadblade wielder; Yinlin commands an electric puppet called Zapstring; Calcharo bears an Electro broadblade; and Changli wields Fusion flame beside a sword. Each has served or traveled within Huanglong's territories. Their skill and dedication have made them important to the defense of Huanglong and, at times, of Solaris-3 as a whole.]

## Cultural Identity

[The cultural life of Huanglong is rich with tradition, ceremony, and artistic expression. Dragon imagery pervades the nation's art, architecture, and spiritual practices. The Sentinel Jué, manifesting as a celestial dragon, is deeply embedded in the national consciousness and serves as a symbol of protection and divine favor. Treasures found within Huanglong's territories, such as Loong's Pearl, further emphasize the dragon-centric mythology. The people of Huanglong are characterized by their resilience, their communal spirit, and their unwavering belief that civilization can endure and thrive despite the catastrophes that have befallen the world.]
