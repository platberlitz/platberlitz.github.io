## Core Concept

[Frequency Modulation is the deliberate manipulation, combination, and alteration of frequency waves. A clear expression appears in the codified art of Echo invocation, where one Echo is overlaid upon another to reshape its manifestation. More broadly, Resonators use modulation to form techniques, protective barriers, and controlled interactions with their surroundings.]

## Modulation in Combat Echoes

[In practiced Echo invocation, Modulation combines two Echoes into one stronger manifestation. When one Echo is overlaid upon another already given form, the overlaying Echo supplants the first and absorbs its destructive potential. The first Echo returns to the Resonator's reserve of attuned frequencies and may be invoked again. The process reflects the resonant principle of one waveform overlaying and absorbing another to create constructive interference. It can also awaken latent properties particular to the Echo.]

## Rules and Constraints

[Strict constraints govern Modulation. Only an Echo held in reserve can serve as the modulating source; two Echoes already given form cannot merge. A manifested Echo can withstand only limited modulation before its structure destabilizes. Echoes of greater resonant weight may require a lesser Echo to be reinforced first. These limitations reflect the physics of frequency manipulation, for stable constructive interference requires preparation and cannot be sustained indefinitely.]

## Broader Resonant Applications

[Beyond formal Echo invocation, Frequency Modulation underpins much of a Resonator's art. Elemental techniques deliberately shape frequency waves, from Glacio's binding cold to Fusion's violent bursts. Overclocking and a swift counterstrike are advanced forms of immediate modulation, demanding rapid adjustment of a Resonator's output in response to danger. Echo absorption is likewise modulation, as a Resonator adapts their own spectrum to receive the residual frequencies of a fallen foe.]

## Theoretical Foundations

[The science of Frequency Modulation in Solaris-3 is still being actively studied and refined. Researchers have identified that modulation operates on principles analogous to both constructive wave interference and biological signal transduction, where information is encoded, transmitted, and decoded through frequency patterns. The development of resonant technology such as Resonance Beacons and Resonance Nexuses demonstrates that modulation can be engineered into devices as well as biological systems, expanding the reach of resonant science beyond the innate abilities of individual Resonators. As understanding of frequency manipulation deepens, new applications continue to emerge, promising further advances in both military capability and civilian infrastructure.]
