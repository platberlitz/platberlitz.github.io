# Remnant Energy

## Definition

[Remnant Energy is lingering frequency residue left by life, emotion, memory, and catastrophe. It is what remains after ordinary forms are gone. It is not simply fuel, debris, or magic in a generic sense. It is the persistence of frequency where something once lived, felt, suffered, broke, fought, or was transformed. Because frequency is a deep part of beings and places, Remnant Energy is a way for the past to remain active inside the present.]

## Where It Remains

[Remnant Energy endures in unstable locations, disaster sites, Sonoro Spheres, Echo traces, Tacet Discord remains, and other places where frequency and memory persist after ordinary forms vanish. This gives it a broad but bounded field. It may be found where catastrophe has pressed too hard on reality, where memories have gathered into unusual spaces, or where frequency-born entities have decayed and left patterns behind. Remnant Energy does not require a living body to continue. It can survive the loss of the form that first carried it.]

## Relation to Other Phenomena

[Remnant Energy connects the Lament, Waveworn Phenomena, Tacet Discords, and Echoes without replacing any of them. The Lament can produce or expose it. Waveworn locations may be saturated by it. Tacet Discord remains may carry it. Echoes can preserve usable patterns that belong to the same ecology of lingering frequency. Remnant Energy is residue with memory-bearing and frequency-bearing qualities, not a universal explanation for every supernatural event.]

## Lingering Consequences

[Remnant Energy makes a place or trace feel haunted by frequency rather than by ordinary history alone. It explains why a battlefield, ruin, Sonoro Sphere, Tacet Discord remnant, or Echo trace still matters after visible events have ended. It also gives researchers, Resonators, and survivors a reason to approach old sites carefully. The residue may preserve information, danger, emotion, or instability, and its continued presence shows how the post-Lament world refuses to let all consequences fade cleanly into the past.]
