## Acolyte of Ragunna

[Phoebe is a religious acolyte from Ragunna, the Italian-inspired region whose culture is deeply intertwined with traditions of faith, artistic devotion, and the complex institutional structures that mediate between individual believers and the concept of Divinity. As a Spectro-element Rectifier user, Phoebe's connection to the light-aligned element reflects her immersion in religious practice and the spiritual disciplines that Ragunna's institutions cultivate. Her status as an acolyte suggests that she is in the process of formal religious training, not yet fully ordained or consecrated, but deeply committed to the path that will lead her toward full participation in Ragunna's religious hierarchy. Her faith is not abstract or merely intellectual; it is lived, practiced, and tested through the daily disciplines of prayer, service, and the cultivation of spiritual sensitivity.]

## Absolution and Confession

[In battle, Phoebe invokes two expressions of her power, Absolution and Confession, echoing the sacramental relationship between the soul and divine grace. In Absolution, she channels concentrated Spectro radiance into blinding strikes that break an enemy's defenses. It recalls transformative release, the lifting of a burden and the clarity granted when guilt is removed. In Confession, her light works patiently, clinging to enemies as searing brilliance that erodes their strength. It reflects the slower work of acknowledging fault, wearing away spiritual corruption, and keeping sin and entropy at bay.]

## Prayer, Divine Voice, and Spectro Frazzle

[The twin energies that Phoebe draws upon in battle, which she calls Prayer and Divine Voice, represent the two-directional flow of communication between the human supplicant and the divine. Prayer is the offering sent upward; Divine Voice is the response received. Her ability to balance these energies, shifting between them as the moment demands to unleash either sudden radiance or patient, sustained light, reflects the theological understanding that the relationship between human and divine is not static but dynamic, a constant exchange of offering and response that requires discernment, timing, and the wisdom to know when to speak and when to listen. She is known to carry the rectifier called Luminous Hymn and to attune herself to Echoes of radiant character, both of which amplify her Spectro capabilities and reinforce her thematic connection to light, truth, and the transformative power of genuine spiritual engagement.]
