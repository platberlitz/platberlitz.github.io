# Jinzhou

## Overview

[Jinzhou is a frontier city within the nation of Huanglong, serving as one of the most important settlements in the post-Lament world of Solaris-3. Positioned at the edge of civilized territory, Jinzhou represents both the resilience of human civilization and the constant struggle against the encroaching dangers of the wilderness. The city is governed by its Magistrate and protected by the Sentinel Jué, whose guardian presence allows its inhabitants to live, work, and sustain their communities despite the ever-present threat of Tacet Discords.]

## Leadership and Governance

[Jinzhou is led by Jinhsi, who serves simultaneously as the city's Magistrate and as the Sentinel's Appointed Resonator. Jinhsi is described as one who gently brightens the hopes of her people, like rays of winter sunlight, displaying humility and wholehearted commitment to guiding her people toward a brilliant future. This dual role as magistrate and Sentinel's Appointed gives Jinhsi authority over both the temporal and spiritual affairs of the city, making her one of the most powerful and respected figures in the region. Her leadership style emphasizes compassion, accessibility, and a genuine connection to the people she serves, qualities that have earned her the deep trust and admiration of Jinzhou's residents.]

## Strategic Importance

[As a frontier city, Jinzhou holds immense strategic importance within Huanglong and across Solaris-3. It is a bulwark against Tacet Discords in the wilderness, a departure point for Resonators defending the surrounding lands, and a hub of trade and communication between Huanglong's heartlands and its frontier. At the boundary between safety and danger, Jinzhou draws travelers, defenders, and merchants. Its walls and defensive works stand as a shield for the nation.]

## Notable Figures

[Jinzhou is home to or closely associated with many important figures. Beyond Magistrate Jinhsi, the city is connected to Sanhua, a Glacio sword wielder who forms ice constructs; Baizhi, a healer accompanied by a pale creature; Lingyang, a Glacio gauntlet fighter; and many other Resonators serving the frontier. The Rover's search for lost memories also brought them to Jinzhou and into contact with its leaders and protectors. The presence of Sentinel Jué further elevates the city's importance.]

## Daily Life and Culture

[Life in Jinzhou reflects the dual nature of frontier existence, the normal rhythms of trade, craftsmanship, and community coexist with the constant awareness of danger and the need for vigilance. The city's markets are active with commerce conducted in Shell Credits, trading in rare materials, tonics, and other goods essential to Resonators and ordinary citizens alike. Cultural practices centered around the reverence of Sentinel Jué are woven into daily life, with ceremonies and traditions that honor the Sentinel's protection. The people of Jinzhou are characterized by their hardiness, their communal bonds, and their determination to build a future in a world that remains dangerous and unpredictable.]
