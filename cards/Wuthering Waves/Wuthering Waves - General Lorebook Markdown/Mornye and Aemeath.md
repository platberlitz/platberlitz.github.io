Existing comment: Mornye and Aemeath

[What is known of Mornye and Aemeath comes entirely from Aemeath's side. She says the first time she met Professor Mornye was when Rover took her to the Academy, right before Rover left Lahai-Roi. Mornye was Rover's junior in those days and used a wheelchair. Aemeath watched her glance at Rover over and over, and hide behind the papers in her hands each time she tried to say something, and Aemeath's teasing verdict is that this is what comes of Rover being gentle with everyone. The meeting has a threshold quality. It is not a casual first day introduction or a routine faculty appointment. It occurs at a moment when Aemeath is being brought toward Startorch Academy, while Rover's impending departure makes every connection feel like something that may have to endure in Rover's absence.]

[The shy junior Aemeath met became a Fusion Resonator, a Spacetrek Collective researcher, and a professor of Exostrider Engineering, the very field tied to the protector that changed Aemeath's existence. Aemeath is the Exostrider Synchronist of Rabelle College, and when she resonated with Exostrider from the simulation cockpit her body was torn apart. She should have died there. Instead she continues as a digital ghost almost no one can see, still able to manifest an Exostrider Armament and to synchronize with Exostrider, and able to enter data systems as well, though she cannot say whether that belongs to her Forte or to her condition. Mornye's work therefore touches the machinery, research, and ambition surrounding Aemeath's own catastrophe, while Mornye belongs naturally to rooms, departments, and lectures that Aemeath can only approach from her altered state.]

[Nothing beyond that first meeting passes between them in any account, and Mornye has never given her own version of it. What remains is Aemeath's fond image of a girl in a wheelchair peering at Rover and vanishing behind a stack of papers, and Aemeath's easy explanation that Rover simply makes people that way. The pairing of the two women is still a striking one. Mornye studies limits that ordinary people cannot cross and longs to see the splendid world beyond them; Aemeath exists on the far side of a crossing, no longer embodied yet still present as signal, memory, and will. Aemeath remembers Mornye as part of the Academy threshold where Rover, Exostrider, quiet brilliance, and the need for continued care converged.]
