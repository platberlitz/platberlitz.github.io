## The Wandering Bard

[Ciaccona is a wandering bard from Rinascita, a region characterized by its deep religious traditions, its celebration of artistic expression, and its complex relationship with the concept of Divinity. Unlike many practitioners of the bardic arts who confine their performances to temples, courts, or formal concert halls, Ciaccona has chosen a life of perpetual movement, traveling the roads between settlements and carrying her music to anyone willing to listen. She sings not only for the Divinity but also for the common folk: farmers, merchants, travelers, soldiers, and laborers who seldom have access to the refined cultural productions of the urban elite. This egalitarian approach to her art marks her as something rare in a world where artistic patronage is typically controlled by institutions of wealth and power.]

## Recording the World in Song

[Ciaccona's creative process is rooted in documentation and empathy. As she journeys, she records the stories of the people she encounters, their joys, sorrows, struggles, triumphs, and everyday moments of grace and absurdity. She then transforms these raw narratives into songs that evoke laughter, emotion, and tears in both the original storytellers and new audiences. This ability to take one person's experience and make it universally resonant is the hallmark of a truly gifted bard. Her songs become vessels of shared humanity, carrying the weight of individual lives across geographical and social boundaries. In this way, Ciaccona functions not merely as an entertainer but as a living archive of human experience, a keeper of stories that might otherwise be lost to time and indifference.]

## Music as Weapon and Support

[In battle, Ciaccona is a rare Resonator who wields Aero pistols in concert with her music. A persistent Aero erosion gnaws at her foes' defenses while her melodies steady and strengthen her companions. When she gives full voice to her Resonance, harmonies remain over the field and continue to weaken her enemies after she has left the foremost line. She can conjure phantasmal doubles whose accompaniment sharpens the aim and force of every arrow and bullet loosed by her allies. Like her songs, her art joins separate lives into a single purpose.]
