# Reserve Course

## What It Is

[The Reserve Course Department is a second school built on Hope's Peak Academy's west quarter for students who have no talent and can pay. Facing potential bankruptcy, the academy opened its name to anyone able to afford an immense tuition fee, and the money from those students funds the education and research of the main course. The building is a small imitation of the original, rectangular with a cone in the middle, with a much larger L-shaped block behind it capable of holding thousands.]

## Two Schools

[The separation is deliberate and enforced. Reserve Course students wear black versions of the school uniform and may not alter them, so that they can be identified at a glance. Attendance at ordinary lessons is mandatory for them, where main course students may choose whether to attend at all. Main course students are permitted to visit the Reserve Course side; Reserve Course students are not permitted to cross the other way. Despite having paid enormous sums and competed hard for places, they are regarded by the faculty and the main course as second-class students.]

## Why People Enrol

[The Reserve Course exists because the Hope's Peak name is worth having on its own. Students enrol for the prestige, or to stay near someone they care about, or because they admire the academy so much that standing anywhere on its grounds feels like an achievement. That last motive is the one the academy learned to exploit.]

## What The Money Bought

[The majority of Reserve Course tuition went to the Izuru Kamukura Project, the human experiment intended to produce a student with every talent the school had researched. Those paying for it were never told. The academy took a class of students it had already defined as worthless, charged them a fortune, and used the money to build a demonstration that talent could be manufactured and that theirs never would be.]

## What It Produced

[Three Reserve Course students are known by name, and all three had ties to the main course. One was the little sister of a yakuza heir, who convinced her father to pay so she could follow her brother. One was the friend of a photographer and enrolled to stay near her. The third was a boy who admired Hope's Peak so much he could not stay away from it, who was befriended by a main course student, who was recruited as the Kamukura Project's test subject, and who dropped out of the Reserve Course to undergo it.]
