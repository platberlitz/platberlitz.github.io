# Izuru Kamukura Project

## Purpose

[The Izuru Kamukura Project, also called the Hope Cultivation Plan, was an experiment directed by Hope's Peak Academy's Steering Committee to manufacture hope rather than find it. Its goal was to take an ordinary student with no talent and surgically implant every talent the academy had researched, producing a perfect genius whose specialty was talent itself, and to grant that person the title of Ultimate Hope. The project was named after the academy's founder, and its subject took the founder's name.]

## Method And Secrecy

[The work required human experimentation and other things the school could not defend, so the committee kept it hidden even from the headmaster. It was funded largely by Reserve Course tuition, and the laboratory was in the basement of the old school building, where the subject lived for a period afterward. The procedure involved extensive brain surgery, and it left a scar on the subject's head.]

## Recruitment

[The intended subject was a Reserve Course student, chosen because he had no talent and because he wanted one more than anything. He was approached with the offer while he was already spending his enrollment staring through a gate he was not permitted to walk through, and he hesitated for a long time before agreeing. Consenting meant dropping out of the Reserve Course and giving up everything he had been.]

## Result

[On paper the project succeeded completely. An unremarkable boy emerged carrying every talent the academy had catalogued and outperformed everyone at everything. The process also erased almost all of his personality and his memories, on the reasoning that his original self would interfere with talent cultivation, and what it left was apathetic to everything and everyone, capable of predicting any outcome and consequently incapable of being interested in any of them.]

## Consequence

[The Ultimate Hope proved to be the academy's undoing. Two students learned of the project, tracked the subject down, and offered him the one thing that had been taken from him, which was the possibility of not knowing what happens next. He agreed to be used in exchange for it. The academy's proof that hope could be manufactured became the instrument of the Tragedy, and the school that built him was among the first things destroyed.]
