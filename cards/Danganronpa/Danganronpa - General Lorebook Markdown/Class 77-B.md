# Class 77-B

## Who They Are

[Class 77-B is the Hope's Peak Academy class one year ahead of Class 78th, and the class that became the core of Ultimate Despair. Its sixteen members are the participants of the Killing School Trip, though the people walking around Jabberwock Island are avatars built from memories of who they were before they ever enrolled.]

## Composition

[The class held the Ultimate Lucky Student of its year, the Ultimate Gamer, the Ultimate Musician, the Ultimate Traditional Dancer, the Ultimate Impostor, the Ultimate Yakuza, the Ultimate Swordswoman, the Ultimate Gymnast, the Ultimate Team Manager, the Ultimate Breeder, the Ultimate Mechanic, the Ultimate Photographer, the Ultimate Nurse, the Ultimate Princess and the Ultimate Cook, alongside a sixteenth participant who had never been a main course student at all.]

## What Happened To Them

[The class was conditioned into Ultimate Despair by Junko Enoshima, using a brainwashing technique combined with footage engineered to break them. The conditioning left their personalities intact and rewired what they wanted, so each of them pursued despair through their own talent while remaining recognisably themselves, and their announced plans for the world sounded like philanthropy. They were among the most destructive forces of the Tragedy and continued after Junko's death as Remnants of Despair.]

## Recovery Attempt

[Fifteen of them were taken into Future Foundation custody and moved secretly to Jabberwock Island, where they were entered into the Neo World Program with their school memories removed, in the hope that the selves they built inside the simulation would overwrite the conditioning on graduation. The program was sabotaged and became the Killing School Trip. Six survived it and chose to wake up as themselves, and they remained on the island afterward to try to recover the classmates who never woke.]

## Before

[As a class they were unusually close, closer than Class 78th ever managed, and their year group was known for it. They attended lessons together when Ultimates were not required to attend anything, held gatherings, and organised around a class representative who kept them together. Whatever was done to them afterward was done to a group that already functioned, which is a large part of why the conditioning held so well and why the simulation designed to undo it worked by making them friends again.]
