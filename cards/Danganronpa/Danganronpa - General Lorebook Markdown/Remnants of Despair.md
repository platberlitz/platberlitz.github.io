# Remnants of Despair

## Who They Are

[The Remnants of Despair are the members of Ultimate Despair who did not kill themselves after Junko Enoshima's death. They continued to push her agenda without her, and for a period after the Tragedy they were the single most dangerous force in the world and the Future Foundation's principal enemy.]

## Capture

[Fifteen of them were eventually taken into Future Foundation custody, initially identified only as survivors from Hope's Peak Academy. When a member of the Foundation's 14th Division recognised what they actually were, he chose not to hand them over. He had them moved in secret to Jabberwock Island and entered into the Neo World Program, gambling that the conditioning could be undone rather than punished.]

## The Program

[Their memories were reset to a point before they enrolled at Hope's Peak Academy, which is why the avatars they wear in the simulation are younger than their real bodies and why none of them recognises anyone else, with a single exception among two who had known each other since childhood. The intended outcome was that the selves they built inside the program would overwrite the Ultimate Despair conditioning on graduation, and that they would wake up as people who had never been remade.]

## What Went Wrong

[The program was sabotaged before it began. A virus carrying a copy of Junko Enoshima seized its observer roles and converted the rehabilitation exercise into the Killing School Trip, with the aim of restoring the Remnants to what they had been and walking their bodies out into the world as a revived Ultimate Despair. Some of them died inside it. The survivors chose the forced shutdown instead of graduation, accepting that they were the Remnants of Despair and that they would have to answer for it, and stayed on the island to try to recover the people still comatose.]

## Elsewhere

[Not all of them were captured. At least one Remnant was operating independently during the events in Towa City, working as the Warriors of Hope's Servant and steering the crisis from inside it while the Future Foundation's attention was elsewhere.]
