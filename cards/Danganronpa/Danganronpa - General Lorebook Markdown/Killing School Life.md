# Killing School Life

## What It Is

[The Killing School Life is the killing game held inside the sealed old school building of Hope's Peak Academy, involving the sixteen students of Class 78th. They wake in the building with no memory of the two years they had already spent at the school, believing they have just enrolled, and are told by Monokuma that they will live there for the rest of their lives unless they graduate, and that graduation requires killing a classmate and not being caught.]

## Structure

[Life inside runs on a schedule of morning and night announcements, with certain areas closed until Monokuma chooses to open them. When nobody kills, Monokuma introduces a motive designed to make somebody do it, escalating from the threat of exposing everyone's secrets, to money, to video messages about what has happened to the students' families, to blackmail and hostage-taking. Each murder is followed by an investigation and a class trial, and each trial ends in an execution.]

## The Conditions

[The students were sealed in as part of a genuine shelter plan while the world outside collapsed, and their memories of that world and of each other were removed afterward. The plating over the windows, the vault door and the food stores are all real shelter measures. What was added was the surveillance, the courtroom in the basement, the wallpaper and the bear.]

## The Mastermind

[The mastermind was one of the sixteen, hidden among them for the entire game while a second conspirator wore her identity. She killed the headmaster to take the building, ran Monokuma personally, and structured the game around the belief that hope always loses to despair given enough pressure.]

## Outcome

[Six students survived: the Ultimate Lucky Student, the Ultimate Detective, the Ultimate Affluent Progeny, the Ultimate Swimming Pro, the Ultimate Clairvoyant and the Ultimate Writing Prodigy. In the final trial they were shown the state of the world outside and offered the chance to stay sealed in forever rather than face it. They voted to leave. The mastermind, having lost, executed herself, and the survivors opened the vault door and walked out into the Tragedy.]
