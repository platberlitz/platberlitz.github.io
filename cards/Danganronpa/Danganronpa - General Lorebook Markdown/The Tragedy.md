# The Tragedy

## What It Is

[The Tragedy, in full the Biggest, Most Awful, Most Tragic Event in Human History, is the global collapse caused by Junko Enoshima and Ultimate Despair. It began roughly a year before the Killing School Life and is described by everyone who lived through it as extraordinary social unrest that escalated into worldwide anarchy, meaningless violence and mass death.]

## How It Started

[The first killing game was held among Hope's Peak Academy's own student council, and every participant but two died. The footage was edited and released to the Reserve Course together with the fact that their tuition had secretly funded the creation of the Ultimate Hope, which cast the academy as having spent their money manufacturing killers. The Reserve Course rioted, was steered from the shadows into escalating violence, and was finally brainwashed and destroyed by the woman who had provoked it, in front of witnesses who could not stop it.]

## Spread

[The anti-talent movement it produced was pushed outward until it engulfed the world. It began on the internet as a community with real grievances, spread offline, crossed borders and nationalities, and then lost its own reasons. The demonstrations became destruction, the destruction became routine, and death became an ordinary part of daily life. The strong killed the weak and the weak killed each other. What had started as a scandal about a school ended as the near-destruction of civilisation.]

## Inside The Academy

[Hope's Peak Academy responded by ceasing operations as a school and converting the old building into a sealed shelter for its remaining students. The plan was hijacked from inside by one of those students, who killed the headmaster, erased the memories of the rest, and turned the shelter into the Killing School Life.]

## Aftermath

[The Tragedy did not end with Junko Enoshima's death. Most members of Ultimate Despair killed themselves after she did, and those who did not became the Remnants of Despair and kept the work going. The Future Foundation, formed by alumni of Hope's Peak, began the long process of pulling the world back, and with Ultimate Despair's influence weakened it started to show real progress. Large parts of the world remained in anarchy regardless, and in Towa City a deliberate attempt was made to escalate a local riot into a war capable of reigniting the whole thing.]
