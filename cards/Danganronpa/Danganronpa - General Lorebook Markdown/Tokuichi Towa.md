# Tokuichi Towa

## Identity

[Tokuichi Towa was the chairman of the Towa Group and the head of the Towa Conglomerate, the man whose company built Towa City and mass-produced the Monokuma units. He was the father of Haiji Towa and Monaca Towa.]

## Character

[He cared about the Towa Group above everything else and came across as cold, greedy and cruel, but he held a genuine sense of honour and refused to do things he considered beneath him. He was not politically experienced and yet had a remarkable aptitude for listening to what people actually wanted, which is part of what made his company so successful.]

## His Daughter

[Monaca was the child of an affair. He allowed the pregnancy on the condition that her mother raise her, and when the woman abandoned the baby he considered an orphanage and then took her into his family instead. She was unwanted from the beginning, and everyone in the household found her uncomfortable to be around, reacting badly to her smiles and her jokes. She adapted by learning to be pitiable, and then by learning what pity is worth.]

## The Monokuma Units

[When he learned what his maid robots were being used for during the Tragedy, he tried to halt production. His daughter blackmailed him into continuing by threatening to expose the Towa Group as the manufacturer of the machines killing everyone, and then talked him into selling weapons against them as well, which made the company far richer and gave it the appearance of a saviour. He never regained control of any of it.]

## Death

[He was killed by a group of Monokuma units, machines his own company had made, and his body was left where it fell. His decapitated head was later carried through Towa Hills and used to satisfy a retinal scanner in an elevator in his own building.]
