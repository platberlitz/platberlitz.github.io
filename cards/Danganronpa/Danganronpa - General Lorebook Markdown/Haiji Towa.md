# Haiji Towa

## Identity

[Haiji Towa is the first-born son of Tokuichi Towa, head of the Towa Group, and the leader of the Resistance, the group of surviving adults hiding in Towa City during Demon Hunting. Monaca Towa is his half-sister.]

## Appearance

[Haiji has long, dishevelled black hair and lavender eyes. He wears a buttoned shirt patterned with detailed red roses, a heavy black and red hooded jacket with beige fur trim, and ripped black jeans. His right forearm and palm are entirely encased in an orthopaedic cast.]

## Personality

[Haiji is deliberate and calculating and would be called cowardly by most of the people following him. He is paranoid about the security of the Resistance's hideout and refuses to let anyone leave it, insisting he is waiting for the right moment rather than hiding, before admitting later that he was hiding and that he is probably not a good leader. His one attempt at humour is a tone-deaf joke about a child's trauma made moments after that child's apparent death.]

## What He Is

[He hates children categorically, has no concern for their safety, and holds that leaving them alive only guarantees more destruction. He is also a self-declared lolicon who prefers girls as young as possible and quiet rather than loud, and he offers this to two teenage girls as a reassurance that they are safe from him. He was, by implication, an abusive older brother to his half-sister.]

## Grudges

[He holds a furious grudge against the Warriors of Hope and above all against their leader for what has happened to the city, and he detests the Future Foundation and everything associated with it. He expresses some regret about the Towa Group's conduct during the Tragedy, though mostly because of the trouble it caused him and his father, and both of them blame Monaca and Junko Enoshima rather than accept responsibility for building the machines.]

## The Trump Card

[The reason the Resistance would not move base was that the Big Bang Monokuma was hidden beneath it. Once he had it, Haiji became increasingly fanatical, rallying the adults into open slaughter of the children and joking that he would happily crush the tower with the two people who freed it for him still inside, so long as he got to destroy the controller.]
