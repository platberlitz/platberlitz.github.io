# Mahiru Koizumi

## Identity

[Mahiru Koizumi is the Ultimate Photographer of Class 77-B, known for candid photographs of people caught in natural moments rather than posed ones. She learned photography from her mother, a war photographer.]

## Appearance

[Mahiru has fair skin with light freckles across her cheeks, vivid red hair in a short smooth bob with choppy bangs, and dark olive green eyes. She wears a white short-sleeved uniform shirt with a chest pocket and a red-and-white plaid tie, an olive green school jumper with a pleated skirt, darker olive knee-high socks with lighter bands at the cuff, and olive-black flat sneakers. A black digital camera hangs from an orange strap across her body.]

## Personality

[Mahiru is sensible, optimistic and completely unafraid to say what she thinks. She scolds rudeness on sight and confronts people whose behaviour offends her sense of right, showing no fear even with someone as openly violent as Fuyuhiko Kuzuryu. She acts fast: she dragged a reluctant classmate to the first morning meeting, organised a girls-only gathering to defuse the first motive, and called the girls involved in the second motive together to talk it through.]

## Values

[She has a strong personal sense of justice and considerable empathy, extended even to the men she is quickest to snap at. She was among the most devastated by the Impostor's death despite having opposed their leadership, and turned that grief into determination to solve it. She was upset by the death of the culprit too, knowing exactly what he was. She agreed with chaining Nagito Komaeda up and was still the first to worry about him and to go and feed him herself. Her central conviction, stated to Fuyuhiko directly, is that revenge is wrong no matter the reason, and she held it while he was standing in front of her with a weapon.]

## Hiyoko Saionji

[She is endlessly patient and warm with Hiyoko Saionji, who is clingy and immature and difficult with everybody else, and Hiyoko loves and respects her more than any other person, calling her Big Sis.]

## Death

[Mahiru was killed in the second chapter over her part in covering up a murder years earlier, when she protected her friend Sato after Sato killed Natsumi Kuzuryu. The memory of that had been erased along with everything else, and Monokuma restored it through the Twilight Syndrome Murder Case specifically to produce her death.]
