# Towa Group

## What It Is

[The Towa Group is a leading technology enterprise owned by the Towa Conglomerate, which built and controls Towa City. Its headquarters were the first structure raised on the artificial island, and the city grew around them. Its isolation allowed it to develop advanced technology on its own terms and made it one of the most powerful commercial entities in the world.]

## The Monokuma Units

[The company's robotics division mass-produced the Monokuma units, presented publicly as maid robots. They were used as killing machines during the Tragedy. When the head of the company learned what his products were being used for and tried to stop production, his own daughter blackmailed him into allowing it to continue, warning that she would have it made public that the murderous bears were built by the Towa Group.]

## Profiting From Both Sides

[The same daughter then suggested that the company also manufacture and sell weapons designed to destroy Monokuma units. The Towa Group made a fortune selling both the threat and the defence against it, and emerged from the arrangement looking like heroes. The Big Bang Monokuma, an enormous unit developed by the robotics division under her supervision, was justified as an emergency defence for the city, and its resemblance to the standard units was her decision.]

## Collapse

[The company's leadership did not survive what it had built. Its head was killed by a group of the machines his own factories produced, and his decapitated head was later used to satisfy a retinal scanner in his own building. His son led the surviving adults in a war against the children, and his daughter led the children. Neither of them accepted responsibility for the machines; both blamed her and blamed Junko Enoshima, while a factory beneath the Resistance's own hideout continued turning out more.]
