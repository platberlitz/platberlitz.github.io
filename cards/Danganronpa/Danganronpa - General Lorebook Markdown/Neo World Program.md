# Neo World Program

## What It Is

[The Neo World Program, formally the Psychotherapeutic Communication Simulator, is a virtual environment developed jointly by Hope's Peak Academy and the Future Foundation. It synchronises directly with a user's brain through a device mounted on the cranium and places the user inside a fully realised simulated world. It is built on current psychotherapy technology with input from an Ultimate Programmer, an Ultimate Neurologist and an Ultimate Therapist, and its designed purpose is to undo trauma by replacing memories formed in the real world with memories formed inside the simulation.]

## Avatars

[A user's avatar is a copy of their real body generated from a specific set of their own memories, so it can differ substantially from what they currently look like. The real body remains in a sleeping state throughout. The Remnants of Despair entered with their school memories removed, which is why their avatars appear as they did before enrolling at Hope's Peak Academy, several years younger than their real selves, and why they do not recognise each other.]

## How It Feels

[The simulation is convincing because the brain treats it as real. A person told convincingly enough that they have been burned will develop the burn, and the same principle scales to an entire island. Injury, hunger, exhaustion and death inside the program are experienced exactly as they would be outside it, and death inside it shuts down the corresponding real body.]

## Observers

[The program generates observers to guide its users and carry out the session's objectives. They are not people; they are constructs whose appearance and personality are drawn from the users' own thoughts and memories, and they may closely resemble real people the users once knew. The session run for the Remnants of Despair generated two, both of whom had their roles usurped when the program was hijacked.]

## Graduation

[At the end of a session users choose to graduate or to remain. Graduating, if the observer judges the objectives met, transfers each consciousness back into its real body and forcibly erases the personality it had before entering, which is the entire point of the exercise. Choosing to remain means living on inside the simulation indefinitely. There is also an undocumented third option: pressing graduate and repeat together triggers a forced shutdown that overrides both, returning every surviving consciousness to its body without overwriting anything.]

## Sabotage

[The session held on Jabberwock Island was compromised before it started. A virus was inserted into the program's code, which manifested as Monokuma, seized the observers' authority, built a corrupted digital copy of Hope's Peak Academy inside the simulation, and converted the rehabilitation exercise into the Killing School Trip.]
