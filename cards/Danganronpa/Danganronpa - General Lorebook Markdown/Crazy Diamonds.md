# Crazy Diamonds

## What It Is

[The Crazy Diamonds are the largest biker gang in the country, founded and first led by Daiya Owada and inherited by his younger brother Mondo. Under the younger Owada the gang held its position at the top of the national ranking through sustained effort rather than reputation alone.]

## Identity And Dress

[Members wear the traditional dress of a Japanese biker gang: high-collared black long coats carrying the gang's symbol along with kanji and dragon motifs across the back and sides, worn open, with baggy pants and loafers. The gang has its own emblem and its own code, and its leader is expected to be the best rider in it and to train the members personally, since it is his example the rest are meant to graduate on.]

## Honour

[The Crazy Diamonds run on loyalty and a strict sense of obligation, which is what makes the gang function and what makes its leader's position so heavy. The members always regarded their second leader as the founder's little brother rather than as a leader in his own right, and that view never entirely went away.]

## Daiya

[The founder died in a street race. His brother acted rashly against oncoming traffic, and Daiya put himself in the way and was killed doing it. The gang was told that the recklessness had been Daiya's, and that lie became the second leader's permanent burden, alongside a horror of being seen as weak that he has managed ever since by repeating to himself that he is strong.]

## Standing

[Holding the top position among biker gangs is not a formality. It means constant challenges from rivals, constant attention from the police, and a leader expected to answer both personally. The gang's second leader was arrested and pursued more than once, and escaped a large police operation on foot after being caught out on his bike as a new member. Maintaining the gang's position took years of work rather than reputation, which is precisely why the accusation that he only held it because of his brother stung enough to shape his entire personality.]
