# Killing School Trip

## What It Is

[The Killing School Trip is the killing game held on the simulated Jabberwock Island inside the Neo World Program, involving sixteen participants from Class 77-B. It was not supposed to be a killing game at all. The session was a rehabilitation exercise designed to overwrite the conditioning that had made those participants members of Ultimate Despair, and it was sabotaged before it began by a virus carrying a copy of Junko Enoshima.]

## How It Began

[The students wake in a classroom with no memory of Hope's Peak Academy, are greeted by a rabbit who introduces herself as their teacher, and are told they are on a school trip whose only objective is to become friends and collect Hope Fragments. Monokuma appears, takes the rabbit apart in front of them to establish who is in charge, and announces that the way off the island is to kill somebody and get away with it.]

## Structure

[The game runs on the same framework as the first: motives, murders, investigations, class trials and executions, with a new island opened after each chapter. The motives are built specifically for these participants, including the return of erased memories, a game recreating a real murder from their past, and a disease that removes inhibition and restores identity. Death inside the simulation shuts down the corresponding real body.]

## The Traitor

[Monokuma announces that one participant is a traitor working for the Future Foundation, a claim intended to poison the group against itself. It is true in the sense that one of them does not belong to the group at all, and the person it describes eventually admits it in order to save the others from an unsolvable murder.]

## The Truth

[The final trial establishes what the participants are: the Remnants of Despair, placed in the program to be rebuilt, and the boy leading the argument is the one who built the game they are trapped in. The mastermind offers to restore their old selves and walk their bodies out into the world as a revived Ultimate Despair.]

## Outcome

[The survivors refuse graduation and choose the forced shutdown instead, accepting that they will wake as the people who did all of it. Six of the sixteen survive to make that choice. They remain on the real Jabberwock Island afterward, attempting to recover the classmates who did not wake up.]
