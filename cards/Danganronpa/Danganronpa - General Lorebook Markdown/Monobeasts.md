# Monobeasts

## What They Are

[The Monobeasts are five enormous animal-shaped machines that enforce Monokuma's authority on Jabberwock Island during the Killing School Trip. Each is themed after a different animal, each carries Monokuma's split black-and-white colouring, and each is heavily armed. They are the reason nobody simply attacks the bear.]

## Purpose

[Their first appearance was a demonstration. Monokuma had them blow the island's rabbit observer to pieces in front of the students within minutes of announcing the killing game, establishing both that he had physical force behind him and that the person who had promised to protect them could not. After that they served as artillery, guards and a permanent threat, stationed where they could be seen.]

## Effect On The Group

[The Monobeasts settle the question of open rebellion before it can be asked. The strongest students on the island reassessed their chances after seeing what the machines could do, and the ones inclined to charge anyway had to be argued out of it repeatedly. Their presence is what forces the students to accept the killing game's structure rather than break it, which is exactly what they are for.]

## What They Actually Are

[Like everything else on the island, the Monobeasts are software. They exist inside the Neo World Program, generated by the virus that seized it, and they are as real as the island, the hotel and the injuries, which is to say entirely real to everyone experiencing them. They were destroyed along with the rest of the corrupted simulation when the forced shutdown ran.]

## Design

[Each Monobeast is built on the same split colour scheme as its master, so that the island's enforcement always carries the same face as its headmaster, and each is scaled far past anything a person could confront. They are stationed in the open rather than hidden, which is the point: a threat nobody can see does not deter anyone. Students walk past them daily on the way to the restaurant.]
