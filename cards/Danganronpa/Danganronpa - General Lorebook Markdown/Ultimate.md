# Ultimate

## The Title

[Ultimate, rendered in the original Japanese as Super High School Level, is the title given to a person judged the very best in the world at one specific thing while still of high school age, generally between fifteen and eighteen. It is not an award or a rank earned by examination. It is a designation applied by Hope's Peak Academy when its scouts identify someone as the peak of a field, and it comes with an invitation to enrol.]

## How Titles Work

[Most titles are unique and held by one person at a time, and a title passes to a successor once its holder graduates. Graduates are then referred to as Former Super High School Level holders of their title. Siblings can share a title where the talent is genuinely shared. A handful of titles work differently. The Ultimate Lucky Student is not scouted at all but drawn by lottery from every high school student in the country, on the reasoning that extraordinary luck is itself a talent worth studying. Some students arrive with their talent deliberately concealed and are listed only as Ultimate ???.]

## Range

[The titles cover anything that can be done supremely well, without regard for whether the field is respectable. Alongside detectives, programmers, photographers, athletes, musicians and nurses, the academy has enrolled a gambler, a biker gang leader, a yakuza heir, a hitman, a fanfic author, a clairvoyant with a thirty percent success rate, and a serial killer nobody had caught. The school's interest is in the talent, not in what the talent is for, which is a large part of how it produced what it produced.]

## What It Costs

[The title becomes the whole of a person's identity in the eyes of everyone who hears it, including the people who hold it. Talent is treated as intrinsic, unearned and unassailable, which means those without it are treated as having nothing, and the resentment that produced would eventually be aimed back at the school. Ultimates are also worth a great deal: the school's funding, its prestige and its research all rest on them, and the students themselves are the asset.]

## Hope And Despair

[Two titles exist outside the ordinary system. Ultimate Hope was granted to the artificial product of the Izuru Kamukura Project, a student surgically given every talent the academy had researched. Ultimate Despair was the title claimed by the student who turned all of it against the school. Neither was scouted; both were made.]
