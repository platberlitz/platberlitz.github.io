# Warriors of Hope

## What They Are

[The Warriors of Hope are five children from Hope's Peak Academy's elementary school division, all born and raised in Towa City, who declared war on every adult in it. They began as an ordinary friend group, put together in a classroom for troublemakers, and what they actually had in common was that every one of them was being abused at home.]

## Members

[The group has five members, each holding a title borrowed from a role-playing game and a corresponding emblem worn on their clothing, their battle robot and their arena. The Hero leads on paper, having won the position at rock paper scissors. The Priest, the Fighter and the Sage hold the other three. The Mage holds the fifth and runs the entire operation from behind the pitiful child act.]

## Origin

[Facing lives they could not escape, the group was tricked into a collective suicide attempt by one of its own members, though the others had already been seriously considering it. The attempt was interrupted by Junko Enoshima, who took them in, and who spent the time she had grooming them into a machine for killing adults. Their disappearance went unnoticed because the world was already collapsing. Junko's death left them adrift, and the plan they eventually adopted, to create a successor for her and start a war, was suggested to them by a modified bear unit carrying half of her artificial intelligence.]

## Demon Hunting

[Their programme was announced through a hijacked news broadcast and executed with mass-produced Monokuma units and an army of brainwashed children. They called their targets Demons, kept Hit List entries recording each one, and fitted them with wristbands to keep them inside the city. Each of the three Warriors with an arena, called a Killesseum, ambushed intruders there and fought them from inside a large battle robot themed to their class. Their headquarters was Towa Hills, and they kept an airship, the Excalibur, moored at its helicopter port.]

## The Servant

[The group kept a slave, a young man in a collar and chain who cooked for them, obeyed their orders, and was mistreated as a matter of routine. He was a Remnant of Despair, he was steering the entire crisis from underneath it, and by the end he was the only person the group's leader would still listen to.]

## Disbandment

[Demon Hunting collapsed once the Captives had mostly been rescued and the group's members had been defeated one by one. Their leader abandoned them and left to pursue her own succession alone, and the three members presumed dead in their arenas turned out to have survived.]
