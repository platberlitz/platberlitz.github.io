# Mikan Tsumiki

## Identity

[Mikan Tsumiki is the Ultimate Nurse of Class 77-B, a nurse of real skill whose medical knowledge repeatedly proves decisive in the killing game, since she performs the autopsies that establish injuries, cause of death and time of death.]

## Appearance

[Mikan is of average height with a skinny frame, fair skin, and long choppy dark plum-purple hair cut unevenly as a result of bullying, its longest point past her fingertips and its shortest above her neck. Her pale grey-mauve eyes almost always look anxious or tearful, and there is a beauty mark under the corner of her left eye. She wears a nurse's uniform: a pale pink short-sleeved shirt with a white collar and puffed sleeves, a short wrinkled pale blue pleated skirt, and a white apron marked with two red lines, tied behind her neck.]

## Personality

[Mikan is extremely meek, paranoid and fragile, with hazardously low self-esteem produced by sustained abuse and bullying. She apologises reflexively, including for things that are not hers to apologise for, and she trips constantly into humiliating positions. She does not fight back when Hiyoko Saionji targets her, and still cares about Hiyoko regardless.]

## At Work

[Her talent changes her. Doing her job she is confident, knowledgeable and firm, raising her voice at people causing a commotion around a patient and giving unwanted health advice at unnecessary length. She says outright that she likes being on Jabberwock Island, because there her classmates acknowledge her, nobody is trying to hurt her, and her talent makes her useful. Being needed is the only form of value she recognises in herself.]

## The Third Chapter

[The Despair Disease strips away her inhibition and restores the memories the Neo World Program had removed, and what is underneath is not the timid girl at all. She murdered Ibuki Mioda and then Hiyoko Saionji, staged the scene, and lied in her own autopsy report to conceal the difference between hanging and strangling. When she was finally identified she declared her love for Junko Enoshima and said she hoped to meet her in death, an explanation none of her classmates could make any sense of.]
