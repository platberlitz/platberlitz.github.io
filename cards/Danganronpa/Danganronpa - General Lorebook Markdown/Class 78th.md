# Class 78th

## Who They Are

[Class 78th is the Hope's Peak Academy class that entered the Killing School Life, sixteen students scouted in the year the academy moved its main course into the new school building. They spent nearly two years at the school as an ordinary class, developing their talents and, by all evidence, becoming friends, before the world outside collapsed and those two years were removed from their memories.]

## Composition

[The class held the Ultimate Lucky Student, the Ultimate Detective, listed on enrolment only as Ultimate ???, the Ultimate Affluent Progeny, the Ultimate Writing Prodigy, the Ultimate Swimming Pro, the Ultimate Martial Artist, the Ultimate Clairvoyant, the Ultimate Fanfic Creator, the Ultimate Gambler, the Ultimate Pop Sensation, the Ultimate Baseball Star, the Ultimate Programmer, the Ultimate Biker Gang Leader, the Ultimate Moral Compass, the Ultimate Fashionista and the Ultimate Soldier. Two of those sixteen were the mastermind and her twin, and one of the students carried a second personality who was a serial killer.]

## What Happened To Them

[As the Tragedy engulfed the world, the class was sealed into the old school building as part of a genuine shelter plan. The plan was hijacked from inside, the headmaster was killed, and the students' memories of the school, of each other and of the outside world were erased. They woke believing they had just enrolled and were told they would live in the building forever unless they killed someone.]

## Outcome

[Six of the sixteen survived. Their families, friends and associates had already been taken as Captives and were used against them as motives, and most of those people were still being held in Towa City more than a year later. The survivors were extracted by the Future Foundation, had their school memories restored, and joined the organisation to fight the Remnants of Despair.]
