# Hifumi Yamada

## Identity

[Hifumi Yamada is the Ultimate Fanfic Creator of Class 78th, a doujin author whose work sells in quantities that professional publishers envy. He stands 170 cm.]

## Appearance

[Hifumi is overweight with large hands and thin legs, and a face frequently compared to a hamster's. He has short dark brown hair curling to points against his cheeks and forehead, a small pointed ahoge, and thick-rimmed black glasses. He wears a white button-down shirt, an orange tie patterned with a vertical double-headed blue arrow, a light grey unbuttoned cardigan, dark grey trousers, grey trainers and an orange backpack, with a white mountain symbol from his former school on the sleeve.]

## Personality

[Hifumi speaks at enormous length and constantly wanders into tangents about anime and fanfiction. He drops creepy or perverted remarks into ordinary conversation, particularly about women, and is oblivious to how he is perceived; when Toko Fukawa insulted him he could not work out whether it had been an insult. His reactions to shocking events run late, arriving calmly at first and then breaking into panic once he has processed them. He is genuinely intelligent in places, and solved part of the dying message in the first class trial.]

## Manner

[He is extremely passionate about his work and has no patience for anyone less dedicated, calling them slackers. His stated dream is to create a story powerful enough to inspire other people to write their own. He addresses everyone with a formal title and their full name, a habit which becomes a decisive piece of evidence at the trial for his own murder, since it establishes that a name he muttered while dying was a surname rather than a given name. He is addicted to diet cola.]

## Motive

[For all his perversion, Hifumi will not tolerate genuine indecency toward others, and it is that principle that gets him killed. Convinced by Celestia Ludenberg that Kiyotaka Ishimaru had assaulted her, he committed murder on her behalf, which shows both the sincerity of his intentions and how easily he is deceived. He had earlier stolen Alter Ego at Celestia's instruction, the act that set the whole plot in motion.]
