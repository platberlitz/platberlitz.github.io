# Hope Fragments

## What They Are

[Hope Fragments are small heart-shaped tokens handed out by the Neo World Program's rabbit observer when two participants grow closer. They are the entire objective of the school trip as originally designed: collect enough of them by making friends and the class graduates together. They are not a metaphor. They are the program's measure of the rehabilitation it was built to perform.]

## How They Are Earned

[A Fragment is awarded for spending genuine time with another participant and learning something real about them. The bond has to be actual rather than transactional, which is why the observer cannot simply hand them out and why the process cannot be rushed. Each participant can produce several with the same person as the relationship deepens.]

## Why They Matter

[The rehabilitation the session was built for depends on the participants forming attachments to each other that are stronger than the conditioning they arrived with. The Fragments are the record of that work, and gathering them is the difference between waking up as themselves and waking up as what Ultimate Despair made of them. The observer insists on this continuously, is laughed at for it continuously, and is right.]

## Under The Killing Game

[Once the school trip became the Killing School Trip, the Fragments were treated by almost everyone as a joke: a cute distraction from a game about murder, offered by a rabbit who kept getting torn apart on stage for interrupting. They kept being awarded anyway, throughout, to people investigating each other's murders. The one thing the mastermind could not do was stop the participants from becoming friends, and that is what decided the final vote.]

## The Shape Of The Design

[The rehabilitation was built on the assumption that conditioning can be overwritten by attachment rather than argued away, which is why the session's win condition is friendship and not insight. Nothing in the program asks the participants to understand what was done to them or to repent of anything they did. It asks only that they become people who would not do it again, and it measures that in Fragments.]
