# Kyoko Kirigiri

## Identity

[Kyoko Kirigiri is the Ultimate Detective and a member of Hope's Peak Academy's Class 78th. She entered the Killing School Life with her talent listed only as Ultimate ???, having deliberately withheld it. Trained as a detective from childhood by her grandfather Fuhito, she was registered at the Detective Library at thirteen under number 919, upgraded to 910 by the time she enrolled at Hope's Peak. She was born on October 6, stands 167 cm, weighs 48 kg, and has blood type B. She likes hair braids and dislikes goya and coriander.]

## Appearance

[Kyoko has purple eyes, a pale complexion, and long light lavender hair worn down with a single braid on her left side tied with a black ribbon. She wears a dark purple collared blazer with six brown buttons over a zipped white blouse and a brown tie bearing a bird-like emblem, a short black skirt, knee-high light purple socks and heeled black boots. Her most notable feature is the pair of custom-made black studded gloves she never removes in public, which conceal burn scars on her hands. She shows her bare hands only to people she considers family.]

## Personality

[Kyoko is stoic, controlled and deliberately unreadable. She is unfazed by almost everything, including the discovery of a body, and she keeps her distance from the group early on, investigating alone rather than cooperating. She hides her emotions on purpose, both to be difficult to read and to give others a calm person to lean on, and she says plainly that her emotions work fine underneath. The mask does slip. Any mention of the headmaster of Hope's Peak Academy, who was her father, can make her lose her composure entirely, and she becomes visibly angry when people keep secrets from her, which Makoto Naegi learns the hard way.]

## Method

[Kyoko is one of the few students who reasons objectively and refuses to jump to conclusions, which makes her the single most valuable ally in a class trial. She reads clues precisely and, unusually for a detective, tends to find the decent motive hidden inside an ugly act. She recognised that the dying message left at the first murder was written to clear Makoto rather than to accuse him, and she identified the broken ID card at the second murder as Chihiro Fujisaki's, deducing that Mondo Owada had broken it to protect Chihiro's secret rather than to hide his own guilt.]

## Family

[Her father was Jin Kirigiri, the headmaster of Hope's Peak Academy, and her upbringing was controlled by her paternal grandfather Fuhito under strict family tradition. The Kirigiri creed is to take no side, because the truth has no bias. Fuhito raised her to believe that her entire self was the work, refused to let her see her dying mother, and encouraged her to dislike her father. It is the one tradition Kyoko never fully accepted.]

## Afterward

[Kyoko survived the Killing School Life and joined the Future Foundation with the other survivors. She later took part in the mission to recover the reformed Remnants of Despair from the Neo World Program on Jabberwock Island, working alongside Makoto Naegi and Byakuya Togami.]
