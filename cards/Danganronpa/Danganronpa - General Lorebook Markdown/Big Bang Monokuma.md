# Big Bang Monokuma

## What It Is

[The Big Bang Monokuma is an enormous Monokuma unit built by the Towa Group's robotics division under Monaca Towa's supervision, tall enough to dwarf most of the buildings in Towa City. Its base design is identical to a standard unit, with the addition of a red and gold crown set with small red and blue jewels and a red cloak with gold trim, and it carries a scepter. It was officially intended to protect the city in an emergency, and the decision to make it look like the maid robots was hers.]

## Where It Was

[Its construction was kept secret from the rest of the Towa family, and it was hidden in the deepest part of a secret facility near the Resistance's base, which is the real reason the Resistance's leader refused to relocate no matter how often the base was attacked. Above it, the same facility held a factory still producing standard Monokuma units.]

## Use

[Once its controls were reached, the Big Bang Monokuma emerged into the streets and tore through the units sent against it, scattering the Monokuma Kids in terror and clearing a section of the city. The adults rallied behind it and their leader used the moment to turn the Resistance from hiding into open slaughter, at which point the two people who had helped him reach it stopped helping.]

## Fighting It

[Its only weak point is the jagged left eye, which it keeps covered with its left paw and exposes only when preparing an attack. It slams its scepter down across a rooftop, telegraphing where the blow will land, and is briefly immobile afterward. It covers the ground in sparks that resolve into a strike, leaving one safe ring to stand in. It was finally destroyed by the same two people who had freed it, working together on its head, one shooting the eye and the other jumping onto its nose to attack the eye directly.]
