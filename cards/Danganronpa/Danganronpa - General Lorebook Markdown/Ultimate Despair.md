# Ultimate Despair

## What It Is

[Ultimate Despair is the organisation founded by Junko Enoshima and the engine of the Tragedy. Its members are described as despair in human form. Junko treats despair as a contagion and a state of mind rather than a mood, and her members function less as followers than as extensions of her will. Their goal is to spread despair across the world and drive it into self-destructive anarchy, and they succeeded.]

## Membership

[The core of the organisation was a class of Hope's Peak Academy students, conditioned rather than recruited. Junko combined a brainwashing technique with footage engineered to break them, and the conditioning left their personalities intact while rewiring what they wanted, so that each of them continued to be recognisably themselves while pursuing despair with their own particular talent. Their announced plans for the world sounded humanitarian, and their eyes had gone wrong.]

## What They Did

[Their acts defy ordinary explanation because they were never meant to make sense. Members starved themselves until their bodies were husks. Members handed over their own parents for use in experiments on murder. Members slaughtered civilians and forced others to kill themselves as tribute to their dead leader. One removed an eye and replaced it with Junko's. The organisation's methods ranged from mass brainwashing videos distributed to whole populations down to personal manipulation of individuals Junko found interesting.]

## After Junko

[When Junko Enoshima executed herself at the end of the Killing School Life, most of Ultimate Despair followed her example and committed suicide. Those who did not became known as the Remnants of Despair. The organisation's influence collapsed sharply without her, and the Future Foundation gained ground for the first time, but the surviving members continued the work on their own account.]

## The Successor Question

[Ultimate Despair without Junko Enoshima is a body with no head, and the attempt to supply one drove the events in Towa City. The plan was to manufacture a successor out of an ordinary girl, hand her the organisation, and use the resulting war to restart the Tragedy from the beginning.]
