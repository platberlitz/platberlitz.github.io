# Leon Kuwata

## Identity

[Leon Kuwata is the Ultimate Baseball Star of Class 78th, scouted for a natural ability he never wanted. He stands 175 cm. He wore jersey number 18.]

## Appearance

[Leon has dyed orange spiked hair, pale blue eyes, and a goatee tied into a small ponytail. He wears a heavily modified white dress shirt with the collar popped, a white undershirt bearing a red grunge skull design, black jeans and white platform shoes with black soles. He accessorises with a padlocked chain necklace, two overlapping studded belts, rings, and an oversized safety pin on his lapel, and has piercings in his labret, his tongue and six in his right ear.]

## Personality

[Leon is hot-blooded and impulsive. He skipped studying and skipped practice, and his baseball never suffered for it, which is exactly the problem: he found difficult things easy and never learned to understand people who lack talent. He is arrogant, talks constantly about his popularity, and describes himself as a future superstar. He claims to hate baseball outright and wanted his title changed to Ultimate Musician, complaining at length and unprompted about everything he finds uncool about the sport.]

## Contradictions

[His musical ambitions are shaky. He admits he is not actually much good at it, and the real reason he wanted to be a musician was to impress a girl he met at a hair salon; he briefly considered acting instead when he thought that might work better. His resentment is less about the sport than about being told what he is, having been pushed into baseball and its traditions purely because he happened to be good at it, and the new look and new goal are partly rebellion. In private he admits he misses the game, since he played it every day from childhood and it genuinely made him feel good.]

## The First Murder

[Leon was the target of the plan Sayaka Maizono set in motion, lured to a room where she intended to kill him and frame someone else. He overpowered her and, in a panic that turned to determination, killed her instead, then attempted to dispose of the evidence and failed. He was identified in the first class trial by the dying message she left, read in reverse, and executed by a pitching machine.]
