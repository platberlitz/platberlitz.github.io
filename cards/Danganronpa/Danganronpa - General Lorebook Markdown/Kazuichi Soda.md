# Kazuichi Soda

## Identity

[Kazuichi Soda is the Ultimate Mechanic of Class 77-B, a mechanical genius able to build almost anything from almost nothing.]

## Appearance

[Kazuichi is slim with shaggy shoulder-length hair dyed hot pink, a small braid on the left side, and a large tuft hidden under a dark grey beanie with a white band. He wears hot pink prescription contact lenses at all times, has thin pronounced eyebrows and sharp shark-like teeth, and wears screw-shaped earrings, the right with a cross head and the left with a slot head. During the Killing School Trip he wears a yellow-green jumpsuit with dark brown side stripes over a white shirt, the right sleeve rolled to the elbow and the left to the forearm.]

## Personality

[Kazuichi presents as a confident, happy-go-lucky guy who gets carried away, and is described that way by Hajime Hinata. He is clingy and whiny, turns up late to meetings he arranged himself without apologising, and pressures people into joining his schemes. He does things for others out of genuine compassion mixed with shallow motives, building a monitor so everyone can play games together while making sure the bragging rights are his.]

## Cowardice

[Behind the flashy exterior he is a coward, and always has been, which made him a target for bullying growing up. Minor incidents send him into full panic, he blurts out worst-case scenarios that make his own anxiety worse, and his confidence collapses from maximum to nothing within seconds. He lies badly and contradicts himself doing it. He is brilliant with machinery and slow with almost everything else, though his habit of throwing out wrong theories in class trials still helps by eliminating dead ends. Hiyoko Saionji mocks him constantly for dressing loudly to hide the loser underneath, and he carries a wrench everywhere for emotional support.]

## Sonia Nevermind

[He is obsessed with Sonia Nevermind and pursues her relentlessly and unsuccessfully, to the visible irritation of most of the class. She turns him down consistently and, in the Funhouse, avoids him entirely, which Chiaki Nanami points out with cheerful precision. His resentment of Gundham Tanaka is entirely about her attention.]
