# Twilight Syndrome Murder Case

## What It Is

[The Twilight Syndrome Murder Case is a handheld game cartridge left on Jabberwock Island by Monokuma as the second motive of the Killing School Trip. It presents as an entry in a horror series and plays as an adventure game about five girls at a school, identified only by letters, and a sixth girl who is murdered. It is not fiction. Every event in it happened, and the girls in it are five of the students playing it.]

## Contents

[The game recreates a real sequence of events from the students' erased time at Hope's Peak Academy. A transferred student, the little sister of a yakuza heir, harassed and threatened one of the five. One of the girls' friends killed her over it, and the girls covered for the killer. The heir then found and killed his sister's murderer in revenge. None of the players remember any of it, and the game is designed so that the two people it implicates will recognise themselves.]

## The Truth Edition

[The cartridge holds a second version, unlocked separately, which strips away the coding and states plainly who each lettered girl is and what each of them did. Almost nobody manages to reach it alone. The students who play the standard version come away knowing that something was covered up and not knowing by whom, which is precisely the uncertainty the motive needs.]

## Effect

[The game did exactly what it was designed to do. It gave a boy already inclined toward an eye-for-an-eye philosophy a name to blame, and gave the girl who had covered for the killer a reason to try to settle the matter privately. The confrontation that followed ended with her death, killed by a third party acting on her own initiative to protect the boy from having to do it himself, which cost the killer her life at the trial and cost him his right eye trying to stop the execution.]
