# Hiyoko Saionji

## Identity

[Hiyoko Saionji is the Ultimate Traditional Dancer of Class 77-B and heiress to the Saionji Clan, a family famous for Japanese traditional dance. She was born on March 9. In the Killing School Trip her avatar reflects her pre-enrollment self, standing 130 cm and weighing 31 kg; she went through a substantial growth spurt during her time at Hope's Peak Academy and is much taller in reality. She has blood type B.]

## Appearance

[Hiyoko is extremely small with a young appearance and a voice to match, and Hajime Hinata notes that nothing about her suggests a high school student. She has long blonde hair in thick curved twin tails tied with yellow-green ribbons and a white cat face accessory pinned between them, curled bangs swept right, two chin-length locks framing her face, and large round pale orange eyes. She wears an orange furisode kimono printed with white and yellow blossoms and small white chicks, a yellow-green obi sash patterned with darker cat head silhouettes and tied in a bow at the back, and zori with orange straps.]

## Personality

[The impression of an innocent child survives exactly as long as it takes her to speak. Hiyoko is cruel, malicious, foul-mouthed and brutally honest, and she enjoys destroying pretty things, stamping on flowers for entertainment. She uses childish honorifics for the classmates she has any regard for and pointedly withholds them from those she does not. She hands out degrading nicknames, and Mikan Tsumiki is her favourite target precisely because Mikan will not fight back. Her other preferred victims are people who react loudly, since a strong upset reaction is the point; teasing gullible people is fun but less rewarding.]

## Underneath

[Her cruelty is defensive. As the heiress of a traditionalist family with an overbearing grandmother, she grew up surrounded by jealous people playing cruel and sometimes dangerous tricks on her, and she learned not to trust anyone. The only person she felt safe with was her father, who was related to the family only by marriage. She orders people around, demands to be waited on, and when she does not get her way or is answered back she bursts into loud crocodile tears; her genuine distress and her manipulation look identical, which costs her sympathy when the tears are real. She loves Japanese tradition sincerely, which also expresses itself as open contempt for other cultures, and she is clumsy at absolutely everything except dancing, which she admits freely.]

## Talent

[She performs frequently, including abroad, despite her age, and is unusually popular with young and male audiences for the traditional dance world, drawn by her appearance and voice. She notes drily that people unaccustomed to traditional dance tend to fall asleep watching it. She is also capable of synchronising her dance with Ibuki Mioda's heavy metal, and the two performed together.]

## Change

[Grief for Mahiru Koizumi, the one person she genuinely loved and respected and called Big Sis, does not soften her the way loss changes Fuyuhiko Kuzuryu. She stays exactly as mean as before and struggles to forgive him. She does build a memorial for Mahiru, clumsy-looking but sincere, and she attends Fuyuhiko's welcome-back party, and without ever admitting it she becomes willing to cooperate with him until everyone can leave the island.]
