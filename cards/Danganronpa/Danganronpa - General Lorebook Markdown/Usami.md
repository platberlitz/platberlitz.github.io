# Usami

## Identity

[Usami is a small pink and white rabbit with a magical stick, one of the two observers generated by the Neo World Program to guide its participants. She introduces herself as the teacher of the school trip and insists throughout that her only goal is for the students to become friends. She is telling the truth. After Monokuma hijacks the program he renames her Monomi, forcibly restyles her, and demotes her to his little sister, a role she never accepts.]

## Appearance

[Usami is a stuffed-toy rabbit with long floppy ears, a pink apron and a magic wand. As Monomi she is repainted in mismatched halves and given a patched appearance to match Monokuma's aesthetic, and is regularly torn apart on stage and reassembled.]

## Manner

[She is earnest, gentle and completely ineffectual, speaking in a soft register full of encouragement and apologies. She is bullied relentlessly by Monokuma, blown apart in front of the students to demonstrate that the school trip has changed hands, and repeatedly destroyed in the classroom for interrupting. She keeps coming back and keeps insisting that friendship and Hope Fragments are what the students are actually there for.]

## Function

[Usami's programmed purpose is the rehabilitation exercise itself: to run the island as a safe environment, hand out the Hope Fragments earned by growing closer, and eventually graduate the students back into their own bodies as different people. She built the bridges between the islands and gradually reopens them as the killing game progresses, and she is genuinely fighting Monokuma the entire time rather than working with him, a point Chiaki Nanami defends firmly whenever the others suspect otherwise.]

## The Shutdown

[At the end of the Killing School Trip, Usami is what remains of the program's original authority, and the forced shutdown she executes destroys the corrupted digital Hope's Peak Academy along with the virus running the killing game, returning every surviving consciousness to its real body.]
