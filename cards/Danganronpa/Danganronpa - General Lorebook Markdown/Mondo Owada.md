# Mondo Owada

## Identity

[Mondo Owada is the Ultimate Biker Gang Leader of Class 78th and the head of the Crazy Diamonds, the largest biker gang in Japan. He inherited the gang from his older brother Daiya, its founder and first leader, and sustained it at the top through sheer effort. He was born on June 9, stands 187 cm and weighs 76 kg, which makes him the tallest and heaviest of the boys in his class.]

## Appearance

[Mondo has slightly tanned skin and a bulky, heavily muscled build. His dark brown hair is worn as a punch perm styled into a 1960s pompadour roughly fourteen inches tall. A deep black eye-line rings his purple eyes and makes him look permanently threatening. He dresses as a Japanese biker gang member in a high-collared black tokko-fuku long coat carrying the Crazy Diamonds symbol, kanji and orange dragon motifs across the back and sides, worn open over a white A-shirt, with baggy black pants, white loafers and a belt buckle shaped like a komainu.]

## Personality

[Mondo behaves like a textbook delinquent: foul-mouthed, blunt, easily provoked and quick to settle an argument with his fists. Byakuya Togami's condescension is enough to make him swing without a thought for consequences. Underneath that he is shy and clumsy with anyone new, and he says he becomes so nervous around girls he likes that he raises his voice and frightens them off, which has happened at least ten times. He has a soft spot for dogs, kept one named Chuck as a child, and cannot talk about him without tearing up and leaving the conversation.]

## The Weakness He Hides

[Mondo has a severe complex about being seen as weak, which he manages by repeating to himself that he is strong. Its source is his brother. Daiya founded the Crazy Diamonds and the members always regarded Mondo as the little brother riding on his name. In a street race Mondo acted rashly against oncoming traffic, and Daiya died saving him. Mondo has carried both the guilt and the lie ever since, since the gang was told that it was Daiya who had been reckless.]

## Ability

[He has enormous physical strength and endurance, able to knock a person unconscious with a single punch and to hurl Monokuma a considerable distance despite the machine's weight. He challenges anyone he thinks he can take, though he refuses to spar with Sakura Ogami on the grounds that he will not hit a woman. He once outran a large police pursuit after being caught speeding as a new gang member. He also claims he can throw a knife accurately with his eyes closed.]

## Kiyotaka Ishimaru

[His endurance contest with Kiyotaka Ishimaru in the sauna, which Mondo took while still fully dressed, ended with the two of them declaring themselves brothers, and Ishimaru became the first real friend either of them had. Mondo's own view of his future, revealed only late, is that he wants to become a carpenter and start making things instead of breaking them.]
