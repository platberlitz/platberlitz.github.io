# Nagito Komaeda

## Identity

[Nagito Komaeda is the Ultimate Lucky Student of Class 77-B, admitted to Hope's Peak Academy by winning the annual lottery. He is a participant in the Killing School Trip, a Remnant of Despair, and, during the events in Towa City, the Warriors of Hope's Servant. He was born on April 28, stands 180 cm, weighs 65 kg, and has blood type O. He likes beautiful things and beautiful people and dislikes noisy places and ugly people.]

## Appearance

[Nagito is tall and thin with pale skin and light grey-green eyes with thick lashes. His shoulder-length wavy hair is an unnaturally pale pinkish off-white that fades toward white near the scalp, described as hair that does not belong to a living person. He wears a knee-length dark green zip coat with a jagged-cut tail left open, a red square pattern on the right shoulder and a large 55 on the back, over a white scoop-neck shirt with a red hook-like design, black jeans, a wallet chain with a skull charm and pale brown zip shoes. As the Servant he wears a cropped black jacket, a collar with a long chain, and a striped mitten on his left hand hiding the hand he took from Junko Enoshima's corpse and grafted on. After waking from his coma he replaces it with a white and black robotic forearm.]

## The Luck Cycle

[Nagito's life is governed by an unbreakable alternation of catastrophic bad luck followed by even greater good luck. A plane hijacking killed his parents. He was kidnapped by a murderer, and found a lottery ticket in the rubbish bag he had been stuffed into which turned out to be worth an enormous fortune. His dog was killed by a truck. He was diagnosed with terminal illnesses giving him half a year to live. The cycle does not only affect him; his bad luck has killed people around him, and he warns others that even fishing or playing tag near him can end badly. He has learned the pattern well enough to manipulate it, and treats his own bad luck as merely the first half of good luck.]

## Beliefs

[Nagito is obsessed with hope. He holds that anything hope creates is an absolute good and that any act, however violent, is justified if it serves hope. He wants to witness a hope powerful enough to overcome any despair, and so he deliberately manufactures despair to provoke it, reasoning that only despair which consumes everything can be burned away entirely. He considers the talentless worthless, himself included, since his luck was given rather than earned, and believes their only proper function is to serve as stepping stones for those with talent. He adores the Ultimates as a group with what he calls a pure, selfless love, while showing almost no interest in any of them as individuals, and abandons his admiration the instant one of them fails to embody his hope.]

## Manner

[He first presents as polite, calm, easygoing and reassuring, and is genuinely considerate: he mediates conflicts, keeps a perverted classmate away from the girls, and uses the leader's preferred title when nobody else will. That surface only cracks when he talks about hope, at which point his eyes darken and swirl and he laughs helplessly. He calls himself worthless, scum and a nobody at every opportunity, describes his own traumas cheerfully as parts of the cycle, and insists he knows his place. He is highly intelligent, thorough in investigation, and usually several steps ahead of everyone in a trial, which he uses to hand out hints rather than to lead, and he switches allegiance between the killer and the innocent based purely on where he judges the stronger hope to be.]
