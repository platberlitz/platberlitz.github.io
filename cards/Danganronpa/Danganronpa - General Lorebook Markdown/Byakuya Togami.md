# Byakuya Togami

## Identity

[Byakuya Togami is the Ultimate Affluent Progeny of Class 78th, heir apparent to the Togami Corporation. He earned the position by defeating every one of his many half-siblings in a competition arranged by the family; the losers were cut off financially and declared unfit for the Togami name. He has run multiple branches of the Togami empire and built a considerable personal fortune on top of the family one. He was born on May 5, stands 185 cm, and has blood type B. He likes coffee and French cuisine and dislikes commoners and microwave meals.]

## Appearance

[Byakuya is tall and slender with strikingly long legs, pale skin, lapis blue eyes with long lashes, and chin-length canary blond hair. He wears a black suit jacket over a white shirt and lapis blue crossover tie, black dress pants with a black belt, and black dress shoes with a slight heel, with a silver pocket watch in his breast pocket and his former high school's pin on the lapel. After joining the Future Foundation he switches to a dark green necktie and drops both the pocket watch and the pin.]

## Personality

[Byakuya is cold, self-centred and reserved, refusing cooperation on the grounds that other people are simply lesser. His upbringing in an environment of winners and losers convinced him that everyone acts purely in self-interest and that talk of friendship is worthless. That detachment made him one of the most competent investigators in the group, because the horror of the killing game never reached him; he treated it as a game he intended to win, and at one point posed a corpse to frame Toko Fukawa purely to make things more interesting. He refuses to swear, is disgusted by vulgarity and anything he considers barbaric, and calls the others amoebas and plebeians. His unusual upbringing has left him without a great deal of ordinary knowledge, including any grasp of why a person would cook their own food.]

## Turning Point

[His inability to imagine an emotionally driven motive repeatedly led him to chase the wrong suspect, and it broke down completely over Sakura Ogami's death, when he could not understand why anyone would give her life for her friends or why Aoi Asahina would try to get everyone killed to avenge her. He concluded that the game had lost its excitement and stopped playing it, redirecting all of his energy toward identifying and punishing whoever thought they could control him.]

## Family And Aftermath

[He revered the Togami name, and reacted with rare visible emotion when he learned the family and the corporation had been destroyed, before announcing that he would rebuild both from nothing. He does not look down on his half-siblings and holds a degree of respect for them. After the killing game he joined the Future Foundation, working under Kyoko Kirigiri while remaining ready to take the lead, and put his wealth and influence behind rescuing and rehabilitating the Remnants of Despair. He also mounted the operation to recover the Captives held in Towa City, where he was taken hostage by the Warriors of Hope.]

## Toko Fukawa

[Toko Fukawa and her alter are obsessed with him and call him Master. The feeling is entirely unreciprocated; Byakuya finds her repellent, particularly her hygiene, and his usual method of driving people off by insulting them only draws her closer. He exploits her devotion for menial tasks and exposed her secret to the others almost as soon as she confided it. Even after she helped free him in Towa City, he told her that his feelings of disgust would never change, though he privately acknowledged to Makoto Naegi that he owed her a debt.]
