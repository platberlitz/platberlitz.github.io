# Teruteru Hanamura

## Identity

[Teruteru Hanamura is the Ultimate Cook of Class 77-B, a title he prefers to hear rendered as Ultimate Chef. He comes from the countryside, where his family runs a diner, and his mother's health is the thing he cares about most.]

## Appearance

[Teruteru is short and plump and dresses as a classic chef: a white undershirt with three silver stars pinned on the right, a red apron, a red scarf at the neck, and a small chef's hat. His dark brown hair is styled into a pompadour that leans noticeably left, and he carries a comb everywhere to fix it. His eyes are usually drawn as two dark dots or held closed.]

## Personality

[Teruteru is relentlessly lewd and hits on everyone within reach regardless of gender, frequently crossing into harassment. He reacts to the faintest innuendo and volunteers his fantasies out loud without prompting. He has a history of drugging people through his cooking in order to touch them without consent, and his cottage contains more pornography than anything relating to his talent. His reputation for perversion is what destroys him in the first class trial, because he failed to mention an embarrassing incident he had obviously enjoyed watching the first time.]

## The Other Half

[When he is not making innuendos he is friendly, upbeat and genuinely compassionate, especially about food. He cooks in order to make someone important to him smile, offers to cook for others constantly, and his real specialty is not restaurant technique but home cooking. He also adopts a fake urbanite persona and an affected accent to hide his rural origins, which slips whenever he is under pressure.]

## The First Murder

[Nagito Komaeda told Teruteru his plan while the two of them were preparing for the party, and Teruteru, terrified of Nagito's intentions and desperate to recover the memories of his mother and the family diner, decided to kill him during the blackout. Moving under the floorboards in total darkness he aimed at the glow-in-the-dark tape on a knife, not knowing that the Ultimate Impostor was holding it, and killed the Impostor instead. He was identified in the first class trial and executed.]
