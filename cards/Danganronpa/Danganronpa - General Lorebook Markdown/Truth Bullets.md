# Truth Bullets

## What They Are

[Truth Bullets are the pieces of evidence gathered during the investigation phase of a killing game and used during the class trial that follows. They are collected in the student handbook, which every participant carries and which doubles as an identification card, a rulebook and a map. Each Bullet is a single verified fact: a wound, an object, a time, a statement, a piece of testimony.]

## How They Are Used

[During a nonstop debate the students argue simultaneously and the weak points in what they are saying have to be identified and destroyed by firing the correct piece of evidence at the correct claim. Nothing else works. A true statement cannot be broken, and the wrong Bullet fired at the right claim proves nothing, which is why the trials reward knowing precisely what one has rather than arguing loudly.]

## Their Limits

[A Truth Bullet is only as good as the investigation that produced it, and an investigation conducted under time pressure by frightened people beside a body will always be incomplete. Evidence can be planted, staged, misread, and deliberately supplied by someone who wants a particular conclusion reached, and every killing game contains at least one culprit intelligent enough to build a scene out of true facts arranged to say something false. The trials turn on the moment somebody notices that a fact everyone accepted is pointing the wrong way.]

## The Name Elsewhere

[The term was carried over to the ammunition of the Megaphone Hacking Gun in Towa City, where the rounds fired at Monokuma units are also called Truth Bullets. The parallel is deliberate: in both cases the weapon is a stated fact, aimed precisely, at something that cannot survive being told the truth.]

## Why The Format Holds

[Restricting argument to verified evidence is what keeps a class trial from collapsing into whoever shouts loudest, and it is also what makes the trials survivable at all. A frightened group left to argue freely will convict the person they like least. A group required to produce an object, a time or a wound in support of every claim will occasionally arrive at the truth instead, which is the narrow margin the survivors of both killing games got out through.]
