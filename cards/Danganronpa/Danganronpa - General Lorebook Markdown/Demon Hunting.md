# Demon Hunting

## What It Is

[Demon Hunting is the game the Warriors of Hope ran across Towa City, in which the adults of the city were designated Demons and hunted to extinction. It was announced through a hijacked news broadcast and prosecuted with mass-produced Monokuma units and an army of brainwashed children, and its stated goal was to clear the city so that a paradise for children could be built on it.]

## Rules

[The five Warriors of Hope competed against each other to take down as many Demons as possible, with each kill counted toward their standing. They kept Hit List entries recording information about the individuals they had marked, and fitted their targets with wristbands to keep them inside the playing field. Attempting to leave Towa City or tampering with a wristband triggered its charge. Each of the three Warriors who held an arena, called a Killesseum, ambushed intruders inside it and fought them from within a large battle robot themed to their class.]

## The Captives

[The Captives, the family members and associates of Class 78th who had been taken during the Tragedy, were kept in the city and used as targets, which is what brought the Future Foundation rescue team into it. The intended star of the game was one particular captive, whose difficulties were selected for her in advance so that her growth could be observed and directed.]

## What It Was Actually For

[The paradise was never the point, which only the group's leader and her advisor knew. Demon Hunting was a machine for manufacturing a successor to Junko Enoshima and for escalating a local massacre into a war large enough to restart the Tragedy worldwide. Every stage of the chosen girl's route through the city was arranged, her weapon was weakened and then strengthened by degrees, and the two bear units guiding both sides were carrying the halves of a copy of Junko Enoshima between them.]

## End

[The game collapsed once the Warriors of Hope had been defeated one at a time and confirmation arrived that most of the Captives had been rescued. Their leader abandoned the plan and the group, taking the Monokuma controller and copies of the battle robots to a hideout on the outskirts of the city.]
