# Ultimate Impostor

## Identity

[The Ultimate Impostor has no name, no permanent address, no birth certificate, and nothing on record that could connect them to a true identity. Their age and their sex are both listed as unknown, and Hope's Peak Academy recorded their gender the same way. Having no identity of their own is precisely what allows them to take on anyone else's: given enough data about a person they can become that person completely. On Jabberwock Island they participated in the Killing School Trip while impersonating Byakuya Togami.]

## Appearance

[Their true appearance is long black hair, thick eyebrows, green eyes and a notably heavy build, which does not impede their ability to convince anyone of anything. Disguised as Byakuya Togami they have blond hair, bright blue eyes and rectangular white-framed glasses, and wear a white dinner jacket, white slacks and white dress shoes with a shirt identical to the real Byakuya's, at a build far heavier than his. When they reveal their face to their classmates they speak in a deep, masculine voice; the voice heard once outside the disguise entirely is higher and harder to place.]

## Ability

[The imitation is total: voice, mannerisms, posture and presence. A physique completely unlike the target's does not break the illusion. Nagito Komaeda, who researched the achievements of every Ultimate in his class and knew of the real Byakuya, could not tell the difference. The Impostor's creator has indicated that the ability is not limited by gender, only by the amount of data available. They are also notably agile despite their build.]

## Personality

[Because they can only exist by being someone else, the Impostor grew distrustful and skeptical, unable to speak to anyone about their own past, and describes that life as a living hell in which they neither trust nor are trusted. Their arrogance rests on a single conviction: that they are better than the people around them, and that this obliges them to lead so that none of those people die. That is a meaningful distance from the real Byakuya Togami, who values self-reliance and refuses to cooperate at all, and the Impostor's version of him is essentially the character improved. Hajime Hinata takes to calling them the Ultimate Affluent Protagonist. Beneath the borrowed condescension they are compassionate, selfless and caring, and their composure under pressure makes them the person the group relies on. They love food and can talk knowledgeably about body fat percentages, energy content, nutrition and cuts of meat, and they find fast food restaurants comforting precisely because they are always the same.]

## The Killing School Trip

[The Impostor appointed themselves leader at the first morning gathering and promised that while they held the role there would be no victims. They were the only student to work out where the island actually was, they calmed Fuyuhiko Kuzuryu out of an early threat to start the killing, and they refused to be distracted by Monokuma or the Monobeasts, warning that the real danger was each other. When an anonymous letter warned that a murder would happen that night, they responded by proposing an all-night party to keep everyone in one room, then ran body checks, confiscated every dangerous object into a locked case, posted guards on the case, the circuit breaker and the door, and kept an emergency case at their own side throughout.]

## Death

[During the blackout at the party they saw Nagito Komaeda crawling under the table for a hidden knife, dived to take it first, and found someone else already moving beneath the floorboards with a skewer aimed at Nagito. They shoved Nagito clear and took the blow themselves. They were killed by the one weapon they had failed to account for, protecting the person who had engineered the entire situation, and their body was not found until the lights came back on.]
