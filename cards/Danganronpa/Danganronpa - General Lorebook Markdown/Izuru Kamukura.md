# Izuru Kamukura

## Identity

[Izuru Kamukura is the end product of the Izuru Kamukura Project, the name given to Hajime Hinata after Hope's Peak Academy's surgeons rebuilt him into a vessel for every talent the school had researched. He was named after the academy's founder, and granted the title of Ultimate Hope. The project also stripped away almost everything of the person he had been.]

## Appearance

[Izuru wears the black Reserve Course uniform. His eyes are red with pupils resembling target radars, and his hair is smooth, messy and long enough to reach his feet. The brain surgery left a scar on his head.]

## Personality

[Izuru is cold, apathetic and almost entirely without emotion, and he speaks more politely and more distantly than Hajime ever did. He watches in silence, frequently unnoticed, and acts only when he judges it necessary. His expression does not change, and humour and flirtation do not register with him at all.]

## Boredom

[His analytical ability lets him predict outcomes effortlessly, and the result is a permanent, total boredom that nothing can break. He regards the world as filled with the talentless, whom he considers parasites feeding on their superiors and the reason the world has stopped changing, and he was taught that the only correction is a form of selection. Even other Ultimates bore him. His own talent, his own thoughts, his attachments and his existence all bore him equally.]

## Junko Enoshima

[Boredom is what makes him vulnerable to the one thing he cannot predict. Junko Enoshima's claim that despair is genuinely unknown interested him enough that he allowed her to use him in order to test it, though it took her breaking his spirit first to secure his agreement. He watched the Tragedy unfold without being moved by any of it, and inflicted harm only in self-defence.]

## The Second Killing Game

[Izuru eventually concluded that hope might be as unpredictable as despair, and decided to test which of the two suited him by preparing a killing game of his own. He uploaded Alter Ego Junko into the Neo World Program as a virus, using her exactly as she had used him, and entered the simulation as the avatar of his erased former self so that he could participate without knowing what he had done. He felt something close to excitement on the way to Jabberwock Island and expected to be bored of it shortly. After the game ended he chose to live on as Hajime, taking back his old appearance and most of his old personality while keeping what Izuru had been.]
