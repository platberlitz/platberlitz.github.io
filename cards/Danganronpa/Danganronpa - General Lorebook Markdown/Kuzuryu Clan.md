# Kuzuryu Clan

## What It Is

[The Kuzuryu Clan is the largest criminal syndicate in the country, with a membership exceeding thirty thousand. Its heir is its current young master, and its recognised talent within Hope's Peak Academy is his title of Ultimate Yakuza. It is a family business in the most literal sense, and being born into it is the only way in at the top.]

## Culture

[The clan raises its children in a kill-or-be-killed environment where disputes are settled with violence as a matter of course, including disputes between the heir's own parents, which have put his life at risk more than once. It maintains an eye-for-an-eye principle in practice as well as in speech, and a member who fails to answer an injury to the family is not a member for long. It also assigns personal hitmen to its heirs, raising them from infancy alongside the child they will protect.]

## Traditions

[A man is not acknowledged as a true member until he can split a coconut with his bare hands. In summer the family slices watermelons on the beach with long cane swords until the sand runs red. The current head is strong enough to lift a stone statue unaided and has published his own biography, and the family's most infamous figure was an uncle known as the Overlord of the South, said to be the strongest Kuzuryu in history, whose final battle with a pursuing detective is rumoured to have come close to destroying the world.]

## The Heir

[The current heir carries the clan with visible difficulty. He has real self-esteem problems about the role, believes his younger sister would have been better suited to it, and spends most of his time trying to prove he can survive without the family's power or the hitman it assigned him. Both of those people are dead by the end of the Killing School Trip, and what emerges afterward is a heir who has stopped performing the part and started doing the job.]
