# Future Foundation

## What It Is

[The Future Foundation is an organisation formed by alumni and former personnel of Hope's Peak Academy during or after the Tragedy, with two purposes: to defeat Ultimate Despair and to undo the damage it had done to the world. It was already well established by the end of the Killing School Life, well enough to reach the survivors of that game and extract them. Monokuma referred to it as World Ender in an attempt to make the students believe it was hostile, and the phrase turned out to mean only that the Foundation intended to end the world Ultimate Despair had built.]

## Structure

[The Foundation is divided into numbered divisions with distinct responsibilities, from overall direction and daily operations through construction, expansion and a military peacekeeping force. The divisions do not agree with each other about method. Some hold that every trace of despair must be exterminated along with anyone who assists it, in order to minimise further deaths. Others hold that the source of despair in human nature has to be removed instead, or that the answer is to demonstrate hope rather than to fight despair at all.]

## The Survivors

[The six survivors of the Killing School Life were rescued by the Foundation and had their school memories restored, and all of them joined it to fight the Remnants of Despair. Five became full members with positions. The sixth was refused full membership and kept as an intern, because the serial killer sharing her body was judged too great a risk, and she was told she would be recognised only once she had permanently stopped that alter from killing.]

## Jabberwock Island

[When a member of the 14th Division realised that fifteen people in Foundation custody were Remnants of Despair, he moved them secretly to Jabberwock Island and put them into the Neo World Program rather than let the Foundation dispose of them. Two other members joined the rescue operation when the program was found to have been sabotaged, and the three of them arrived on the island as the killing game inside it reached its end.]

## Towa City

[The Foundation received information that the Captives, the family members and associates of Class 78th who had been taken during the Killing School Life, were being held in Towa City. It sent a team to recover them. The rescue helicopters were shot down by Monokuma units, the team on the ground was scattered, and its leader was taken hostage by the children running the city, which left the operation in the hands of an intern and a civilian girl.]
