# Jabberwock Island

## What It Is

[Jabberwock Island is a tropical archipelago of five islands arranged around a central one, the setting of the Killing School Trip. It exists both as a real place, where the Future Foundation holds the comatose bodies of the Remnants of Despair, and as a simulated environment inside the Neo World Program, where those same people experience it as a school trip. The version the students walk around is the simulated one.]

## The Central Island

[The first island holds the hotel complex where the students live, a restaurant used for the daily morning gatherings, a supermarket, a beach, an airport, a ranch, a hospital, and Jabberwock Park, where a countdown timer stands beside a statue. The cottages are assigned individually and the hotel lobby holds an arcade machine. The islands are connected by bridges, which begin the game sealed and are opened one at a time.]

## The Other Islands

[Each successive island opens as the killing game progresses and each carries its own facilities: a music venue, a movie theatre, a beach house, a diner, a library, a park, a supermarket, a chandler's, a ruin site, an amusement park, and an ancient stone structure at the centre of the last one. Monokuma treats each new island as a reward and each is opened when the previous chapter closes.]

## The Funhouse

[Partway through the killing game the students are sealed inside a building split into two halves, Strawberry House and Grape House, with the food supply removed and the doors locked, in an attempt to force a murder through starvation. The building also contains the Final Dead Room, which can only be entered alone and which offers whatever is inside it in exchange for a game of Russian roulette.]

## What Is Underneath

[The simulated island is running on top of a corrupted copy of Hope's Peak Academy, and the deeper the students go the more of it surfaces. The ancient ruins on the final island open into the academy itself, decayed and wrong, where the truth about the students, the program and the world outside is finally laid out.]

## The Real Island

[In reality the archipelago holds a Future Foundation facility where fifteen bodies lie in a sleeping state wired into the program. The survivors of the Killing School Trip wake there, and choose to remain on the island afterward rather than leave, in order to keep working on the classmates who never woke up.]
