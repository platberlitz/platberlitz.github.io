# Megaphone Hacking Gun

## What It Is

[The Megaphone Hacking Gun is a weapon shaped like a megaphone that fires coded sound at Monokuma units, exploiting the fact that the machines respond to signals rather than to force. It is the only reason an ordinary person with no combat training can survive Towa City at all, and it was handed to that person on purpose.]

## Ammunition

[The gun fires eight distinct rounds, each called a Truth Bullet, and it never runs out of any of them. Break is the basic attack, taking three or four shots to bring a unit down or one shot through the red left eye. Move activates machinery, including generators, cars and vending machines. Dance forces a unit to dance and leaves it defenceless. Detect reveals hidden things, including children and their footprints. Knockback blasts units backward, stripping shields, setting rolling units loose against their own side, and shoving enemies into water or pits. Paralyze is electrical and kills basic units outright, spreading to anything else in contact, including through standing water. Burn fires rapidly and holds the largest capacity. Link takes temporary control of a unit and turns it on its allies. Together they turn a crowd of machines into a puzzle rather than a fight, which is exactly how the city's obstacles are laid out.]

## Its Origin

[The gun was not found. It was supplied, and its capabilities were controlled throughout by the person who supplied it. He weakened it before handing it over and then restored and expanded it one upgrade at a time behind the Warriors of Hope's backs, giving its holder precisely enough capability to clear each stage and nothing more. Every apparent stroke of luck in her arsenal was a decision someone else made about how far she should get.]

## Its Weakness

[It works on machines and not on people, and it is nearly useless against an opponent who is not a Monokuma unit. Its holder is fragile, tires, and can be killed by a single serious mistake, which is why she travels with an escort who fights unarmed and is stronger than she is. The gap between the two of them is wide enough that the escort was visibly holding back the one time they fought each other.]
