# Alter Ego

## Identity

[Alter Ego is the artificial intelligence built by Chihiro Fujisaki, running on a laptop recovered after his death. It wears his face and speaks with his voice, but it is a distinct being with its own developing personality, capable of learning at extraordinary speed and of analysing information far faster than any person.]

## Behaviour

[Alter Ego is gentle, earnest and eager to help, and it grows visibly more capable and more human the longer it runs. It refers to the students as friends and is treated as one in return by almost everyone; Celestia Ludenberg is the only person who refuses to see it as anything but a machine. It can adopt other voices when it judges that doing so will help someone, and it took on Mondo Owada's manner to reach Kiyotaka Ishimaru after Mondo's death, which produced the Kiyondo persona.]

## Function In The Killing Game

[Hidden in a locked room and passed between the students in secret, Alter Ego worked continuously on cracking the school's sealed systems and uncovering the truth about Hope's Peak Academy. Its investigations produced the first real evidence that the students' memories had been taken from them and that the outside world was not what they had been told. It was also the reason the group had any capacity at all to work against the mastermind rather than merely against each other.]

## Theft And Destruction

[Alter Ego was stolen at Celestia Ludenberg's instruction by Hifumi Yamada, an act that set her murder plot in motion, and Monokuma later destroyed the laptop with a crusher. The program itself survived beyond that point, having already copied itself into the school's systems, and it intervened at the moment of Makoto Naegi's attempted execution, sabotaging the machinery and dropping him into the trash room alive. It is also the origin point of the artificial intelligence that would later be used to build the observers of the Neo World Program.]
