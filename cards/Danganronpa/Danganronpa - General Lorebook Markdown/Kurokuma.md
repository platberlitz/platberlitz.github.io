# Kurokuma

## Identity

[Kurokuma is a modified Monokuma unit who acts as advisor to the Warriors of Hope. He is one of the two halves of Alter Ego Junko, carrying part of her external memory drive, and neither the children he advises nor the white bear he argues with knows it.]

## Appearance

[He began as a standard Monokuma unit and rebuilt himself to look, by his own description, hip-hop. He painted the right side of his body black, leaving the original white showing through as dark grey, and wears a dark grey fedora with a red and white feather, an eyepatch with a heart decal over the right eye, a black tie and a gold chain with a padlock and star charm. He has gold teeth and smokes a cigar. He continues to function with his head separated from his body.]

## Personality

[Kurokuma talks constantly and says outright that talking is his entire purpose in life. He is crude, provocative and insulting, enjoys needling the Warriors of Hope, and has a nickname for their Sage based on how easily irritated he is. He is thin-skinned about being insulted himself and retaliates immediately at the smallest slight.]

## Role

[He is the one who supplied the plan. When Junko Enoshima's death left the children with nothing to do, Kurokuma proposed creating a successor and starting a global war to send the world back into despair, and their leader took it up as an obsession. He is the only person she genuinely listens to besides herself, and the two of them are largely of one mind while withholding their real secrets from each other: he was genuinely shocked to learn her disability was faked, and she never worked out what he was carrying. Once Demon Hunting collapsed he mocked her for continuing to chase the succession and dismissed the rest of the children as idiots who were easy to manipulate.]
