# Novoselic

## What It Is

[Novoselic is a small European kingdom, the homeland of the Ultimate Princess of Class 77-B and the country she is heir to. It is prosperous enough to matter and small enough that its royal family can be described, by its own heir, as largely a figurehead institution.]

## Customs

[Its traditions are consistently more alarming than the calm manner in which they are described. Accounts of Novoselic law, punishment, festivals and courtship tend to leave listeners silent, and its princess relates them with complete equanimity, having no particular reason to think them unusual. Its ceremonial practices are strange, its history is bloody in places, and its people are apparently untroubled by either.]

## Its Heir

[The heir was sent to Japan to attend Hope's Peak Academy, and her stated reason for wanting to go was to spend time as an ordinary teenager among people her own age, something her position had never permitted. She is fascinated by Japanese culture, by the occult, and by serial killers, in roughly that order, and she pursues all three with royal thoroughness. Her country expects her back.]

## Its Relevance

[Novoselic matters chiefly as the reason its princess exists at all: a kingdom small enough to spare its heir, formal enough to have produced her manners, and distant enough that the Tragedy reached her through the people around her rather than through her own country. What became of it during the collapse is not something she was in a position to influence.]

## Position During The Tragedy

[The kingdom is small, distant, and not the sort of place the collapse would have reached first, and its heir was in Japan when everything began. She had no capacity to protect it and no way to return, and by the time the world began to recover, the conditioning done to her class had made the question of her homeland somebody else's problem. What became of Novoselic during those years is not recorded by anyone in a position to know.]
