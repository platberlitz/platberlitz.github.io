# Yasuhiro Hagakure

## Identity

[Yasuhiro Hagakure is the Ultimate Clairvoyant of Class 78th. He is older than his classmates, having been held back several times, and states his age as twenty one. His mother is Hiroko Hagakure. His fortune telling is reported to be accurate about thirty percent of the time, and he charges heavily for it.]

## Appearance

[Yasuhiro has light brown skin, stubble on his chin, thin dark brown eyes, and dark brown hair styled in large spiked locs that bend backward. He wears three layers of shirts: a green Japanese school uniform jacket draped over his shoulders, an unbuttoned white shirt beneath it, and a small yellow short-sleeved shirt under that, with a brown haramaki, a yellow rope for a belt and large black pants rolled up past the ankles.]

## Personality

[He is one of the more cheerful and laid-back students and also one of the least popular, a fact he admits freely, describing himself as friendless. He is cowardly, lazy, slow-witted and prone to selfishness. He is disastrous with money and permanently buried in debt, having once paid a hundred million yen for a crystal ball made of glass on the strength of a claim that Napoleon, Genghis Khan and George Washington had owned it. He talks at length about conspiracy theories and about the time aliens stole his hamburger. He is terrified of ghosts and, whenever he senses one, immediately begins reciting chants or calling on gods, heroes and celebrities at random.]

## Manner

[He is friendly toward his classmates in his own way, attaching an affectionate suffix to everyone's surname. The exception is Sakura Ogami, whom he is afraid of and refers to by an insulting nickname. He hates having his fortune telling compared to the occult and insists constantly that the two are unrelated. His hygiene is poor, and he claims to wash his hair once a season.]

## In The Killing Game

[He initially treated the killing game as an elaborate prank, and once he understood it was real he became panicky and was generally regarded by the others as useless. His worst moment came when he pushed the accusation of Sakura Ogami's murder onto Toko Fukawa, a betrayal she never forgave. He survived to the end regardless, joined the Future Foundation with the other survivors, and was granted full membership.]
