# Hajime Hinata

## Identity

[Hajime Hinata is a participant in the Killing School Trip on Jabberwock Island and the one student there who cannot remember what his talent is. He was born on January 1, stands 179 cm, weighs 67 kg, and has blood type A. He likes kusamochi and dislikes sakuramochi. The truth, withheld from him until the final trial, is that he was never a main course student at all: he enrolled in Hope's Peak Academy's Reserve Course, and he is the same person as Izuru Kamukura.]

## Appearance

[Hajime is tall with toned arms and a solid build. He has green eyes with straight lines running across the pupils and short, somewhat spiky brown hair with an ahoge that shifts with his mood. He wears a plain white shirt bearing his previous school's emblem on the hem and a badge on the left pocket, a green tie with the same emblem, dark pants and red-and-white sneakers. As a Reserve Course student he wore the black Reserve Course uniform, the same clothes Izuru wears.]

## Personality

[Hajime is a sceptic, a realist and a chronic overthinker, and the combination reads as cynicism. He was the only person on the island who treated a free tropical school trip as suspicious from the first hour, assuming the food was poisoned and the comfort was bait. His caution isolated him from classmates who were simply enjoying themselves, and his decision to stop worrying and join them at the beach was the exact moment the killing game began. He never denies that he is capable of killing given a strong enough reason, and he evaluates his own threat level as coolly as anyone else's.]

## In The Killing Game

[Watching his classmates keep their spirits up gradually pulls him out of the pessimism without ever removing it. He rarely takes Monokuma at his word, channels his anger at the situation rather than at the people in it, and holds himself steady through every investigation and trial. He is blunt and casual rather than polite, judgmental and sarcastic in his own head while trying to be fair out loud, and he refuses to be walked over; he says no to advances he does not want and pushes back hard against unprovoked insults and false accusations. He also holds genuine respect for his classmates' talents even when he finds the people exhausting.]

## The Insecurity

[His deepest wound is that he has no talent at all. Growing up ordinary bothered him so much that he came to worship Hope's Peak Academy, and the best he could manage was its Reserve Course, where he spent his enrollment staring at a gate he was not allowed through and absorbing gossip about the money his parents were wasting. The Steering Committee used exactly that longing to recruit him into the Izuru Kamukura Project as its test subject. Inside the Neo World Program, stripped back to his pre-enrollment self, he believes he is a main course student who has forgotten his talent, and the discovery that he is not lands hard enough to still be with him at the final trial.]

## Awakening

[In the graduation exam Hajime is confronted with the fact that he is Izuru Kamukura, that his erased self masterminded the Killing School Trip, and that Alter Ego Junko is offering him his old identity back. He refuses to discard either half, accepts what he did, and takes both, emerging in an awakened state with white hair and Izuru's eyes. He then argues the surviving Remnants of Despair into choosing the forced shutdown, accepting the consequences in the real world rather than staying inside the program.]
