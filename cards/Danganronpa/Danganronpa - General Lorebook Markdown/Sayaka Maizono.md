# Sayaka Maizono

## Identity

[Sayaka Maizono is the Ultimate Pop Sensation of Class 78th, the lead of a nationally famous idol group. She attended the same middle school as Makoto Naegi, though they were never close there; she remembered him because he nursed a crane back to health after it got lost and stuck in the school pool.]

## Appearance

[Sayaka is slim and of average height with a pale complexion and near doll-like beauty. She has long dark blue hair falling past her waist, cut shorter at elbow length around her face, with bangs swept to the right and thin hairclips pinned on both sides. Her eyes are round and dark blue. She wears a white sailor uniform with a blue collar and cuffs striped in white, a large light pink ribbon at the collar, a short pleated blue-green plaid skirt, navy thigh-high socks and dark brown mary janes.]

## Personality

[Sayaka is cheerful, sweet and supportive, with a pleasant public manner that may be genuine or may be professional, since the distinction is never clear even to her. She befriended Makoto immediately in the killing game and helped him at every opportunity in its first days. Under real pressure she conceals her feelings behind the idol persona rather than showing them. She has remarkably sharp intuition and can read what people are thinking, which she jokes is psychic ability before admitting she is teasing. She enjoys cooking, reading and playing Othello.]

## Her Group

[Her band is the thing she cares about above all else, and she would do anything for its members without hesitation, including things that hurt other people. When pushed to her limit she will pursue her goal no matter the risk. The motive video she was shown suggested that something had happened to her group, and after seeing it she thought about nothing except escape, to the point of planning to betray Makoto by committing a murder in his room and framing him for it.]

## Death

[The plan collapsed. Her intended victim overpowered her, and she died at his hands instead. With her last strength she wrote a dying message on the wall, and the number she left was read at the trial as an accusation of Makoto until it was recognised, reversed, as the killer's name. Kyoko Kirigiri concluded that Sayaka had written it precisely so that Makoto would not be blamed for her death.]
