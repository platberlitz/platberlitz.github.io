# The Resistance

## What It Is

[The Resistance is the group of adults who escaped the opening of Demon Hunting and went underground in Towa City. Its stated goal is to restore order by destroying the Monokuma units and to grow its numbers by finding other survivors. It operates out of a hidden base and is led by the son of the man whose company built the machines they are fighting.]

## Conditions

[Its members live in a sealed hideout and are not permitted to leave it, on the leader's insistence that the base's security depends on nobody going outside. He describes this as waiting for the right moment rather than hiding, and later admits it was hiding. Morale rots accordingly. Beneath the base is a secret facility still manufacturing Monokuma units, and deeper still is the reason he refused to relocate.]

## Escalation

[The longer the siege runs, the more fanatical the group becomes. Its members stop distinguishing between the machines and the children controlling them and conclude that the children are as bad as the robots, killing them on sight without hesitation. When the Big Bang Monokuma finally cleared part of the city, the adults poured out behind it into open slaughter, which is the point at which the two people who had helped free it stopped helping and went the other way.]

## The Problem It Represents

[The Resistance is not the opposite of the Warriors of Hope. It is the same argument from the other side of the age line, and it demonstrates the thing the Warriors of Hope had been claiming all along: that the adults of Towa City would kill children given the opportunity. The city's crisis could not be resolved by either side winning, since both were pursuing extermination, and its resolution required somebody to refuse the choice entirely.]
