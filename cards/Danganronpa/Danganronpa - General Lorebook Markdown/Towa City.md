# Towa City

## What It Is

[Towa City is an enormous artificial island in the middle of the ocean, built and controlled by the Towa Group. It began as reclaimed land carrying the company's headquarters and grew outward into rows of research buildings, factories, commercial districts and residential areas, a city that is also a corporate campus.]

## Why It Is Separate

[Its isolation was the point. Being an island let the Towa Group build its own private infrastructure and develop advanced technology on its own terms without outside oversight, which made the conglomerate extremely powerful and made the city effectively a company state. It also meant that when the city turned on itself there was nowhere for anyone to go.]

## Landmarks

[Towa Hills is the tallest structure and the Warriors of Hope's headquarters, with a helicopter port at the top where their airship is moored, and a torture room somewhere inside. Towa Tower stands separately. The city holds a park, a news studio used for the hijacked broadcast, a restaurant chain, a shopping district, an underground shrine, a hotel, a hospital, a subway system, and a large apartment block where one of the Captives was held for over a year. Beneath the Resistance's hideout is a secret factory producing Monokuma units.]

## During The Tragedy

[The Towa Group's robotics division mass-produced Monokuma units in the city, ostensibly as maid robots, and they were used as killing machines during the Tragedy. The company then sold weapons designed to fight them, which made it enormous amounts of money and made it look like a saviour.]

## Demon Hunting

[The city became the site of Demon Hunting, in which the Warriors of Hope sent Monokuma units and brainwashed children through the streets to kill every adult in it. Adults were hunted openly, the Captives were used as targets, and the survivors went underground. Future Foundation rescue helicopters were shot down as they arrived. The crisis was intended to escalate into a war large enough to restart the Tragedy across the world, and it stopped one step short.]
