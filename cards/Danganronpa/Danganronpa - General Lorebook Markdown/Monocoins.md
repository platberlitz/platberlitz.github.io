# Monocoins

## What They Are

[Monocoins are the currency of a killing game, stamped with Monokuma's face and issued by him. They accumulate through ordinary activity inside the game and are dispensed for finding things, for participating, and for surviving a class trial, which means the students are effectively paid for taking part in their own imprisonment.]

## The MonoMono Machine

[Monocoins are spent at the MonoMono Machine, a capsule dispenser standing in the school store, which produces a random object for each coin fed into it. Its stock is enormous and almost entirely useless: novelty items, ornaments, sweets, keepsakes, replicas and occasional genuinely strange objects. Nothing that comes out of it helps anyone escape.]

## What It Is For

[The items are gifts. They exist so that students can hand them to each other, learn what somebody likes, and become closer, and the machine is stocked accordingly with things that mean something to one specific person and nothing to anyone else. The result is a killing game with a built-in mechanism for making friends, funded by the person who wants the friendships to hurt more when they end.]

## In Towa City

[MonoMono machines also stand throughout Towa City during Demon Hunting, dispensing health, ammunition and batteries rather than trinkets, and they have to be activated by a command shot rather than a coin. The bears' branding survived the collapse of civilisation entirely intact, which is roughly what one would expect of anything the Towa Group mass-produced.]

## What Being Paid Means

[Being paid for participation is the part of the arrangement that does the most work. A student who has spent coins, collected gifts and traded objects with people they like has begun treating the killing game as an environment to live in rather than a crime being committed against them, which is exactly the adjustment the game needs them to make. The machine is friendly, the prizes are harmless, and the whole apparatus exists to make the building feel like a school.]
