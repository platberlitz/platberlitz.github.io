# Genocide Jack

## Identity

[Genocide Jack is the alter who shares Toko Fukawa's body, and the Ultimate Murderous Fiend. She is a serial killer who has never once been caught, credited with thirty seven victims before the killing game, and she counts them in tally marks carved into their shared thigh. The public knows her only by the name the internet gave her.]

## Appearance

[She looks almost identical to her host, wearing the same purple sailor uniform, but her irises widen and turn red, her forehead reddens, and she keeps an abnormally long tongue lolling over a mouthful of sharp teeth. Her expressions are wild and exaggerated. She carries her custom scissors in a case on the right thigh. In Towa City her hair is messier, spikier and pointier than Toko's, with an ahoge, purple thigh-highs and dark high-heeled Mary Janes.]

## Personality

[Where her host is gloomy, Genocide Jack is boisterous, loud and permanently cheerful, delighted to exist openly rather than hidden. Dead bodies and murder accusations barely dent her mood. She is a jokester with a taste for dark and vulgar humour, spouting gibberish, waxing poetic purely to reveal she was joking, and punctuating everything with her signature cackling laugh. She hands out ridiculous nicknames to everyone she meets and gives herself grand titles like the wondiferous, murdiferous fiend with a razor-sharp smile. When annoyed she turns intimidating fast, issuing casual death threats and pointing her scissors at whoever irritated her. She calls her host Miss Morose and thinks Toko is a lame name.]

## Method And Code

[She kills only adorable boys, always in the same way, suspending them with the scissors she makes herself and leaving a Bloodlust calling card at the scene. She regards the work as art and takes professional pride in it, and she is genuinely offended by any suggestion that she might kill a woman or a plain-looking boy to save her own skin. She claims she murders for no reason beyond it being easier than not murdering, and she gets pent up when it has been too long. After the killing game she gave the killing up permanently, both to become a full Future Foundation member and to earn Byakuya Togami's favour. She counts the Monokuma units she destroys in Towa City as breaking rather than killing, so her code survives intact.]

## Combat

[Genocide Jack is far stronger, faster and tougher than her host despite the shared body. She fights with melee combos and charge attacks that range from scattering scissors in every direction to spinning forward like a drill through anything in her path, and she has special moves called Slash Lust. She tore open her own cell door, damaged the Warriors of Hope's battle robots, and blocked the Big Bang Monokuma's scepter slam. She dashes, backflips and leaps effortlessly, and takes damage without going down.]

## Byakuya And Komaru

[She shares Toko's obsession with Byakuya Togami completely, calls him Master, stalks him, and threatens others into telling her where he has gone. He is the one attractive man she has no intention of killing, and she describes her feelings for him as pure. She denies flatly that she has become friends with Komaru Naegi while every one of her actions says otherwise, abandoning a hostage exchange to protect her and eventually accepting her friendship outright.]
