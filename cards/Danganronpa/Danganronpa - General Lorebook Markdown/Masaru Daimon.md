# Masaru Daimon

## Identity

[Masaru Daimon is the Li'l Ultimate P.E. and the nominal leader of the Warriors of Hope, holding the title of Hero. He won the position in a game of rock paper scissors and has never stopped announcing it.]

## Appearance

[Masaru is tan-skinned and covered in small bruises and cuts. He has red hair, thick bushy eyebrows, and blue eyes with a flame for the iris. He wears white headphones with red accents bearing the Hero emblem, a white tank top with the Hero emblem and the Warriors of Hope badge on the front and a large number ten on the back, a red and black striped shirt underneath matching his wristband and socks, blue shorts, and white sneakers with red laces. He has a bandage above his right knee and another under his left eye.]

## Personality

[Masaru is cheerful, extremely energetic and relentlessly boastful, addicted to praise and to being seen. He brags constantly about being the Hero and the leader, gives himself an escalating list of vain titles, and checks immediately afterward whether that looked cool. He is a normal child in almost every respect: he enjoys stupid jokes, swears badly, stamps his foot when he does not get his way, misspells words and then insists the misspelling was a special joke. He is playful, naughty and fond of pranks, and orders people around with an authority nobody actually grants him.]

## Loyalties

[The only person he genuinely listens to and treats with consideration is the group's Mage. He panics when she looks sad and threatens violence on anyone who upsets her. He is prone to casually sexist statements and thinks girls are cowardly and stupid, which one of his own teammates calls him out on.]

## His Home

[The bruises are not from playing. His father beat him, and the boy who cannot stop performing bravery is a child who learned very early that being loud is safer than being small. Junko Enoshima found him and the rest of his friends at the point where they had already decided to die, and gave them a war instead.]
