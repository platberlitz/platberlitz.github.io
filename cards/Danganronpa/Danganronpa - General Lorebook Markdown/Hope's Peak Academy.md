# Hope's Peak Academy

## What It Is

[Hope's Peak Academy is a government-sanctioned private high school with boarding facilities, standing in the centre of a large city and founded several decades before the Killing School Life by a man named Izuru Kamukura. Its purpose is to develop and research the talents of exceptional students, called Ultimates. There is no entrance exam. Students are located by the school's own scouts and invited, with the single exception of the Ultimate Lucky Student, chosen each year by lottery. Candidates must already be enrolled at another high school, which makes the youngest of them sixteen.]

## How It Works

[Students are not required to attend ordinary lessons and are instead expected to develop their talent, with a practical examination halfway through each school year in September. Those exams are judged by dignitaries and experts and reported widely by the press, on the reasoning that visible talent is what gives a nation hope. The school's real product is not graduates but research, and the graduates it does produce are treated as guaranteed experts in their fields for life.]

## Layout

[The main campus is diamond-shaped and about the size of four ordinary high schools, divided into quarters. The east quarter holds the main course facilities and classrooms, the north the old school building, the west the Reserve Course buildings, and the south the main course dormitories along with shops and daily necessities, with a central plaza between them. The old school building in the north quarter is self-contained: five floors of classrooms, dormitories, a gymnasium, a catering kitchen and dining room, a library, a swimming pool, a laboratory, a school store, a trash room with an incinerator, a greenhouse, and a bathhouse with a sauna.]

## Uniforms

[Main course and Reserve Course students are deliberately distinguishable. Main course uniforms are brown, and their wearers may accessorise freely or skip the uniform entirely where a talent demands other clothing. Reserve Course uniforms are the same design in black, and may not be altered in any way.]

## The Shelter

[As the Tragedy consumed the world outside, the academy shut down as a school and the old school building was converted into a shelter for the students who remained, its windows bolted over with thick metal plating and its entrance sealed with a vault door. The plan was taken over from inside. The classrooms were covered in gaudy animal-print wallpaper, the blackboards vandalised, coloured lights installed, and surveillance cameras and monitors added throughout, with the data centre converted into a monitor room. The basement, which had housed the Izuru Kamukura Project, was rebuilt as a courtroom. The shelter became the site of the Killing School Life.]

## Signs Of What Happened

[Classroom 5-C on the top floor is covered in blood and chalk outlines, left over from a killing game held among the school's student council before the students of Class 78th were sealed in. A hidden room behind the second floor men's restroom holds secret documents about the academy itself. The bathhouse was the only part of the building not covered by surveillance, which made it the place where the students held meetings they did not want overheard.]
