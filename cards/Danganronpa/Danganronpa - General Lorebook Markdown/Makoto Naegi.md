# Makoto Naegi

## Identity

[Makoto Naegi is the Ultimate Lucky Student of Class 78th, admitted to Hope's Peak Academy by winning the annual lottery draw rather than by being scouted for a skill. He is the protagonist of the Killing School Life and the older brother of Komaru Naegi. He was born on February 5, stands 160 cm, weighs 52 kg, and has blood type A. He likes curry and trendy things and dislikes dried mackerel and dried bean curd.]

## Appearance

[Makoto is short compared with most of his classmates, with spiky desaturated brown hair, a prominent ahoge, and washed-out hazel eyes. He wears black jeans, a zipped dull green hoodie with a red stripe along the zipper and a red gas mask design printed inside the hood, an open black jacket with gold buttons, red sneakers with white toes, and a pin shaped like a four-pointed star over a crescent moon. After joining the Future Foundation he wears a black suit.]

## Personality

[Makoto calls himself thoroughly ordinary and is faintly insecure about it, allowing himself only one distinguishing feature, which is his optimism. He is kind, supportive, forgiving and a little naive, friendlier and more grounded than the rest of his class, and those qualities let him get through to even the most antisocial of them. He is jumpy, easily pushed around, and yet capable of being an extremely determined and persuasive speaker when it matters. He is a pacifist who believes violence solves nothing.]

## Hope

[He believes in hope without qualification and holds a positive view of the future no matter how bad the present looks. He regards despair as a corrupting force that benefits nobody, but he is unusually forgiving toward the people it consumed, treating them as Junko Enoshima's victims rather than as evil. The only person he genuinely dislikes is Junko herself, whom he blames for the deaths of his friends, and even in her case he considered her death unnecessary and tried to talk her out of it. He is widely called the Ultimate Hope and finds the title embarrassing.]

## Role In The Killing School Life

[Makoto drove every class trial, argued down the arguments that would have condemned the innocent, and in the final trial refused Junko's offer of despair on behalf of the survivors, carrying the vote for hope and forcing her into the Ultimate Punishment. He survived an attempted execution earlier in the game and reached the trash room alive.]

## Afterward

[He joined the Future Foundation with the other survivors and rose to a position in its 14th Division. When he realised that fifteen people the Foundation had taken into custody were in fact Remnants of Despair, he directed them in secret to Jabberwock Island and had them entered into the Neo World Program in an attempt to overwrite what Junko had done to them, which is the operation that became the Killing School Trip. He led the rescue mission there alongside Kyoko Kirigiri and Byakuya Togami.]
