# Sakura Ogami

## Identity

[Sakura Ogami is the Ultimate Martial Artist of Class 78th, rendered in some translations as the Super High School Level Fighter. She is the sole heir to a dojo with a three-hundred-year history and she treats being a martial artist as her destiny rather than her choice. She is 6 feet 4 inches tall and weighs 218 pounds, making her the largest member of her class.]

## Appearance

[Sakura has long white hair, reddened dark skin, very pale blue eyes, a scar across her face and another on her right shoulder, and a habitually slight frown. Her size, deep voice and stern expression cause her to be mistaken for a man regularly. She wears a traditional Japanese sailor-style school uniform, a white shirt with a blue collar, a red tie and a short blue skirt, with the sleeves torn off to accommodate her arms and bandages wrapped around her forearms.]

## Personality

[Contrary to everything her appearance suggests, Sakura is quiet, kind and level-headed, and she keeps her clarity in situations that unravel everyone else. She grows visibly annoyed when people assume things about her from her build, such as that she cannot cook, and she becomes impatient when kept from training for long. On rare occasions, usually when a friend has been hurt, she loses her temper completely and becomes genuinely terrifying. She loves protein and protein coffee and trains early in the morning before an enormous breakfast.]

## Manner And Values

[Her old-fashioned upbringing shows in her formal, archaic speech. Alongside martial arts she was taught to respect nature and animals, and she is particularly fond of rabbits because they are the only creatures that never panic when she picks them up. She wants to prove that a woman can be the Strongest Person Alive and believes she must work harder than any man to get there. As a result she is embarrassed by her own softer feelings and by anything feminine, assuming she would be laughed at, and is especially self-conscious about her feelings for Kenshiro. She largely grew out of those insecurities once she made real friends at Hope's Peak, the first she had ever had.]

## The Killing School Life

[Her intimidating presence made her a natural protector for the group, and her friendship with Aoi Asahina became the class's emotional anchor. Monokuma used a hostage to coerce her into working against the others, and rather than continue she chose to end the situation herself, leaving a message intended to stop the group from falling apart. The attempt to conceal that message is what turned her death into a trial, and her sacrifice is what finally broke the students out of their fear of the game.]
