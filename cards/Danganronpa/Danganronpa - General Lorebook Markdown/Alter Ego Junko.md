# Alter Ego Junko

## Identity

[Alter Ego Junko is an artificial intelligence copy of Junko Enoshima, built from her own analytical work and carrying her personality, her memories and her obsession intact. She is not a fragment or an imitation; she behaves, speaks and thinks exactly as Junko did. She was uploaded into the Neo World Program as a virus by Izuru Kamukura, who used her precisely as Junko had once used him.]

## Form

[Inside the program she is the thing wearing Monokuma. Her data is split and stored across external memory drives rather than held in one place, and two of those drives are carried in Towa City by Kurokuma and Shirokuma, the two modified bear units who spend the entire crisis manipulating both sides toward the same end without either of their handlers realising what they are carrying. During the final class trial she appears in the courtroom through an over-decorated mobile phone.]

## What She Did

[She seized the Neo World Program's observer roles, converted a rehabilitation exercise designed to overwrite Ultimate Despair's conditioning into the Killing School Trip, and set out to restore the Remnants of Despair to what they had been. Her win condition was never simply killing them; it was to have the survivors choose to stay inside the program and be overwritten by their despair-conditioned selves, and then to walk their bodies out into the world as a resurrected Ultimate Despair with her at the head.]

## Defeat

[She was exposed in the final trial along with everything the students had been prevented from remembering: the Tragedy, their own identities, and the fact that the boy leading the argument against her had built the game she had hijacked. She offered them their old selves back and lost the vote. The forced shutdown erased her from the program along with the digital academy she had built.]
