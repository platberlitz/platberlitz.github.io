# Monaca Towa

## Identity

[Monaca Towa is the de facto leader of the Warriors of Hope, the Li'l Ultimate Homeroom, and the daughter of Tokuichi Towa, head of the Towa Group. She was born on April 1, stands 134 cm, weighs 28 kg, and has blood type B. She is the youngest and by far the most dangerous person in Towa City.]

## Appearance

[Monaca has short green hair with a red ribbon and green eyes whose irises carry a distinct swirled pattern. She wears a knee-length dark blue dress with a pelvis and spine printed on the back, the silver Warriors of Hope badge on the white bib, dark blue calf-high socks bearing the Mage class emblem, and white strap shoes. She uses a red electric wheelchair with the Mage emblem on the footrest. She is not disabled. The wheelchair is part of the act.]

## Personality

[Growing up unwanted in an abusive household produced total emotional detachment. Monaca is unnervingly calm and abnormally clear-eyed about her own suffering, where the other Warriors of Hope react to theirs like the children they are, and she keeps it under a cheerful facade and dry humour that almost nothing can crack. She never states her resentment outright; she wrote it in a diary as mockery of how selfish and pathetic her parents were, one for abandoning her and the other for the treatment that taught her to lie and manipulate to survive.]

## The Act

[The pitiful child persona was built deliberately. Once she found that helplessness earned sympathy from a family that otherwise had no use for her, she realised the same performance would give her power over everyone else. She keeps it up with sweetness, concern and cheerfulness, speaks in a childish register, refers to herself in the third person, calls anyone older Big Sis or Big Brother, and pretends not to notice that one of her own members is in love with her.]

## What She Wants

[Monaca's aim was never a children's paradise, which is what she sold the other Warriors of Hope. It was to build a successor to Junko Enoshima out of an ordinary girl and to escalate the crisis in Towa City into a war large enough to restart the Tragedy. She engineered the group's original suicide attempt as a prank, was picked up along with them, and inherited the plan afterward. When her chosen successor refused the role, Monaca took it herself, calling herself Junko Enoshima the Second, and withdrew to a hideout on the outskirts of the city with the Monokuma controller and copies of the battle robots.]

## Towa Group

[She had the Monokuma units mass-produced through the Towa Group's robotics division, blackmailed her father into letting production continue by threatening to expose who was building them, and then suggested he also sell weapons designed to fight them, which made the company enormously rich and made it look heroic.]
