# Despair Disease

## What It Is

[The Despair Disease is an illness released by Monokuma on Jabberwock Island as a motive during the Killing School Trip. It spreads by contact, produces a high fever, and then alters the mind of whoever catches it, stripping away one specific restraint rather than damaging the body. It is not lethal on its own, and it wears off, which is exactly what makes it useful as a motive.]

## Known Strains

[Three strains appeared among the students. One removes the ability to tell the truth, so that the sufferer says the opposite of what they mean without being able to stop. One removes courage entirely, reducing the sufferer to terror at everything. One removes inhibition, leaving the sufferer cheerful, unashamed and utterly without restraint.]

## The Announcement

[Monokuma declared that anyone infected could not be punished for a murder, since the disease and not the person would be responsible, and that the illness was contagious enough that quarantine was the only safe option. The infected were moved into the hospital on the first island and the healthy were kept out overnight, which produced the isolation the motive required.]

## What It Actually Did

[The disease does not create desires that were not already there. It removes the barrier holding one back. In the case that mattered, the fever restored memories the Neo World Program had erased, and the student who woke from it was not the meek girl the others knew but the person she had been before entering the program, with her devotion to Junko Enoshima entirely intact. Two classmates died before anyone understood what had happened, and by the time the trial ended the illness had already faded, leaving nothing to blame.]

## As A Motive

[The design is more precise than it appears. The motive does not supply a reason to kill, which every previous motive had done. It supplies permission, and it supplies it publicly, so that every healthy student spends the quarantine wondering which of the infected has stopped being restrained by whatever normally restrains them. The result is that the survivors distrust the patients, the patients distrust themselves, and the group divides along a line drawn by an illness nobody can test for. By the time it burns out the damage is already done and there is no longer anything to hold responsible.]
