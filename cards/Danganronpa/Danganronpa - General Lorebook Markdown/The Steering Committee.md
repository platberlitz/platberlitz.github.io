# The Steering Committee

## What It Is

[The Steering Committee is the body that actually governs Hope's Peak Academy. It meets in a dedicated room on the top floor of the faculty building with the sitting headmaster to decide the school's direction and its ongoing projects, and it is where the annual lottery draw for the Ultimate Lucky Student is held. Its authority runs above the headmaster's rather than beside it.]

## Method

[The committee's interest is in the school's research programme and in the money and prestige that sustain it, and it is willing to conceal any part of that work from anyone, including the headmaster. The Izuru Kamukura Project was directed by the committee and kept secret from Jin Kirigiri entirely, because the human experimentation it required could not survive being known. The Reserve Course was created in part to fund the same project, so the committee financed its most indefensible work with tuition taken from the students it valued least.]

## Scouting

[Almost every Ultimate is located by the school's talent scouts. Only twice is a student known to have been scouted by the Steering Committee personally, and both of those students were the Enoshima twins.]

## Consequence

[The committee treated talent as a resource, the Reserve Course as a revenue stream, and secrecy as a management tool, and it produced exactly the results those choices imply. The project it hid created the person who would mastermind the Tragedy's first killing game, and the resentment it manufactured among the students it despised was the fuel that turned a school scandal into a global collapse. The committee did not survive the events it set in motion.]

## Reach

[The committee's authority extends past the school gates. Hope's Peak Academy's graduates occupy the top of every profession they entered, its research underpins work in fields well beyond education, and its endorsement is what makes an Ultimate title worth anything at all. A body able to decide who is named an Ultimate is a body able to decide who the world regards as exceptional, and the committee treated that power as an asset to be managed rather than a responsibility. Its members are never named to the students, appear in no photographs the students see, and answer no questions.]
