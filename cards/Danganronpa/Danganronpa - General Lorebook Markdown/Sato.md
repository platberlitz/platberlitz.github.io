# Sato

## Identity

[Sato was a Hope's Peak Academy Reserve Course student and a childhood friend of Mahiru Koizumi. She enrolled in the Reserve Course to stay near Mahiru after Mahiru was scouted into the main course, and she is one of only three Reserve Course students known by name.]

## The Feud

[Sato was fiercely protective of Mahiru and increasingly hostile toward Natsumi Kuzuryu, who transferred into the Reserve Course partway through the year and began harassing and threatening Mahiru. The animosity between the two of them was known to everyone around them and escalated steadily.]

## The Killing

[Sato killed Natsumi, striking her from behind, and afterward said she had done it because she was afraid of what Natsumi would do to Mahiru. Mahiru and the other girls of her class concealed what they knew and protected Sato rather than report her, a decision Mahiru justified to herself as protecting a friend and which she never resolved.]

## Her Death

[Natsumi's brother, the heir to the Kuzuryu Clan, found Sato and killed her with a metal bat in revenge, matching the weapon to the crime deliberately. Hope's Peak Academy covered up both deaths rather than allow a scandal involving its Reserve Course and one of its Ultimates. The whole chain of events was erased from the memories of everyone involved by the Neo World Program, and returned to them years later inside a game cartridge as a motive for murder.]

## Character

[Sato was ordinary and knew it, and she made her peace with being ordinary by attaching herself completely to somebody who was not. Her loyalty to Mahiru Koizumi was the organising fact of her life, which is why she followed her to a school she could not otherwise have attended and why she reacted to a threat against her the way she did. She was not a violent person and she did not plan what she did; she struck once, from behind, out of fear rather than anger, and then went on attending classes.]

## What Her Case Established

[Two students died and the academy recorded neither. What that established for everyone who learned of it later is that Hope's Peak Academy would bury anything rather than admit its Reserve Course existed on the same campus as its Ultimates, and that a Reserve Course student's death was a public relations matter rather than a crime. That understanding is the ground the Tragedy grew out of.]
