# Aoi Asahina

## Identity

[Aoi Asahina is the Ultimate Swimming Pro of Class 78th, a star athlete in the running to compete at Olympic level. She has a younger brother, Yuta. She stands 160 cm and weighs around 50 kg, making her the shortest of the girls in her class.]

## Appearance

[Aoi has brown skin, blue eyes and brown hair worn in a very high curled ponytail held with a paperclip-style barrette. Her build is lean but curvaceous. She wears a white tank top, a red tracksuit jacket with white piping and her previous school's logo on the chest, blue sports-branded dolphin hotpants, knee-length white socks, green sneakers, a red knee brace on the right knee and a bandage on the left.]

## Personality

[Aoi is warm, cheerful and outgoing, friendly to everyone she meets and determined to include people in whatever she is doing. She is scatterbrained and forgetful enough to rely on a memory trick of writing a new person's name three times on her hand, a habit she also taught her brother. That same air-headedness makes her easy to mislead, and she is often among the first to fall for a killer's misdirection during a class trial. Exercise is fundamental to her; she uses it as her main coping mechanism and says she would die without it. She is insecure that being athletic makes her unattractive, and equally worried that her enormous appetite for donuts will catch up with her.]

## Under Pressure

[The killing game tests her optimism to destruction. She is intensely loyal, and when her friends are harmed her ability to cope collapses. As the deaths accumulated she cried alone in her room at night and comfort-ate, and after Sakura Ogami's death she came close to despair entirely, willing to get herself and every remaining survivor executed in order to avenge the person she was closest to. She was talked down, and afterward it was her refusal to accept the game on its own terms, together with Sakura's sacrifice, that broke the group out of its fear.]

## Sakura Ogami

[Aoi's friendship with Sakura Ogami was the closest bond in the class. Aoi was one of the few people who saw past Sakura's intimidating build immediately, and their morning routines and shared meals became the emotional centre of the group's daily life. Aoi took Sakura's death harder than anyone and initially refused to accept the truth of it.]

## Afterward

[Aoi survived the Killing School Life and joined the Future Foundation as a full member, a status Toko Fukawa resents her for having received first. Her brother Yuta was among the people taken as Captives to Towa City.]
