# Natsumi Kuzuryu

## Identity

[Natsumi Kuzuryu was the younger sister of Fuyuhiko Kuzuryu and a student in Hope's Peak Academy's Reserve Course. She was murdered before the Tragedy, and her death is the origin of the events dramatised in the Twilight Syndrome Murder Case.]

## Standing In The Clan

[A faction within the Kuzuryu Clan considered her better suited to lead it than her brother, on the strength of a talent for getting people to do exactly what she wanted, and she was said to be the reincarnation of the family's most powerful member. She was offered the chance to lead on Fuyuhiko's behalf and turned it down. She was fond of her brother and maintained that she was only impressive because she was his sister.]

## The Reserve Course

[When Fuyuhiko was scouted as the Ultimate Yakuza she saw him off with her usual mockery and then found herself left behind. She began calling herself the Ultimate Little Sister, reasoning that being the sister of an Ultimate was itself a qualification, and asked her father to pay the Reserve Course tuition so that she could follow him onto the same campus. She arrived partway through the year.]

## Her Death

[At the Reserve Course she reignited a feud with another student, and her harassment and threats escalated until that student's friend killed her out of fear for what she would do next. The killing was covered up by a group of main course girls who protected the murderer. Fuyuhiko learned who was responsible and killed her with a metal bat, and the academy buried both deaths. Neither he nor anyone else involved remembered any of it once the Neo World Program removed their school memories, until Monokuma handed it back to them as a motive.]

## What She Was Like

[Natsumi was sharp-tongued, aggressive and used to getting her way, and she treated the main course students as people standing between her and the brother she had followed onto the campus. She teased and provoked constantly, and the harassment that ended her life began as an attempt to make somebody react. She had genuine ability, which the clan recognised and the academy did not, and the Reserve Course gave her nothing to use it on. Her most dangerous quality was the one the clan valued most, which was that she could talk almost anyone into almost anything, and at the Reserve Course she aimed it at a person who was already frightened.]
