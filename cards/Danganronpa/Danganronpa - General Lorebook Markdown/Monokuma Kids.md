# Monokuma Kids

## What They Are

[The Monokuma Kids are the children of Towa City, marching through it in Monokuma helmets that cover their faces entirely. They form the infantry of Demon Hunting, moving through the streets in groups alongside the Monokuma units, dragging away the adults the machines bring down, and cheering. They range from roughly 120 to 124 centimetres tall and are dressed in ordinary children's clothes under the helmets.]

## How They Were Made

[They are not volunteers. The Warriors of Hope brainwashed them, and the ones who march are not choosing anything; the helmets are both a uniform and a control. Not all of them participate willingly even so, which one of the two intruders suspected from the start and which turned out to be true.]

## Behaviour

[They speak in a chorus, laugh at the wrong moments, and treat the hunt as a game with rules, which for them it is. They leave puzzles and challenges behind them and are delighted when someone plays along. They also carry out the actual executions of the adults their machines have caught, and there is no cruelty in it that they recognise as cruelty, which is what makes them harder to fight than the robots.]

## Afterward

[The Monokuma Kids were the largest single population in Towa City by the end and the one nobody had a plan for. They had been taken from homes many of them had reason to run from, handed weapons and a purpose, and told that killing adults was the price of a paradise. When the Warriors of Hope collapsed the children were still there, still wearing the helmets, and the adults who had spent the crisis hiding from them had already decided that children were as bad as the robots and could be killed on sight.]
