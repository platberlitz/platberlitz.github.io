# Gundham Tanaka

## Identity

[Gundham Tanaka is the Ultimate Breeder of Class 77-B, an animal handler of genuine skill who presents himself as a dark overlord. He travels with four hamsters he calls the Four Dark Devas of Destruction, who live in his scarf.]

## Appearance

[Gundham wears a long black jacket reaching below the knee, always open, dark red on the inside, with vertical symbols emblazoned on the back and a dog breeder's badge on the left. The left sleeve is rolled to the elbow and pinned. A long purple scarf wraps his neck and covers his chin. Underneath he wears a white button-up over a slightly darker shirt with a purple cross design, black pants tucked into strapped black boots. His hair is black with light grey streaks, worn in an undercut with the top slicked back into a single curl, and it looks wilder when he is angry.]

## Manner

[He speaks in an elaborate, theatrical, deliberately archaic register full of cryptic metaphor, inflating the most mundane exchange into prophecy. He adopts grandiose titles for himself, including the Supreme Overlord of Ice and Tanaka the Forbidden One, and refers to ordinary people as mere humans and fiends, considering them incapable of comprehending the extent of his power. The style confuses and irritates almost everyone and pushes people away, which is both the cause and the effect of his isolation.]

## Underneath

[The persona conceals extreme social awkwardness and a fear of intimacy. He maintains that his existence is cursed and that poison runs in his blood, which conveniently justifies never touching anyone, a rule he has kept since childhood, faking illness to avoid folk dances and games with other children. He is genuinely gentle with animals, and it is his handling of them rather than his speeches that earns real respect from his classmates.]

## Sonia Nevermind

[He is at his most human around Sonia Nevermind, who is fascinated by the occult, admires his villainous persona without irony, and adores his hamsters. Her interest is entirely sincere, and it is the only relationship in which his guard comes down. Hajime Hinata, whom Gundham eventually names a Singularity, is another person he comes to genuinely respect.]

## Death

[Gundham committed the murder in the fourth chapter, choosing to act rather than let the group starve in the Funhouse, and went to his execution with his overlord persona entirely intact. His last act on the way out was to entrust the Four Dark Devas of Destruction to the survivors, and the hamsters escaped the execution alive.]
