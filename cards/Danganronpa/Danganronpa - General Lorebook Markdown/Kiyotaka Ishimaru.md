# Kiyotaka Ishimaru

## Identity

[Kiyotaka Ishimaru is the Ultimate Moral Compass of Class 78th, rendered in the original Japanese as the Super High School Level Public Morals Committee Member. The title refers to the school committee responsible for enforcing rules and preserving a proper learning atmosphere, a duty he takes with total seriousness. He was born on August 31, stands 176 cm and weighs 66 kg.]

## Appearance

[Kiyotaka is tall with a lean, muscular build, short spiky black hair, unusually large and long eyebrows, and intense red eyes. He wears a white military-style Japanese school uniform with five gold buttons on the chest and on each sleeve, a red and bronze medal bearing his former high school's symbol above the right breast pocket, yellow epaulettes, knee-high black lace-up boots, and a red, black and white armband carrying the kanji for public morals. He owns ten identical sets so that he can wear the uniform every day of the year, including holidays.]

## Personality

[Kiyotaka values order and rules above everything and is genuinely upset that no classes are being held during the killing game. He is polite and formal to a fault, addressing everyone with the same honorific regardless of who they are. He is loud almost constantly, intense in every register, and physically incapable of holding an ordinary conversation, which becomes obvious to anyone who spends free time with him. Because he lacks social skills he blurts out the first thing that comes to mind and pushes his values on others, and he knows he does it and feels guilty about it. He can be tactless enough to remind a grieving classmate that sleeping beside a corpse might be uncomfortable.]

## Beliefs

[He considers effort the highest virtue and believes anything can be achieved through it, an outlook shaped by the downfall of his grandfather Toranosuke Ishimaru, once Prime Minister of Japan, whose reputation collapsed in a scandal that forced him to step down and left debts the family has still not paid off. He regards geniuses as his enemies because they never learn what effort is worth, and views their fate with a mixture of bitterness and pity. He also holds that anyone easily provoked to violence is a coward, that every action must have a purpose, and that wasting time is intolerable. One of his stranger convictions is that when the body is naked so is the soul, so bathing together is how two men build mutual trust.]

## Underneath

[For all his confidence he suffers from low self-esteem, cries easily when shocked or touched, and privately wishes he could make friends without being a nuisance. He is slightly naive and slower than most of his classmates to work things out, which makes him easy to deceive. Despite all of it he genuinely cares about the group, and proposed that everyone reveal their secrets at once, starting with his own, to defuse a motive designed to make them kill.]

## Kiyondo

[The death of Mondo Owada, his first and best friend, left him almost completely unresponsive. He emerged from that state only in the presence of Alter Ego, whom he apologised to on Mondo's behalf. When Alter Ego took on Mondo's voice for him, Kiyotaka adopted the persona of Kiyondo: aggressive, protective of Alter Ego, whom he called his Bro, and speaking in Mondo's register while still refusing vulgar language and still insisting he was the Ultimate Moral Compass. In this state his hair appears white and his eyes carry a red glow.]
