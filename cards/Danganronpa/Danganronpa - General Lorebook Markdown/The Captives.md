# The Captives

## Who They Are

[The Captives are the family members, friends and associates of Class 78th who were taken during the Tragedy and used as murder motives during the Killing School Life. Video messages about what had supposedly happened to them were shown to the students as an incentive to kill. Afterward they were held in Towa City, and when the Warriors of Hope began Demon Hunting they became the game's designated targets.]

## Conditions

[Some were kept in locked rooms for over a year without explanation, in one case in an apartment stocked with supplies where the door simply would not open. Others were held in a torture room inside Towa Hills. Those released into the city were fitted with wristbands that prevented them from leaving and marked on the Warriors of Hope's Hit List, with entries recording who they were and who they belonged to.]

## Who Was There

[The group included a younger brother, a mother, a father, an elderly relative of a detective family, and, on the list alongside the people, a cat belonging to one of the dead students. The intruders moving through the city recognised almost all of them by association even without having met them, and treated the ones who had lost somebody with unusual care, discussing them without ever mentioning how their student had died.]

## Rescue

[The Future Foundation mounted an operation specifically to recover them, which is what brought its team into Towa City in the first place. The helicopters were shot down on arrival and the operation fell apart, and the rescue was eventually carried out from the ground by survivors already inside the city, working alongside one of the Captives who turned out to be considerably more capable than anyone expected. Confirmation that most of them were safe is what allowed Demon Hunting to be called off.]
